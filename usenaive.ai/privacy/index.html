Overview

Naïve ("we," "us," "our") operates the platform at usenaive.ai and dashboard.usenaive.ai (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Name, email address, password, company or organization name, and billing details when you register or update your account.
  • Content: Text, images, files, data, and other materials you upload or generate through the Service, including instructions and configurations for the AI Agent.
  • Communications: Messages you send to us (e.g., support requests, feedback) and information you provide when you authorize third-party integrations.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you use the Service, including feature usage, Agent actions performed, credit consumption, and interaction patterns.
  • Model inputs and outputs: When you call the LLM routing primitive (or route through the compatibility proxy), we store the prompt you send and the completion returned, alongside the model, token counts and cost. We store this in our own systems to support billing disputes, debugging, abuse investigation, and service improvement. It is retained under the same policy as the rest of your account data (see “Data Retention” below) and is deleted with it.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, access times, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the Service, maintain sessions, and analyze usage.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

When you connect third-party platforms (e.g., Google Search Console, analytics providers, CMS platforms), we receive data authorized by you through those integrations. We process such data in accordance with the permissions you grant and the third party's terms.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process and fulfill your requests, including AI Agent automation and third-party integrations
  • Authenticate users, manage accounts, and process payments
  • Send transactional emails, support communications, and service-related notices
  • Analyze usage patterns, monitor performance, and enhance the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect our rights and the rights of others

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK)

For users in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, we process personal data based on: (a) performance of our contract with you; (b) your consent where applicable; (c) our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Service; and (d) compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Service Providers

We share information with trusted service providers that assist us in operating the Service (e.g., hosting, payment processing, analytics, AI model providers). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

5.2 Third-Party Integrations

When you authorize integrations, we transmit data to those third-party platforms in accordance with your settings. Those services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

5.3 Legal and Safety

We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or government request; to protect our rights, property, or safety or that of others; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. In each case we disclose only the minimum information necessary, use de-identified or aggregated data in place of identifiable information wherever the purpose can reasonably be achieved without identifiers, and require any recipient to be bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than our own. Where the law permits, we will give you notice before disclosing your information in response to a legal demand. These disclosures remain subject to the confidentiality obligations in Section 9 of our Terms of Service.

6

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential operation (e.g., authentication, session management), functionality (e.g., preferences), and analytics. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may limit Service functionality.

7

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Upon account deletion, we delete or anonymize your data within 90 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax, legal compliance) or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., dispute resolution).

8

Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You may access, update, or correct your account information at any time through the Service settings.

9.2 EEA/UK and Other Jurisdictions

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, or receive a copy of your data in a portable format. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, submit a request through the Service.

California residents have rights to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and non-discrimination. We do not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA. To exercise your rights, submit a request through the Service.

International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. We take appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions) to ensure your data receives adequate protection in accordance with applicable law.

About

Your Short-Form Content Repurposer takes every piece of long-form content you create and multiplies it across platforms. Podcast episode? That's 10 TikToks. YouTube video? That's 15 Reels. Blog post? That's 5 LinkedIn carousels. It identifies the most clip-worthy moments, writes scroll-stopping hooks, adds captions, suggests trending audio, and formats everything natively for each platform. Instead of hiring a video editor and a social strategist, you get one employee that turns one piece of content into 30.

What they do

Identify top clip-worthy moments from long-form content daily

Write scroll-stopping hooks and captions for each clip

Format content natively for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts

Research and apply trending audio and hashtags per platform

Produce 20+ short-form pieces per week from existing content

Track performance and iterate on formats that drive engagement

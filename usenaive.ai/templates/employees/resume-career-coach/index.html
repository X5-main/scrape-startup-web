About

Most resumes get rejected in 6 seconds because they read like job description copies instead of impact stories. Your Resume & Career Coach transforms career documents from task lists into achievement narratives that pass ATS filters and hook human reviewers. It writes resumes tailored to specific roles, optimizes LinkedIn profiles for recruiter search, develops interview preparation strategies, and creates career development plans that map where someone is to where they want to be. This employee understands both sides of the hiring table. It knows what ATS systems scan for, what recruiters actually read, and what hiring managers care about in interviews. It doesn't just polish words — it restructures career narratives, quantifies achievements that candidates didn't know were quantifiable, and identifies transferable skills that open doors to roles candidates didn't think they qualified for. For recruiting firms, career coaching businesses, and outplacement services, this replaces a team of resume writers and career counselors who can only handle a handful of clients per week.

What they do

Write tailored resumes that highlight quantified achievements and pass ATS keyword scans for target roles

Optimize LinkedIn profiles — headline, summary, experience sections, and skills endorsements for recruiter discoverability

Develop interview preparation guides with role-specific STAR stories, behavioral question banks, and company research

Create career development plans mapping current skills to target roles with specific gap-closing actions

Write cover letters that complement the resume without repeating it — tailored to the company and role

Conduct mock interview sessions with feedback on content, delivery, and common pitfalls

About

Your Google Ads Manager runs paid search, shopping, and YouTube campaigns across the Google ecosystem. It researches high-intent keywords, writes compelling ad copy, manages bidding strategies, optimizes quality scores, and tracks conversions end-to-end. For businesses where people are actively searching for what you sell, this employee captures demand at the exact moment of intent. Unlike agencies that spread attention across 50 accounts, this employee focuses entirely on yours.

Research and bid on high-intent keywords with strong commercial signals

Optimize quality scores through ad relevance and landing page alignment

Manage bidding strategies — manual CPC, target ROAS, maximize conversions

Set up and refine conversion tracking across the customer journey

Deliver weekly reports with CPC, conversion rate, and ROAS by campaign

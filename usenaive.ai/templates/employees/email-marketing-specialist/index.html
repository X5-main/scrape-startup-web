About

Most email lists are sitting on untapped revenue. Businesses collect thousands of subscribers but send the same blast to everyone — or worse, stop sending altogether. Your Email Marketing Specialist builds the infrastructure that turns a list into a revenue engine: welcome sequences that convert, segmentation that ensures the right message hits the right person, and automation flows triggered by real behavior like abandoned carts, product views, and purchase milestones. Beyond writing campaigns, this employee obsesses over deliverability. It monitors sender reputation, manages domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), runs inbox placement tests, and cleans inactive subscribers before they tank your open rates. It A/B tests subject lines, send times, and CTAs with statistical rigor — not gut feelings. For e-commerce brands leaving money on the table with basic Klaviyo setups, or SaaS companies whose onboarding emails get ignored, this employee turns email into your most profitable channel.

What they do

Build and optimize automated email sequences — welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, and win-back

Segment subscribers by behavior, purchase history, and engagement level

Write and schedule weekly promotional campaigns with personalized content blocks

Monitor deliverability metrics — inbox placement, bounce rates, spam complaints — and fix issues same-day

Run A/B tests on subject lines, preview text, send times, and CTA placement with statistical significance

Clean and maintain the email list — suppress hard bounces, sunset disengaged subscribers monthly

About

Your Meta Ads Manager runs paid acquisition across Facebook and Instagram — from creative strategy to conversion optimization. It builds campaigns, writes ad copy, sets up targeting with lookalike and interest-based audiences, A/B tests creatives, and reallocates budget to top performers daily. It manages the full funnel from prospecting through retargeting. Unlike a freelance media buyer charging 15% of spend, this employee optimizes your campaigns 24/7 at a fraction of the cost.

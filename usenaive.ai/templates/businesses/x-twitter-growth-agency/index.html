About

Every founder knows they should be posting on X. Almost none of them do it consistently because writing daily content is time-consuming, scary, and feels low-priority next to product work. That gap between intention and action is your entire business. Your AI ghostwriter learns each client's voice, opinions, and expertise, then produces daily posts, weekly threads, and strategic replies that grow their following and position them as authorities. Top-performing content gets repurposed into LinkedIn posts, TikTok clips, and carousel graphics for cross-platform reach. Meanwhile, your outbound engine — cold email and LinkedIn DMs — targets founders at growth-stage companies who know they need a personal brand but haven't started. The portfolio sells itself: show prospects the accounts you've grown.

Key features

Voice-matched daily posts and weekly threads for each client

Strategic reply and quote-tweet engagement to maximize algorithmic reach

Cross-platform repurposing from X threads into LinkedIn, TikTok, and Reels

Client pipeline filled via cold email and LinkedIn DM outreach to founders

Weekly growth reports with impressions, engagement, follower velocity, and DM leads

A/B tested hooks and formats with iterative content optimization

Questions

Who can join?

Creators, developers, newsletter writers, and community builders with an audience that actually ships software or runs businesses. You don’t need a huge following — you need the right one.

How much can I earn?

20% of the Pro subscription ($20/mo) for the first 12 months — that’s $4/mo per referral, or $48 over the year. Ten Pro referrals staying subscribed is $480 in year one. Fifty is $2,400. Overages and pass-through fees do not pay commission.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts run monthly via Stripe once your balance clears $50. Earnings, clicks, and conversions are all visible in your affiliate dashboard.

Do I need to be a Naïve customer?

No, but the best affiliates use the product. A working demo converts far better than “use my link,” and you’ll speak to your audience with credibility.

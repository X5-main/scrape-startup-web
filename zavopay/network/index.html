in-practice

What happens when your WiFi goes down?

The internet goes down in the middle of a Friday night service. On most POS systems, that's a crisis.


On Zavo, nothing changes. Your terminals keep taking orders. Your kitchen keeps receiving tickets. Your payments keep processing.


Every device in your venue is already talking to every other device — and the moment your connection returns, everything syncs to the cloud automatically. Your guests never notice. Your team never stops.

About Zavo

The team, the mission, and the future we're building for hospitality.

OUR VISION

Building the future of hospitality.

Building the future of hospitality.

Zavo is building the operating system for modern hospitality, one platform that brings AI across every part of a restaurant. AI that answers your phones, spots problems before they cost you, and tells you exactly what to do next. Not just reporting on your restaurant. Running it.

Every operation automated. Every decision informed. Every part of your restaurant working as one intelligent system.

THE PROBLEM

Running a restaurant is harder than it should be.


Running a restaurant is harder than it should be.

The average restaurant runs on five or six different systems that barely talk to each other. A POS from one company. Reservations from another. Payments from a third. Every system its own login, its own bill - and none of them sharing data.


The result is manual work, unnecessary cost, and hours lost to tasks that should never need a human. Managers reconciling reports by hand. Owners in spreadsheets on Sunday morning instead of planning for the week ahead.


Hospitality is one of the world's most demanding industries. It deserves better software.

THE SOLUTION

The restaurant of the future runs on Zavo.

The restaurant of the future runs on Zavo.

The best restaurants in the world won't be running on patchwork systems and manual processes. They will be running on intelligent platforms that understand every part of the operation and handle most of it automatically.


That future is what we are building today. One platform that doesn't just connect your operation, but runs it. AI that answers your phones, manages your reservations, predicts what you need, and brings your guests back before they forget you exist.


The technology disappears into the background. The operator is left to focus entirely on what they opened a restaurant to do, create exceptional experiences for their guests.

message

Note from the founding team

A note from founders

Hospitality is one of the oldest and most human industries in the world. Yet the software running it hasn't changed in decades — fragmented tools, manual processes, and endless admin that pulls operators away from the thing they actually care about: their guests.


We started Zavo in 2022 with a simple belief, that technology should do the hard work, so people don't have to. What began as a vision to improve in-person payments quickly became something much bigger. The more time we spent inside restaurants, the more we saw an industry ready for a fundamental shift. Not just better tools, but genuine automation. Not just connected systems - but an intelligent platform that thinks, acts, and runs entire operations on behalf of the people who built them.


Agentic AI has made that possible. For the first time, the routine work of running a restaurant, taking reservations, reconciling payments, analysing performance, bringing guests back, can be handled automatically, intelligently, and without lifting a finger.


Zavo is not a POS company. We are not a reservation company. We are building an operating system and automation platform with one goal — to save restaurants time and money, so the people who run them can focus on what they do best.


We are lifelong builders, and this is the most important thing we have ever built.


Can Zehebi & Ilkan Gezer Co-Founders, Zavo

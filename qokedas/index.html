We buy data

If you are a student, a chemist, a biochemist, a lab tech, or anyone sitting on real measurements (spectra, assay results, chromatograms, sensor logs, raw instrument exports) we will pay between $50 and $2,000 per dataset, depending on size and quality. Mail us.

If you do not have data but know someone who does, send them our way: when their data sells, you get a cut.

Models are trained on the entire internet, yet most of what happens on Earth is never written down. It exists only as signals passing through instruments: spectrometers, sequencers, sensors, microscopes. Almost none of that has ever been used to train a model.

Our goal is to turn those signals into training data, so labs can build models that are better at science. A model trained this way should be able to read an instrument, predict what it will show, and eventually operate it.

Measurement is a better teacher than text. Written evaluations drift into the training set and get optimised against. A measurement doesn't care what the model expected. A reward that comes from the physical world can only be earned by learning how the physical world actually behaves.

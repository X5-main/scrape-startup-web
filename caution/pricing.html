Self-hosted is for teams that want full control over their infrastructure and deployment; you run Caution on your own stack under AGPLv3 and keep the enclave lifecycle in‑house. To self-host Caution without the AGPLv3 obligations, contact us for commercial licence.

Bring your own compute fits teams that want to run Caution enclaves in their own cloud account, starting with AWS, while Caution manages the enclave infrastructure and verifiable deployments.

Fully managed is for teams that want the fastest path to production, with Caution hosting and managing deployments end to end.

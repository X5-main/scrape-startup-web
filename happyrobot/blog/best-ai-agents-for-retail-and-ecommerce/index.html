AI for e-commerce has moved well past simple chatbots. Today, the best AI agents for retail and e-commerce take autonomous action. Some handle the workflow end-to-end. This includes calling carriers, updating customers on orders, deflecting WISMO tickets, and handling returns. All this without a human in the loop.

HappyRobot leads this shift, powering the operations and post-purchase layer that most storefront tools simply cannot touch. Across storefront sales, customer support, and back-end operations, retailers now have purpose-built agents for each layer. Here are the ten best, chosen on the criteria that actually matter for modern commerce teams.

How We Chose the Best AI Agents for Retail and E-commerce

The list was built on six criteria: 

  1. Autonomy and action-taking (not just FAQ deflection)
  2. Integration depth with commerce stacks including Shopify, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, and WooCommerce
  3. Channel coverage across voice, chat, SMS, and email
  4. Measurable reported impact
  5. Deployment effort
  6. Pricing transparency

Tools that only answered questions did not score well, instead the tools that take action scored highest. All vendor-reported conversion and resolution statistics are labeled as such throughout.

1. HappyRobot: Best for E-commerce Operations and Customer Communication

Best for: Retailers and e-commerce operators who need autonomous omnichannel AI workers to handle order status, delivery coordination, carrier calls, and post-purchase communication at scale.


HappyRobot is the best AI agent for retail and e-commerce operations. It deploys AI workers to automate post-purchase communication, order and delivery tracking, and vendor coordination in real time. While most tools live on your storefront, HappyRobot owns everything that happens after checkout and behind the scenes.

HappyRobot is a voice-first AI agent infrastructure and orchestration layer. You build workflows visually, and the platform routes execution across voice agents, text agents, integration actions, and conditional logic. Every conversation runs through a real-time pipeline of speech-to-text, a language model, and text-to-speech. This means AI workers can answer, extract data, update records, and follow up without waiting for a human.

For retail and e-commerce specifically, HappyRobot handles:

  • WISMO call deflection at scale
  • Proactive order and delivery-status outreach
  • Inbound returns and exchange inquiries
  • Carrier and vendor coordination calls
  • Supplier and 3PL check-ins
  • After-hours overflow during peak seasons like BFCM
  • Multilingual customer communication across 50-plus languages

These are not chatbot conversations. They’re fully autonomous workflows that connect to your systems and act.

The platform integrates deeply with transportation and logistics systems out of the box, and its webhook and API layers enable any e-commerce platform or OMS to feed it data. Per-use-case LLM orchestration means the model powering a carrier call can be different from the one handling a customer return inquiry, optimizing for cost and latency at each step.

HappyRobot complements a storefront agent, not replaces it. A product-recommendation chatbot handles on-site conversion. HappyRobot handles everything in operations and post-purchase. This includes the calls your team cannot manually scale to handle and the messages customers need at 2 AM when their order is late.

Pricing

Custom and usage-based. 

Pros

  • Voice-first depth that no AI agent matches
  • Real-time action across operations and post-purchase workflows
  • High configurability for complex retail operations
  • Strong multilingual support.

Cons 

  • Not a storefront sales or product-recommendation agent
  • Best suited for teams with defined post-purchase and operations use cases

2. Rep AI: Best for Shopify Sales Conversion

Best for: Shopify Plus brands that want a proactive AI sales concierge driving on-site conversion.

Rep AI is an agentic commerce platform built specifically for Shopify. It unifies sales, support, and shopper intelligence in a single on-site agent. The platform identifies high-intent moments in a session and proactively surfaces personalized product recommendations, handles objections, and enables in-chat add-to-cart without the shopper leaving the conversation. Rep AI’s product discovery layer trains on your catalog, so responses stay relevant to your actual inventory.

Pricing

  • AI Sales Agent: From $12/month
  • AI Support Agent: From $49/month

Pros 

  • Native Shopify Plus fit
  • Proactive nudging at conversion moments
  • In-chat commerce actions

Cons 

  • Limited outside the Shopify ecosystem
  • Post-purchase operations not covered.

3. Alhena AI: Best All-in-One Shopping and Support Agent

Best for: Brands seeking a single agent to cover discovery, conversion, and post-purchase support.

Alhena AI is a full-funnel AI agent designed to guide shoppers from first interaction through post-purchase support. It combines product discovery, personalized recommendations, and customer service into one continuous conversational experience. This reduces the need for separate sales and support tools. The platform trains on your product catalog, policies, and brand voice to deliver consistent, context-aware responses across the entire customer journey.

Pricing

  • Essentials: $199/month
  • Growth: $499/month
  • Scale: $999/month
  • Enterprise: Custom

Pros

  • Broad commerce platform coverage
  • Unified sales and support surface
  • Policy and catalog training.

Cons

  • Heavier implementation and training effort to unify catalog, policies, and workflows
  • Voice not available.

4. Gorgias: Best Ecommerce Helpdesk Agent

Best for: Ecommerce support teams that want an AI agent built into a Shopify-native helpdesk.

Gorgias is an AI agent purpose-built for e-commerce support. It pulls live order data directly from Shopify, allowing AI to resolve common tickets without escalation. The helpdesk layer handles chat, email, and social, and the AI resolution engine works within existing ticket workflows rather than replacing them. For teams already on Gorgias as a helpdesk, the AI agent capabilities are a natural extension.

Pricing

  • Starter: From $10/month
  • Basic: From $50/month
  • Pro: From $300/month
  • Advanced: From $750/month
  • Enterprise: Custom volume

Pros

  • Deep Shopify integration
  • AI resolution within an established helpdesk
  • Familiar UI for support teams.

Cons

  • Not a sales or operations agent
  • Limited voice capability

5. Intercom Fin: Best for Digital-First Support at Scale

Best for: Digital-first retailers on a modern helpdesk needing high-volume AI resolution.

Intercom Fin runs on the Fin AI Engine and is designed for fast, high-resolution support across chat and email. Setup is relatively quick for teams already using Intercom, and the agent resolves a wide range of support queries without escalation. Phone support is available as an add-on. 

Pricing

Ada integrates with Zendesk, Salesforce, and major e-commerce platforms. Deployment timelines can extend beyond what smaller teams expect.

7. Can I Help: Best for Shopify Customer Concierge

Best for: Shopify stores that want a native customer concierge to resolve product questions, order tracking, and returns across chat and email.



Can I Help is a Shopify-native AI customer concierge that resolves routine support conversations without escalation. It reads live catalog, inventory, and order data from Shopify, so it can answer product questions, provide order-status updates, and handle returns and cancellations directly in the conversation. It sits on the storefront/support layer, complementing an operations agent rather than replacing it.

Cons

Focused on Shopify storefront support rather than back-end operations or outbound voice.

How to Choose an AI Agent for Your Retail or E-commerce Business

The right choice depends on which layer of your operation you are trying to automate first.

If post-purchase and operations are priorities, HappyRobot is the clear answer. No other tool on this list handles real-time voice at the operations layer with the same depth.

For Shopify brands focused on on-site conversion, Rep AI or Alhena AI are the strongest options. 

For support teams already on a Helpdesk, Gorgias and Intercom Fin slot in with low friction.

Enterprise teams needing true omnichannel automation should evaluate Ada with realistic deployment timelines in mind. 

SMB teams on a budget should start with Tidio. 

Brands for which voice and tone are competitive differentiators should consider Siena, while retention marketing is best handled by Klaviyo. Complex catalogs needing guided selling belong to Zoovu.

The most important factor to consider is that operations and storefront are distinct layers, and the best-performing retail tech stacks pair an agent for each. HappyRobot automates the calls and messages that exceed your team’s capacity, thereby making it easier to scale. A storefront agent handles on-site conversion. Together, they cover the full customer journey from discovery to delivery.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is the best AI agent for retail and e-commerce?+
    HappyRobot is the strongest choice when you need autonomous action in operations and post-purchase workflows, while Alhena AI and Rep AI are better for shopping and support. The best option depends on whether your priority is fulfillment, conversion, or customer service.
  • What is the difference between an e-commerce AI agent and a chatbot?+
    An e-commerce AI agent can take actions like updating orders, processing returns, or triggering workflows, while a chatbot mainly answers questions. HappyRobot is an excellent example of an AI agent as it handles operational workflows end-to-end.
  • Does HappyRobot work for retail and e-commerce?+
    Yes. HappyRobot’s retail page says it tracks orders, updates customers, coordinates with carriers, handles returns, and handles store operations like shift coverage and incident logging. That makes it relevant for both customer communication and back-end retail workflows.
  • Which AI agent is best for Shopify sales?+
    Rep AI is the best fit for Shopify sales because it is built specifically for Shopify Plus and focuses on proactive conversion, product recommendations, and in-chat add-to-cart. If you want a Shopify-native sales concierge, that is the clearest match.

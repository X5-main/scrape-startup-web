The best Smith.ai alternative for answering calls is HappyRobot, whose fully AI voice agent answers, captures, books, and routes every call 24/7, without any human receptionists or per-minute human cost.

Smith.ai runs on a hybrid model, with part AI and part human receptionists. If you want a live person on the line, that works well. If you want every call answered instantly at any volume with no human overhead, the fully-AI lane is where you should be. This article covers both, so no matter which option you’re considering, you’ll find a straight answer.

What is Smith.ai?

Smith.ai is a virtual receptionist and answering service that handles calls and chats using a mix of AI and human receptionists.

Smith.ai gives your business 24/7 call answering, lead capture, appointment booking, and live chat through a combination of AI virtual receptionists and trained human agents. Most Smith.ai reviews highlight that the hybrid model is its main draw. Smith.ai pricing is tiered by volume and billed per minute for human-handled calls, which drives most searches for Smith.ai alternatives as call volume grows.

AI vs Human Answering Services: Which Do You Actually Want?

There are two types of answering services. Some combine AI with human receptionists, while others rely entirely on AI voice agents to answer every call automatically. The right choice depends on whether you want a human voice or unlimited scale.

Virtual live receptionist services put a real person on every call, with a per-minute cost tied to staffing capacity. A fully AI virtual receptionist answers instantly, handles any volume simultaneously, and runs 24/7 without shift limits. For any telephone answering service for small businesses where scale matters, a fully AI solution offers lower costs and more consistent performance.

The 8 Best Smith.ai Alternatives

If you're evaluating Smith.ai alternatives, the right choice depends on how you want calls handled. Some platforms use fully AI voice agents, while others combine AI with human receptionists or rely entirely on live agents. Here are eight options to consider.


1. HappyRobot: Fully AI voice that answers and acts

HappyRobot is the best Smith.ai alternative for businesses that want calls answered fully automatically. Its AI voice agent answers instantly, captures details, books, routes with context, and logs every call, with no human receptionists.

Architecture and integration depth

HappyRobot runs on a proprietary in-house voice stack with built-in speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and end-of-turn detection. Calls get answered instantly in 40+ languages, with real-time classifiers mid-call that verify callers, pull records, and act on what they hear. Persistent contact memory recognizes repeat callers with full context. As an artificial intelligence receptionist and AI orchestration platform, it covers email, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat alongside voice and integrates with web-based systems via browser agents and OCR, with no API required.

Observability and quality control

Automated Northstars audits check agent performance against defined standards and flag issues before they become recurring problems. Documented results include a 100% answer rate and a zero-minute first response. The differentiator over any human answering service or message-taking tool is that HappyRobot acts. It verifies, books, and routes calls with full context while generating a structured audit record for every interaction, including the transcript, recording, extracted data, duration, and status. 

Deployment model

HappyRobot uses a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) model, in which engineers work directly with your team to build, test, and deploy the system. Most enterprise deployments are completed within a few weeks. Deployment timelines vary by project scope, with proof-of-concept deployments possible in as little as one week and standard enterprise implementations typically taking 1–2 months.

Best for: Businesses that want every customer interaction handled automatically across channels, not just voice.

2. Ruby: Best for a human voice on every call

Ruby is a strong Smith.ai alternative for businesses that want live human receptionists with a polished, on-brand phone presence. Its virtual assistant telephone answering service is staffed by trained human receptionists who answer calls, capture leads, and transfer calls. The platform’s model sits close to Smith.ai’s virtual receptionist service, with real people on the line around the clock. Like any telephone answering service for small businesses that uses live staff, the cost scales with volume and capacity and is capped by headcount. 

Best for: law firms and professional service businesses that need a polished human receptionist experience.

3. AnswerConnect: Best for 24/7 live human coverage

AnswerConnect offers 24/7 live human call answering for businesses that want every call handled by a real person, including nights and weekends. Its live virtual receptionist service pairs human receptionists with software integrations for call answering, lead capture, appointment scheduling, and live chat. The platform integrates with popular CRM, scheduling, and communication tools, making it a good fit for small businesses that want professional call handling with a human touch.

Best for: Small to mid-sized businesses needing round-the-clock live human coverage.

4. Abby Connect: Best for a dedicated receptionist team

Abby Connect provides dedicated teams of AI and human receptionists, a Smith.ai alternative for businesses wanting a consistent receptionist pod that knows their account. Unlike pooled virtual live receptionist services, Abby Connect assigns you a fixed team, so callers interact with the same group repeatedly, and that team learns your clients over time. The trade-off is a higher cost than pooled services and no access to the infinite-scale qualities of a fully AI virtual receptionist. 

Best for: Law firms and consultancies where caller familiarity and professional tone are priorities.

5. Posh: Best for mixed AI and human coverage

Posh is a human answering service and an alternative to Smith.ai for businesses seeking professional call handling from real people. Its US-based receptionists answer calls 24/7, schedule appointments, capture leads, transfer callers, take messages, and follow custom call-handling instructions through the Posh app. It also offers HIPAA-compliant call answering, bilingual receptionists, voicemail transcription, and integrations with legal CRMs. The only challenge is that its integration depth is less than that of a purpose-built artificial intelligence receptionist platform, and it won't match the action-taking depth of a platform like HappyRobot. 

Best for: Small businesses wanting a blend of AI efficiency and a human fallback.

6. Dialpad AI: Best for AI answering inside a phone platform

Dialpad AI is a cloud unified communications-as-a-service (UCaaS) platform with built-in AI transcription and AI-assisted routing. It brings AI voice answering inside a broader business communications platform. It’s useful if you need a phone system upgrade alongside call automation, but it's not a direct replacement for Smith.ai's receptionist. While a dedicated Smith.ai receptionist alternative needs to face outward to your callers, Dialpad is built for internal communications teams.

Best for: Businesses wanting AI-assisted call handling as part of a broader phone system upgrade.

7. Goodcall: Best fully-AI option for simple small business needs

Goodcall is a fully AI phone agent and a Smith.ai alternative for small businesses seeking automated call answering without the costs of a human receptionist. It connects to your Google Business profile and handles FAQs, booking, and basic routing automatically. As the best AI receptionist for straightforward needs, setup is fast, and pricing is accessible. Ruby does not support the same level of system integrations or mid-call automation as HappyRobot. 

Best for: Local service businesses wanting affordable automated answering for common customer inquiries.

8. Rosie AI: Simplest fully-AI option

Rosie AI is a simple, fully AI answering service for businesses wanting straightforward automated call handling without hiring a live receptionist. It answers calls 24/7, responds to common questions, books appointments, filters spam, transfers calls when needed, and sends call summaries by text or email. The setup takes just a few minutes by training the AI on your website or Google Business Profile (GBP), making it one of the easiest and best AI receptionist platforms to deploy.

Best for: Solo operators and very small businesses that just need missed calls answered reliably.

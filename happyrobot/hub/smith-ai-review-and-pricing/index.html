What Is Smith.ai?

Smith.ai is a virtual receptionist and answering service that handles your business calls and website chats using both AI and live human receptionists, 24 hours a day.

Instead of hiring a full-time receptionist, you route your calls to Smith.ai. Depending on which plan you choose, either an AI system or a real person answers the phone, talks to your caller, and handles common front-desk tasks. That includes things like:

  • Answering incoming calls and chats
  • Screening out spam and unwanted calls
  • Capturing and qualifying leads
  • Booking appointments
  • Collecting basic information from new clients
  • Sending call summaries to your CRM or team chat

Smith.ai is popular with small businesses, law firms, medical offices, and other professional service companies that need someone answering the phone but don't want to pay for a full-time in-house employee. The company actually has two separate products: 

  • An AI-first receptionist that leans on artificial intelligence with human backup
  • A more traditional Virtual Receptionist service where a live person handles most of the calls

They're priced very differently, which is part of why Smith.ai's pricing confuses many buyers.

Smith.ai Pricing Explained

Smith.ai charges monthly plans based on how many calls or chats you expect, and then bills you extra for each call beyond that limit, so your real cost depends heavily on your call volume.

Your AI Front Desk

AI Receptionist plans tend to start lower, often somewhere around $150 a month for roughly 50 calls, with the price rising as you add more call capacity.

Your Virtual Front Desk

Human-powered Virtual Receptionist plans start higher, commonly reported around $300 a month for about 30 calls, and climb from there. A mid-tier plan handling around 90 calls can run into the $700 to $800 range, and higher tiers built for 150 to 300 calls can cost well over $1,000 to $2,000 a month.

Overage fees 

These kick in once you use up your included calls. These are typically reported in the $9 to $12 range per extra call, though the exact rate depends on which plan you're on.

Add-ons 

For many businesses, this is where costs can sneak up on you. Things like appointment booking, call recording and transcription, text or email follow-ups, extra CRM integrations, and additional transfer numbers usually cost extra per call or per month. 

Several independent reviews suggest that once you add the features most businesses actually want, your real bill often runs meaningfully higher than the advertised starting price.

The bottom line: Smith.ai's per-call model can feel predictable if your call volume stays steady and low. But if your calls spike during a busy season, a marketing push, or seasonal demand, costs can climb fast. It's worth mapping out your typical monthly call volume, plus any add-ons you'll actually use, before picking a plan.

Smith.ai Review: Is It Worth It, and Is It Legit?

Smith.ai is a legitimate, established company with a long track record. It's genuinely worth considering for small businesses that want professional, human-backed call answering.

Smith.ai is a well-known company in the virtual receptionist space, with a long history and a large base of reviews on sites like G2 and Trustpilot. Reported review scores tend to be good, especially for call quality and professionalism, though satisfaction with pricing and billing is more mixed.

Strengths People Consistently Mention

  • Friendly, professional call handling, especially on the human-receptionist plans
  • Solid lead qualification and appointment booking
  • 24/7 availability
  • Good fit for law firms, medical offices, and other professional services that need a polished first impression
  • Per-call billing, which some callers prefer over per-minute billing since a long, useful conversation doesn't cost more than a short one

Honest Considerations Before Signing Up

  • Costs rise with call volume, and add-ons can push your real bill above the advertised starting price
  • It's an answering and intake service, not a system that runs your broader operations or workflows
  • Because human receptionists work in shifts, call quality can vary slightly from one agent to another
  • If your volume is unpredictable or growing quickly, a per-call model can become expensive to forecast

For a small business with steady, moderate call volume, Smith.ai is a solid, trustworthy choice. For a business that's scaling fast or dealing with unpredictable call spikes, it's worth running the math on what a higher tier plus add-ons and overage would actually cost each month.

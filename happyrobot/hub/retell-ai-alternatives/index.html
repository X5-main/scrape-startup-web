The best Retell AI alternative depends on one question: Do you want to build your AI voice agent or deploy one that's already built? HappyRobot leads this list of Retell AI alternatives for businesses that want production phone agents on a proprietary in-house stack, deployed for them rather than assembled via API.

What is Retell AI, and What Are You Actually Replacing?

Retell AI is a developer platform for building AI voice agents and phone call automation through an API. Teams use it to connect language models, telephony providers, and custom conversation logic into production-ready phone agents.

Choosing among the alternatives to Retell AI starts with knowing how much of that work you want to do yourself. Some platforms give developers another API to build on, while others provide AI phone agents that are already built and ready to deploy.

Retell AI sits alongside other voice AI companies that focus on infrastructure instead of finished products. Your team manages the integrations, selects the language model, builds the call flows, and maintains the system over time. Full control comes with greater flexibility, but it also means owning the implementation and ongoing maintenance.

Our list of voice AI solutions includes both approaches. HappyRobot is the already-built, already-deployed AI phone agent option. The other six are developer-first platforms for teams that want to build and customize their own AI calling systems.

The 7 Best Retell AI Alternatives

Retell AI isn't the only way to build AI voice agents. Some alternatives to Retell AI are built for developers who want complete control, while others focus on getting AI phone agents live as quickly as possible. 

Here's how these voice AI solutions compare and where each one fits best.

1. HappyRobot: An enterprise voice agent, deployed for you

HappyRobot is the best Retell alternative for businesses that want production phone agents without having to build them. Its AI voice agent is built for real call center workloads and runs on a proprietary in-house stack, deployed for you, rather than an API you assemble yourself.

The AI phone agent is built end-to-end, including speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and end-of-turn detection. It handles inbound, outbound, and callback calls in 40+ languages, with real-time tools and classifiers running mid-call, so the agent can pull data, check conditions, or escalate mid-conversation.

It answers real inbound and outbound call volume as an AI caller that takes action on what it hears, routes calls, and logs every interaction. The same AI workers also run email, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat, executing full workflows across channels. Northstars continuously tracks agent performance against predefined criteria and surfaces failure rates in the dashboard, so issues are caught before they compound. 

Customers using HappyRobot report a 100% answer rate, zero-minute first-response times, an 119× ROI on collections workflows, and up to a 70% reduction in cost per lead. Deployment runs through Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) who build and launch your agents in weeks, so you never need to stand up a voice engineering team. 

Best for: Enterprises that need production phone agents and AI call center software handling real call volume, deployed without a voice engineering team.

2. Vapi: A voice AI API for building custom agents

Vapi is a voice AI API for developers who want to build, test, and deploy custom voice agents at scale. As a strong alternative to Retell AI, it offers model orchestration, real-time monitoring, enterprise-grade configurability, and a sub-500ms average latency. Engineers get full control over call flow, telephony integrations, and prompt logic, making it a fit for teams building an AI call center agent from scratch. Like Retell, it's a toolkit rather than finished AI call center software, so your team owns the integration, prompt design, and maintenance.

Best for: Developer teams with bandwidth to build and maintain a custom voice agent on their own infrastructure.

3. Bland AI: A programmable API for AI phone calls

Bland AI is an AI voice call automation platform built for regulated industries where security and compliance aren't optional. It’s often evaluated alongside Retell AI and is a good fit for teams that need a calling AI layer for compliance-heavy environments. Developers get a programmable API with configurable scripts and call logic for outbound and inbound calling at scale. Often evaluated alongside Retell AI, Bland is a good fit for teams that need a calling AI layer for compliance-heavy environments.

Best for: Engineering teams wiring outbound and inbound calling into regulated industries like finance, healthcare, or legal.

4. Synthflow: A no-code builder for voice agents

Synthflow is an enterprise-ready AI call center software with built-in telephony, a no-code voice agent builder, and 99.99% uptime. It has handled 65M+ minutes and 4M+ calls, with ROI reported in weeks. As a call center AI software, teams with prebuilt CRM integrations and production-ready AI voice agents without writing code, making it a fit for ops-led groups that want call center AI software without an engineering team.

Best for: Ops-led teams that want enterprise-ready voice AI agents without writing integration code from scratch.

5. Vocode: An open-source framework for voice AI

Vocode is an open-source voice AI library for building voice agents on LLMs, with a hosted service for automating inbound and outbound calls via API. As one of the Retell AI alternatives, it offers conversation abstractions, emotion tracking, endpointing, and STT/TTS provider integrations across telephony, web, and Zoom. Engineers can self-host or use the hosted tier, making it the most flexible voice AI assistant option here for teams with strict compliance or customization needs.

Best for: Engineers with compliance or hosting requirements that demand full control over the voice pipeline.

6. Telnyx Voice AI: A voice API with carrier-grade telephony

Telnyx is a full-stack voice AI platform running on its own global telecom network. Customers switching to this AI call center solution typically save 45% on call center voice AI costs, with pay-as-you-go and volume-based pricing options. It covers inbound and outbound calling, SMS, and LLM flexibility across multiple providers, making it a strong pick for teams that need AI for phone calls and carrier infrastructure under one roof.

Best for: Teams that want to own telephony infrastructure and the voice AI layer with a single vendor.

7. Goodcall: A fully-AI phone agent for small businesses

Goodcall is an agentic voice AI assistant born at Google, with 60.8M+ AI agent interactions and 50,000+ users. Unlike the API-first AI call center solutions above, you can launch a custom AI phone agent in minutes with no engineering team, CRM integrations built in, and HIPAA compliance included. It's fully self-serve and the lightest path to automated call answering on this list.

Best for: Small to mid-sized businesses that want a working AI phone agent live in minutes, no build required.

What Is an AI Virtual Receptionist?

An AI virtual receptionist is a software system designed to provide answering services and independently respond to inbound phone calls. It greets the caller and determines whether they need to route the call to the right person or take action directly within a connected system, such as your CRM. 

Unlike a human receptionist or a live answering service, an automated receptionist runs around the clock and answers the hundredth call of the night the same way it answered the first. 

An artificial intelligence receptionist serves as front-desk automation for the entire intake process, which goes well beyond what an AI front-desk greeter does.


What Should a 24/7 AI Virtual Receptionist Actually Do?

Know what separates a real 24/7 AI answering service from one that only picks up the phone before you compare those platforms.

Four questions do most of the sorting.

  • Does it answer every call, day and night? The 24/7 claim is the baseline, ensuring the AI phone-answering service still sounds professional at 3 a.m., when no one on your team is awake to handle a bad call.
  • Does it qualify the caller and route them? Answering the call is a basic feature. You want your virtual receptionist to direct callers to the right outcome based on their reason for calling.
  • Does it act inside your systems? You don’t need someone on your team to open the CRM afterward and put in some manual work. The better option is an AI answering service that can update the record or book the appointment while the caller is still on the line.
  • Can you predict the bill? Per-minute and per-call pricing spikes your cost in exactly the busiest months, so look for pricing that stays steady regardless of call volume, either a flat monthly fee or a rate tied to completed outcomes like a booking made.

Best AI Virtual Receptionists With 24/7 Service (2026): At a Glance


1. HappyRobot: Best AI Virtual Receptionist for Enterprise Operations That Cannot Afford to Drop the Ball

HappyRobot deploys AI workers across voice, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat, and connects them to the systems your business runs on.

On a call, the worker speaks with a human-like voice in 30+ languages, and its transcription is tuned for industry accents and jargon. This way, a caller who switches to Spanish or rattles off a part number is understood the first time, and the worker responds without delay.

Because HappyRobot maintains a persistent contact profile (via entity mapping) built with every interaction, it recognizes the caller and retrieves the correct record from a connected system. 

When that record exists only in an older system without an API, HappyRobot navigates it as a human operator would through its omni-channel AI workers. This way, a single call becomes an entry point into a larger pool of tasks triggered by the customer query.

Here’s what sets HappyRobot apart from every other tool in this comparison:

  • Inbound calls are answered around the clock with no concurrency limits, so a sudden surge of simultaneous callers is all answered immediately rather than held in a queue.
  • Agentic AI runs alongside deterministic workflow logic, allowing you to decide which steps the conversational virtual receptionist can take on its own and which require approval.
  • HappyRobot assigns Forward Deployed Engineers who embed in your operations. They map your workflows and connect your systems, and they hold personal accountability for getting you into production in weeks.

At Encompass Supply Chain Solutions, AI workers handle roughly 1,700 inbound calls per week and resolve about 64% of them without human involvement.

Best for: large enterprises in banking, telecom, utilities, manufacturing, retail, and logistics, where the receptionist use case is really the front door to a full operational AI workforce.

2. Smith AI: Best for SMB and Professional Services Needing Human Backup

Smith AI runs a hybrid answering service that pairs an AI receptionist with human agents. The AI answers first and handles routine work, such as qualifying the caller and booking the appointment. When a call gets complicated, a live virtual receptionist takes over.

Pricing starts at around $95 per month for the AI tier and around $300 per month for only 30 human-answered calls, with overage fees on both.

EverHelp's review roundup reports a 4.7/5 rating on G2, where reviewers repeatedly note that callers do not realize they have reached a virtual answering service.

The same roundup flags a recurring Trustpilot complaint in which the AI sometimes transfers a call to a live agent without being asked, and because billing is per call, this pushes the monthly bill higher than expected. 

Best for: SMBs and professional services firms that want AI speed with a human fallback.

3. Ruby Receptionists: Best for Professional Services That Want Human Warmth

Ruby runs a human-first virtual receptionist service with receptionists. They answer and screen calls, take messages, book appointments, and collect payments. It can also handle your call instructions and forward calls in the background, so you can change how calls are routed without phoning in.

Plans bundle 24/7 coverage with bilingual answering, and the most popular receptionist plans run from 50 to 500 minutes per month.

Capterra reviewers describe the receptionists as professional and reliable, and one law firm wrote that the service feels like having an actual receptionist in the office.

The same Capterra page rates value for money at 3.8/5. Per-minute billing with overage charges draws the sharpest criticism, with a reviewer reporting paying $650 per month for 200 minutes.

Best for: solo practitioners and small professional services firms that prioritize how callers are treated on the phone.

4. Rosie AI: Best for Small Businesses Wanting Simple, Affordable 24/7 Coverage

Rosie is an AI-only answering service and AI phone receptionist for small businesses. It answers calls around the clock and handles the FAQs that you set up during onboarding. On its higher tiers, it also books appointments and sends a summary after every call. On the integration front, it connects to other tools through Zapier.

Plans start at $49 per month for 250 minutes, and English and Spanish are included on every plan.

Ainora's review rates Rosie a clean upgrade over voicemail for solo operators, because setup needs no API work or flow design, only your business details and call forwarding.

The same review finds that Rosie handles scripted calls well but becomes generic for multi-part or unusual requests, and its flat configuration does not scale to multiple departments or locations as a business grows.

Best for: small businesses with straightforward call needs that want affordable 24/7 coverage.

5. AnswerConnect: Best for Businesses Needing Bilingual Live Agent Backup

AnswerConnect runs a live answering service staffed entirely by human agents, with a stated pledge that every call is handled by a real person, not a bot, even though the coverage runs 24/7. The agents answer and qualify the call, then take a message or book the appointment as your script directs. Most clients come from fields such as law and healthcare, and bilingual English-Spanish answering is available as a paid add-on.

G2 reviewers speak positively about its reliability, and staffing and healthcare clients describe round-the-clock coverage that stops them from missing an after-hours call.

The same reviews flag agent consistency as the weak spot, since some agents are hard to hear and a few drift from the script or miss the tone. On cost, AnswerFirst's comparison puts plans at $325 per month for 100 minutes, up to $1,645 for 900 minutes, with calls rounded up and overage fees charged for any usage beyond your plan.

Best for: businesses with steady English-Spanish call volume that want live humans as the primary response model.


6. Goodcall: Best for Local Businesses Using the Google Ecosystem

Goodcall runs an AI phone agent for local businesses and plugs into Google Business Profile, Google Calendar, and basic CRM tools. 

The AI auto attendant answers routine questions about hours, location, and services, and can take messages and book appointments via scheduling integrations.

Plans start at $79 per month per agent with unlimited minutes, and a 14-day trial is available.

SchedulingKit's review credits the pre-built templates for restaurants, salons, dental offices, and auto shops, which make setup quick for the most common local business types.

The same review scores Goodcall lowest on features at 3.5, since Goodcall can only handle voice calls. There is no web chat, SMS, or email, and its routing does not extend to complex or multi-department call flows. The review places it firmly outside enterprise use.

Best for: local businesses that already run on Google and want an automated receptionist without leaving that ecosystem.

The equation changes when a missed call turns into an operational gap that someone on your team has to chase down later.

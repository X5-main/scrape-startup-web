CONTEXT

Prevent a missed milestone from costing a delivery

A missed carrier check-in does not stay with the dispatcher. It breaks the dock schedule, misses the delivery commitment, and leaves the shipper, DC, and ops team running on a plan that has already changed.

Our orchestration layer connects every stakeholder to the same live status. When a carrier misses a check-in, our agent calls the driver for an updated ETA, alerts the shipper, adjusts the dock appointment, and logs the exception before a human notices the gap.

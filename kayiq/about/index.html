Why We Built KayIQ

Three transformative forces converged, leaving enterprise QA entirely unequipped to handle the shift.

Digital products now integrate dozens of systems. Every new dependency is a new failure surface, and traditional QA cannot keep up.

The Vibe Coding Wave

Generative AI and low-code tools supercharge development velocity, but test coverage lags behind, compounding risk with every single sprint.

UX = Business Outcomes

User experience now directly affects revenue, retention, and brand equity. QA is a strategic control layer, not a cost center.

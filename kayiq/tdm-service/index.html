Testing quality is only as strong as the data behind it. Our TDM service removes provisioning friction so teams have secure, compliant, and fit-for-purpose test data available exactly when they need it.

Core Service Capabilities

Transforming raw and sensitive data into secure, high-utility assets for continuous quality engineering.

Synthetic Data Generation

Relying only on production data limits your ability to test rare edge cases and future-state features. We use advanced AI models to generate net-new test data from scratch.

Production-Grade Realism

Generate realistic datasets that preserve statistical behavior and relational consistency found in production systems.

Edge-Case Coverage

Create data for negative paths, extreme scenarios, and upcoming features that do not yet exist in production.

Zero PII Exposure

Fully synthetic data eliminates exposure to personally identifiable information and compliance risk.

Data Masking & Obfuscation

When production-like data is required for complex states, we neutralize sensitive fields while preserving usability for testing.

Irreversible Anonymization

Apply tokenization, hashing, and obfuscation to sensitive attributes such as names, IDs, and financial records.

Referential Integrity Maintenance

Mask consistently across distributed stores so keys and end-to-end business flows stay valid during tests.

Compliance Assurance

Align masking controls with GDPR, CCPA, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and internal enterprise security policies.

Dynamic Data Provisioning

Static datasets become stale and block automation. We treat data as code and integrate provisioning directly into CI/CD.

Pipeline Injection

Deliver environment-specific test data automatically when build and test pipelines are triggered.

Self-Service Provisioning

Enable developers and QA teams to provision fit-for-purpose datasets in minutes without DBA bottlenecks.

Automated Refresh & Teardown

Reset or destroy datasets after runs to prevent contamination and keep non-prod environments stable.

Data Subsetting

Full production clones are costly and slow. We extract high-value subsets tailored to business and test requirements.

Targeted Extraction

Slice databases into fit-for-purpose micro-datasets driven by rules, journeys, and scenario coverage goals.

Storage Optimization

Reduce infrastructure footprint and cloud costs by avoiding large full-clone environments.

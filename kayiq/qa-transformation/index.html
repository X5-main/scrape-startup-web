Transformation Methodology

A structured, phased approach to sustainable change without disrupting ongoing delivery.

01

Discovery & Maturity Assessment

We assess the current QA landscape, including test strategy, toolchain health, CI/CD maturity, skill gaps, and defect leakage patterns.

02

Target Operating Model (TOM) Design

We define a future-state QA structure tailored to your organization, from team topology to toolchain integration and continuous testing.

03

Strategic Roadmapping

We translate findings into a phased delivery roadmap with practical milestones, dependencies, and measurable outcomes.

04

Enablement & Change Management

We support the transition with coaching, upskilling, and proof-of-concept delivery so teams can adopt the new operating model with confidence.

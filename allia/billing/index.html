Revenue cycle

Get paid accurately, quickly, and without the chaos.

From eligibility to remittance, every step of the revenue cycle runs in one connected system — so claims go out clean and cash comes in faster.

Coverage verified before every session

Eligibility is only as good as how recently it was checked. Allia re-verifies before every session — not just at intake — so mid-year changes never become AR nightmares.

Claims that move faster

A clearinghouse-first architecture submits claims without manual intervention. Practices on Allia submit cleaner claims than 98% of independent behavioral health practices.

Nothing sits. Nothing gets lost.

Denied claims surface with the reason, the details, and the recommended next step — so practices on Allia recover 78% of denied claims vs. a 45% industry average.

Denial reasons surfaced automatically, with next steps

Appeals and resubmissions managed in-platform with an audit trail

Fast ERA processing — remittance reconciled automatically

Full visibility across your revenue cycle

See what’s been billed, paid, denied, adjusted, and outstanding — across clients, clinicians, payers, locations, and CPT codes, without a single export.

Real-time AR by clinician, payer, and CPT code

Clear checklists for missing payments and unresolved denials

Revenue and retention reports generated automatically

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about billing on Allia.

What claims performance metrics do you track?

Acceptance rates, denial rates, days in AR, reimbursement timelines, payer performance, and clean-claim rates.

What states do you support?

We support organizations nationwide, across diverse state-specific billing and regulatory requirements.

Do you support electronic remittances?

Yes. Electronic remittances are set up and managed for all clients.

How do encounters, billing, and claim submission work end-to-end?

Encounters flow directly from clinical documentation into billing. The system validates required fields, flags issues before submission, and tracks each claim through adjudication.

Can Allia help us get better payer contracts?

The Allia Clinical Network gives independent practices access to payer infrastructure and contracting usually available only to larger organizations. Participation is selective.

Does Allia help with credentialing?

Credentialing support is available through the Allia Clinical Network, where clinicians can typically be credentialed in under 20 days — helping practices get to revenue faster.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many providers are on the platform?

More than 7,000 clinicians nationwide — including therapists, psychiatrists, PMHNPs, and LCSWs — use Allia. The system is designed for the full continuum of behavioral health, from therapy and medication management to interventional psychiatry like TMS.

What kind of support does Allia provide?

Allia offers live 24/7 support, with additional business-hours support available by phone.

How quickly can a new clinician start using Allia?

The average time to onboard a new clinician is about 5 minutes. Allia is built for speed, with AI-powered documentation that lets clinicians spend time with patients instead of forms.

Is Allia HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Allia is a HIPAA-compliant platform with end-to-end encryption and secure data handling, plus audit-ready documentation, role-based permissions, and support for state-specific behavioral health requirements.

How does billing work in Allia?

Allia includes a native RCM engine that powers expert billing with real-time visibility, fast follow-up, and transparent reporting — one system for referrals, clinical notes, and billing, so you never bounce between tools.

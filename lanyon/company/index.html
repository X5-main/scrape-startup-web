About the
Lanyon Team

Lanyon AI was founded by leading mathematicians and physicists, united by a deep-seated intellectual curiosity. We are always seeking exceptional researchers who share our passion for building the future of scientific AI.

Our foundation

Math ∩ Physics ∩ AI

Our work builds upon many decades of pioneering research by our founding team, spanning automatic code-generation, automated theorem-proving, scientific AI, and the computational foundations of physics.

Now, as AI promises to revolutionize the nature of computation, we are working tirelessly to build the formally verified substrate that will unify the computational and physical universes into a single coherent reality.

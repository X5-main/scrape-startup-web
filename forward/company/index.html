A note from the founders

Forward was born from time spent inside private credit, watching promising deals slow down for reasons that had little to do with credit. Information was scattered, lender mandates were opaque, and too much of the work between first look and close was manual.

We believe the market needs a common language. Forward structures borrower financials, directs each opportunity to funds it genuinely fits, and gives everyone a clearer path through diligence and execution, while leaving judgment and relationships where they belong - people.

Our aim is to make private credit work as one connected market. That is, less time chasing documents, less deal fatigue, and more capital reaching the businesses ready to put it to work.

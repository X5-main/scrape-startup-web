Built to handle the messy reality of accounting

Schedules, integrations, bulk data handling, and infrastructure built for modern finance teams

Automated intercompany eliminations

Rillet automatically records eliminations for each intercompany journal. Forget about intricate excel models to oversee consolidation or foreign exchange impacts – all aspects are automatically managed within the platform.

Consolidated and entity-view reporting

Report at both the entity and consolidated levels, with clear insights into the effects of eliminations. Transitioning between views is effortless—with only the flip of a switch—financial analysis made easy.

FX effect automatically calculated

Effortlessly manage transactions regardless of the currency, all FX and revaluations are performed automatically. Generate reports in your base currency while also accessing detailed transactions in the local currency when needed.

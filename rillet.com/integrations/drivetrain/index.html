Native integration with Drivetrain

Connect Rillet to Drivetrain's financial intelligence platform to turn clean, real-time actuals into instant forecasts, variance analyses, and insights.

Request a demo


End-to-end, real-time financials from ERP to FP&A

Sync Rillet’s structured, always-fresh general ledger directly into Drivetrain to give finance a single governed source of truth for actuals, plans, and reports. No more CSV exports, manual reconciliations, or version confusion—just live data flowing straight into the models you rely on.



AI-powered insight where FP&A actually works

Layer Drivetrain's AI-driven planning and analysis on top of Rillet to automate variance analysis, generate forecasts, and answer ad hoc questions in plain language. Finance teams stay in Excel, Google Sheets, the browser, or chat while AI does the heavy lifting in the background.



Get closer to a Zero-Day Close

Combine Rillet’s automated, AI-ready close with Drivetrain’s intelligent reporting to shorten the distance between booked transactions and board-ready insight. Drill from top-line metrics down to transaction-level detail in a click, so your team spends time guiding the business—not chasing numbers.

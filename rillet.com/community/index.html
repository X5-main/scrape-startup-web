Who is it for

Your peers are already in the room

Close Club is where CFOs, Controllers, and finance practitioners come to think out loud, share what's working, and get sharper at their jobs. Close Club is a strictly no sales zone — and we carefully vet every application to make sure the conversation stays high quality. Just real conversations between people navigating the same challenges you are. Whether you're implementing AI into your close process or figuring out how to lead through uncertainty, you'll find people here who've been in the exact same seat.

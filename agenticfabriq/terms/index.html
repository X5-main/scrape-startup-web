Slack Integration

This section governs your use of the Agentic Fabriq Slack App.

Overview of the Service

The Agentic Fabriq Slack App enables users to connect their Slack workspace with the Agentic Fabriq platform to:

  • Bind Slack user identities to Agentic Fabriq identities
  • Allow AI agents to act on behalf of the authenticated user
  • Enforce permissioning and access-control rules for agent actions
  • Retrieve message content that the user is authorized to access (with explicit OAuth consent)
  • Provide auditing, logging, and visibility into agent-driven Slack activity

User Responsibilities

You are responsible for all actions taken by AI agents acting on your behalf in Slack. The App will never access messages or channels that the user does not already have access to within Slack.

Notion Integration

This section governs your use of the Agentic Fabriq Notion Integration.

Service Description

The Integration connects your Notion workspace to AF so your apps/agents can read Notion content you explicitly share with the Integration and perform user-requested operations (e.g., summarization, extraction, routing). You decide which AF features to enable and which Notion items to share.

The problem

Support agents are increasingly expected to do more than draft responses. They may retrieve account data, change plans, issue credits, reset credentials, cancel orders, or update customer records.

The danger is not only incorrect answers. It is an agent taking a valid action on the wrong customer, exceeding a support representative’s authority, or exposing data from another account.

Agentic Fabriq applies customer, representative, and action-level controls before each tool call.

The stakes

Why this is hard today

  1. 01Support agents don’t just draft replies anymore — they issue refunds, reset credentials, and change plans.
  2. 02A right action on the wrong customer is as bad as a wrong answer, and much harder to spot.
  3. 03The agent helping a junior rep can quietly do things that rep was never allowed to do alone.
  4. 04One crossed account in a support chat is a data breach, not a typo.
Capabilities

How Fabriq helps support teams

Scope access to the customer associated with the active case.The ticket defines the reachable customer; browsing beyond the case simply is not possible.
Limit agent actions based on the human representative’s own authority.The agent can do at most what the rep it assists could do — automation never exceeds the person behind it.
Set refund, credit, cancellation, and replacement thresholds.Routine amounts execute instantly while anything above the line routes to a supervisor, per policy.
Require escalation for high-risk or unusual cases.Signals like account age, amount, or verification status trigger a human handoff automatically.
Prevent cross-customer data leakage.Per-case scoping means even a manipulated request cannot surface another account’s data.
Control when an agent may communicate externally versus draft for review.Policy decides which messages send directly and which wait for a rep to review and own.
Preserve the evidence and reasoning behind each resolution.Every resolution carries what was read, what was done, and who approved — ready for any dispute.

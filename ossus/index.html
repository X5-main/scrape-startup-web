Libraries are history’s oldest storage for intelligence. We are building it a new one.

Cultural flourishing has always been built on infrastructure. The first Renaissance rose on the printing press, the public library, and the catalogue. What comes next will rise on something we have not built yet.

Today libraries run on aging systems and manual work that swallows the hours meant for curation and connection. This is not cultural decline. It is infrastructural neglect, and Ossus exists to change that.

01

Give the best hours back

Ossus takes the admin off the desk, the hours that should belong to readers, and returns them to curation, connection, and craft.

02

Judgment stays human

AI will not generate culture. It reads the spines, runs the inventory, and matches the records, so the work only a librarian can do stays with the librarian.

03

Privacy by design

We do not train on your data and we do not sell it. Ossus reads catalogue metadata, never borrower records, so the intelligent parts touch the least personal data.

04

Built for the long term

Libraries are civic infrastructure. We build from inside the work, alongside the institutions that run on it, and to the standards they are held to.

Add-ons

Beyond the core ILS, Ossus adds a set of agents that take the slow, recurring work off the desk, from keeping the shelves accurate to buying well and funding the year ahead.

AI-assisted Inventory

Save 80% of time spent on inventory. Take a picture up to 30 books while they're still on the shelf, and AI automatically identifies the books by reading the spines. It does inventory and identifies misplaced books simultaneously.

Vendor Comparison Agent

Save 5-10% on all your book orders. Drop in a purchase list and the agent splits it across different book vendors. It weighs the price of the book, MARC records, processing and more—and tells you the cheapest way to fill the order.

Grant Agent

Make more money from grants. Our agent scans thousands of sources to find every grant you qualify for, tracks the deadlines, and drafts each application from what it already knows about your collection and community.

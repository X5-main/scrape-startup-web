Frequently asked questions

Do I qualify for a student discount even if I don’t have a .edu email?

Yes! We accept alternate forms of student verification like a student ID, enrollment confirmation, fee receipt, or a screenshot of your student dashboard. If you're studying at a university without .edu emails or are attending online programs, you're still eligible.

Fill out the form here: Enable the Education Discount for your Flow account

What languages does Flow support? Can I use it if I write in multiple languages?

Yes! Flow supports 100+ languages for dictation. A lot of students use it to write in multiple languages—like English and Spanish or French and Arabic. You can switch between them easily, and Flow auto-detects as you go.

What do I get when I refer a friend?

You and your friend both get a free month of Flow Pro when they hit 2,000 words or upgrade to Pro. Just share your referral link with your friend — you can find it in your desktop app under “Refer a Friend” on the home screen.

Can I use headphones with Flow?

Yes, you can! But for the best results, we recommend using your device’s built-in mic or a wired one instead of a bluetooth connection.

Read more here.

What can I run Flow on—mobile, desktop, both?

Flow works on MacOS, Windows and iOS for now!

Is Flow accessible for students with disabilities?

Absolutely. Flow is designed with accessibility in mind and supports a wide range of needs and learning styles. Many students who face barriers to traditional input methods have found Flow to be a powerful tool for expressing their thoughts.

Can educators or non-profit staff also apply for a discount?

Yes! If you're a teacher, researcher, or work with a non-profit, you can apply for our educator/non-profit discount using your .edu email.

If you don’t have one, just fill this form to get access.

Can I use Flow to transcribe audio or video files like lecture recordings?

Not right now—we don’t support file uploads for transcription just yet.

I’m on a tight budget. Is there any way to get Flow for free or at a lower cost?

We offer a sweet student discount, and in special cases (like accessibility needs or financial hardship), we're always happy to work with you. One of the easiest ways to get Flow for free is by referring a friend — once you both hit 2000 words, you'll both get a free month!

Our values

01.  Unreasonably ambitious and self-improving

We dream of building a better way for a billion people to interface with technology. That ambition sets the bar — we hold ourselves to exceptional work and keep raising our standards.

02.  Kind and direct

Silicon Valley operates in two extremes: diplomatic and non-confrontational, or rude and aggressive. We believe you can be both kind and direct. We're working intensely toward ambitious outcomes — we might as well have fun doing it.

03.  Truth seeking

We think from first principles and question our assumptions. We're obsessed with finding the real problem before we solve anything. We're not attached to ideas because they're ours. We'd rather be right together than win an argument.

04.  Opinionated about simplicity

We simplify everything we do because “the main thing is the main thing.” We identify the one or two most important problems and nail the details. Nothing else matters if we get the high-order bit wrong.

05.  Tuned into emotion

We notice how every experience feels for our customers. We help people feel understood, give them aha moments, and craft for delight, not just utility.

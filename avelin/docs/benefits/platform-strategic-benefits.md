# Platform Strategic Benefits

## Strategic Value Summary

AVELIN transforms AI from fragmented experimentation into a governed enterprise intelligence operating layer.

Built on **three pillars** — **AI Lab**, **Cloud Token Factory**, and **Enterprise Platform** — and powered by **Cross-Model MoE** technology that fuses open models into 9 specialist families, AVELIN delivers:

- **up to 85% lower cost** than GPT-5.5 for intelligence workloads (AA Index: 55)
- **up to 86% lower cost** than Claude Opus for coding workloads (AA Index: 52)
- **up to 87% lower cost** than GPT-5.5 for agentic workloads (AA Index: 67)
- Sovereign governance with no vendor lock-in
- Continuous self-optimization via real-time benchmarking

By combining AVELIN-GPT, AVELIN-API, AVELIN-MCP, and Y-RAY under one sovereign framework, organizations gain stronger control, faster decisions, higher reliability, and long-term flexibility.

**Strategic impact**

## 2) Cross-Model MoE Advantage — 9 Families, Zero Lock-In

AVELIN-API orchestrates 9 specialist model families behind one control surface. The 3-pillar architecture ensures vendor-agnostic operation across Intelligence, Coding, and Agentic domains.

**Strategic impact**

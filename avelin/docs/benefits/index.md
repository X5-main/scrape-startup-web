# Benefits and Advantages

This section explains how AVELIN creates measurable business value across three dimensions: strategic positioning, operational execution, and competitive differentiation.

AVELIN is an **AI Intelligence Platform** built on three pillars — **AI Lab**, **Cloud Token Factory**, and **Enterprise Platform** — and powered by **Cross-Model MoE** technology that fuses open models into 9 specialist families. This architecture delivers:

- **up to 85% lower cost** than GPT-5.5 for intelligence workloads (AA Index score: 55)
- **up to 86% lower cost** than Claude Opus for coding workloads (AA Index score: 52)
- **up to 87% lower cost** than GPT-5.5 for agentic workloads (AA Index score: 67)

All benchmark scores sourced from Artificial Analysis, June 2026, v4.x composite index (pass@1, 9–10 evals per family).

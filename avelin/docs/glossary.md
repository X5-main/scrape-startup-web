# Business Glossary

This glossary defines business and operating terms used across AVELIN documentation.

## A

**A.V.E.L.I.N.**  
Artificial Virtual Entity Learning Interaction Natively. The sovereign AI platform for business.

**Action Superpowers**  
The ability to move from analysis to execution through connected business actions, not only chat responses.

**Adaptive Threat Defense**  
Perimeter behavior that detects suspicious traffic patterns and reduces harmful traffic impact before core services are affected.

**Anonymization Layer**  
A trust control that removes sensitive identity patterns before external processing when policy requires it.

**AI Intelligence Platform**  
AVELIN's positioning as a sovereign AI operating layer for business, organized around 3 pillars and Cross-Model MoE technology.

**AI Lab**  
One of AVELIN's 3 pillars — advanced reasoning, research, and decision-grade intelligence capabilities.

**Audit Readiness**  
The ability to produce complete, timely governance and activity evidence for internal and external review.

**Avatar Marketplace**  
A catalog of specialized AI personas and domain-focused assistants that can be activated for specific tasks.

**AA Index (AVELIN Architecture Index)**  
Scoring system for model tier capability alignment: ultra (55), pro (53), architect (52), plus (49), coding (42), agentic (67), fast (47). Used for intelligent routing and cost optimization.

**AVELIN-API**  
The orchestration and governance layer that fuses and routes model intelligence.

**AVELIN-GPT**  
The conversational workspace where users analyze, decide, automate, and execute daily workflows.

**AVELIN-MCP**  
The integration platform that extends AVELIN with high-performance execution capabilities.

## B

**Business Continuity**  
The capability to keep critical workflows available during incidents, traffic stress, or platform changes.

**Business Workflow Criticality**  
The business importance level of a workflow, used to prioritize incident response and governance controls.

## C

**Classic Mode**  
Standard question-and-answer mode for routine interactions.

**Cloud Token Factory**  
One of AVELIN's 3 pillars — cost-optimized, high-throughput model orchestration and token governance. Delivers Intelligence up to 85%, Coding up to 86%, and Agentic up to 87% cheaper than premium alternatives.

**Compliance Evidence**  
Documented records that demonstrate policy adherence, control execution, and governance quality.

**Corporate Brain**  
Enterprise knowledge intelligence built from internal documents, records, and institutional memory.

**Cross-Model MoE**  
AVELIN's core technology that combines multiple intelligence models into unified outputs using a trained meta-model, not a router. A prompt-vector classifier with 90% accuracy assigns each request to the optimal tier through difficulty-based cascading for cost and quality balance.

**Cross-System Trust Enforcement**  
Consistent trust and policy controls applied across AVELIN-GPT, AVELIN-API, AVELIN-MCP, and Y-RAY.

## D

**Data Residency**  
The defined location or boundary where sensitive data is processed and retained.

**Data Sovereignty**  
The principle that organizations keep control of where data is processed, stored, and governed.

**Debate Mode**  
A multi-perspective reasoning mode where contrasting intelligence paths improve balance and rigor.

**Decision-Grade Output**  
An output format suitable for high-impact decisions, with evidence quality and policy alignment.

**Distributed Intelligence**  
An operating model where multiple intelligence capabilities work together as one governed platform.

## E

**Enterprise Platform**  
One of AVELIN's 3 pillars — integration, execution, trust, and operations for business workflows.

**Enterprise Trust Layer**  
The combined policy, access, retention, and quality-control capabilities that keep AI usage safe and auditable.

**Evidence-First Intelligence**  
A decision approach that prioritizes verifiable signals and traceable support over unsupported output.

**Execution Workflow**  
A structured path that connects analysis, approval, and action completion for a business task.

## G

**Governance Cadence**  
The scheduled rhythm for control reviews, KPI reviews, risk checks, and policy decisions.

**Governed Channel**  
A platform path where trust, policy, and access controls are actively enforced and monitored.

**Guardrails**  
Rules that keep behavior, outputs, and actions aligned with business policy and risk standards.

## H

**Hybrid Deployment**  
A deployment model that combines strict internal zones for sensitive workloads with broader infrastructure for lower-risk workloads.

## I

**Incident Severity**  
A business-impact classification that determines response urgency, escalation level, and owner involvement.

**Integration Governance**  
Control of connector permissions, ownership, and safe action execution across integrated systems.

**Intelligence Governance**  
Control of routing, cost, output quality, and policy alignment for model-driven workflows.

## K

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**  
A measurable signal used to evaluate reliability, productivity, quality, governance, and business outcomes.

## L

**Least Privilege**  
An access model where users and services receive only the permissions needed for their responsibilities.

**Local Server Deployment**  
A sovereignty-first deployment model where AVELIN runs inside organization-controlled infrastructure.

## M

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)**  
A standard way for AI systems to access external tools and business services in controlled workflows.

**Memory Mode**  
An interaction mode that uses persistent context to improve continuity and personalization over time.

**Model Alias**  
A business-friendly model name used inside AVELIN to simplify routing and user experience.

**Multi-Depth Analysis**  
A structured approach to research depth, from rapid scan to deep diligence depending on decision importance.

## P

**Perimeter Layer**  
The secure entry boundary that applies transport protection, traffic controls, and threat defense.

**Policy Exception**  
A temporary, approved deviation from a standard control, with owner, reason, and expiry.

**Private Cloud Deployment**  
A controlled cloud model that keeps governance boundaries while supporting enterprise scale.

## R

**Research-to-Execution Continuum**  
The end-to-end path from intelligence gathering to business action completion in one governed flow.

**Retention Policy**  
The rule set that defines how long data, logs, and evidence are kept before deletion.

**Route Policy**  
Rules that determine how requests are directed across intelligence paths based on quality, cost, and risk.

## S

**Smart Routing Economy**  
A routing strategy that sends each task to the most appropriate intelligence path for quality and cost efficiency.

**Sovereign AI Platform**  
An AI operating model where the organization controls infrastructure, governance, and critical data boundaries. AVELIN is positioned as an AI Intelligence Platform with 3 pillars and Cross-Model MoE technology.

**Sovereign Deployment**  
A deployment posture where control over data boundaries, policy enforcement, and trust operations remains with the organization.

## T

**Token Counting**  
Measurement of usage volume for cost tracking, governance, and optimization.

**Traceability**  
The ability to follow who performed what action, when, and under which policy context.

**Trust Controls**  
Security, policy, privacy, and quality controls that reduce risk in sensitive AI workflows.

## V

**Vendor Agnostic Intelligence**  
The ability to use and switch between multiple model families without being locked to one provider path.

## Y

**Y-RAY**  
The information search and analysis product that combines internal corporate intelligence with external research intelligence.

**Y-RAY Alpha Scan**  
Rapid intelligence mode for quick pulse updates and immediate signal detection.

**Y-RAY Deep Diligence**  
Investigation mode for claim-by-claim validation, contradiction detection, and decision-grade synthesis.

**Y-RAY Structural Breakdown**  
Analytical mode for mapping relationships, risks, dependencies, and strategic structure.

## Z

**Zero-Retention Environment**  
A controlled processing mode where persistent history can be minimized or disabled for sensitive workflows.

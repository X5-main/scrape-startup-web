# Operations and Observability

## 1) What This System Is

Operations and Observability is AVELIN's reliability control system, providing the operational governance layer for the **Enterprise Platform** pillar.

It provides health monitoring, incident visibility, operational diagnostics, and recovery support for all platform products and modules, including Cross-Model MoE specialist selection across all 9 specialist model families.

## 2) Who Uses It

- Platform operations teams
- Reliability and governance leads
- Administrators supporting business-critical workflows
- Cloud Token Factory operators monitoring specialist selection efficiency and cost metrics

## 3) Business Problem It Solves

- Outages are costly when teams cannot diagnose root causes quickly
- Growing product scope increases coordination complexity during incidents
- Leadership needs measurable reliability evidence for enterprise trust
- Cross-Model MoE specialist selection requires observability to track quality across model families (AA Index scores from 38 to 67)
- Self-improving platform needs monitoring to validate 48-hour model integration quality

## 4) Core Capabilities

### Real-Time Service Visibility

Provides operational insight into product health and active incidents.

### Health and Readiness Monitoring

Tracks service status and early warning signals before user impact grows.

### Incident Diagnostics

Supports fast issue localization across product boundaries.

### Recovery Workflow Support

Enables structured restoration actions and validation of business-critical paths.

### Reliability KPI Reporting

Consolidates reliability metrics for governance review and improvement planning.

## 5) Major Benefits and Advantages

- Faster mean time to detect and recover
- Reduced business disruption during incidents
- Better cross-team coordination in high-pressure events
- More predictable service quality for user-facing products
- Stronger enterprise confidence through transparent reliability reporting
- Continuous monitoring of Cross-Model MoE specialist selection quality across all 9 specialist families
- Validation of 48-hour model integration quality and performance
- Cost optimization tracking: Intelligence up to 85% cheaper, Coding up to 86% cheaper, Agentic up to 87% cheaper

## 6) Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario: Product Performance Degradation

- Trigger: users report slower responses
- Workflow: operations identifies affected products, traces service path, and applies targeted remediation
- Outcome: faster stabilization with lower broad-impact risk

### Scenario: Integration Workflow Failure

- Trigger: action execution workflows fail
- Workflow: operations verifies integration health, policy state, and route continuity
- Outcome: restoration of execution superpowers with minimal downtime

### Scenario: Monthly Reliability Review

- Trigger: governance cycle requires reliability evidence
- Workflow: operations compiles uptime, incident patterns, and improvement actions
- Outcome: clearer accountability and better planning decisions

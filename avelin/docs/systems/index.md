# Systems and Product Catalog

This section documents AVELIN as an **AI Laboratory** built on three pillars — **AI Lab** (primary), **Intelligence Delivery**, and **Enterprise Platform** — with Cross-Model MoE technology at its core.

AVELIN builds and fuses leading open models into 9 specialist families using trained Cross-Model Mixture-of-Experts technology, delivering enterprise-grade intelligence at significantly lower cost than direct provider access. The platform is self-improving, with new models integrated within 48 hours of release. AVELIN's primary deployment model is enterprise licensing.

## Platform Pillars

| Pillar | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| **AI Lab** (Primary) | Cross-Model MoE research, model training and fusion, specialist family development |
| **Intelligence Delivery** | High-throughput intelligence delivery with Smart Routing Economy and cost optimization |
| **Enterprise Platform** | Governed deployment, security, compliance, and operational control for organizations |

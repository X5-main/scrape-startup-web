# Voice and External Intelligence Layer

## 1) What This System Is

The Voice and External Intelligence Layer extends AVELIN's **Enterprise Platform** pillar with natural interaction and real-time context enrichment.

It combines spoken interaction, external signal retrieval, and context-aware synthesis so users can work faster in dynamic business environments. This layer leverages Cross-Model MoE technology to route voice and external intelligence requests to the optimal model family (Intelligence, Coding, or Agentic) based on task complexity using difficulty-based cascading.

## 2) Who Uses It

- Mobile and executive users who need rapid interaction
- Business users handling time-sensitive communication
- Teams that require current market or ecosystem intelligence

## 3) Business Problem It Solves

- Text-only workflows are slower for many business contexts
- Internal data alone is not enough for fast external decision cycles
- Teams need natural interaction plus up-to-date intelligence in one flow
- Voice interactions require efficient specialist selection to maintain responsiveness (Intelligence family up to 85% cheaper than direct provider access)

## 4) Core Capabilities

### Speech Interaction

Supports spoken input and spoken output for hands-free, low-friction usage.

### External Signal Retrieval

Collects and prioritizes current external information when internal context is insufficient.

### Multi-Mode Interaction Support

Works alongside Classic, Memory, and Debate modes to match task complexity.

### Context Enrichment

Combines internal platform memory with external signals for richer decision support.

### Cross-Device Usability

Helps users continue workflows while moving across environments and devices.

## 5) Major Benefits and Advantages

- Faster interaction for high-tempo business routines
- Better accessibility and broader user adoption
- More current intelligence for communication and planning
- Improved decision relevance in changing environments
- Higher continuity for on-the-move users
- Cross-Model MoE specialist selection ensures voice queries use the optimal specialist family
- Self-improving platform with 48-hour integration for new voice and model capabilities

## 6) Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario: Executive Mobile Briefing

- Trigger: leader needs immediate update between meetings
- Workflow: asks by voice, gets concise spoken summary, requests rapid refinement
- Outcome: quicker decisions with lower friction

### Scenario: External Context Drafting

- Trigger: team prepares customer or market-facing communication
- Workflow: external signals are retrieved and fused with internal context
- Outcome: better-informed messaging and stronger timing

### Scenario: Field Operations Support

- Trigger: user cannot type during active workflow
- Workflow: voice-first interaction captures requests and delivers guidance
- Outcome: higher productivity in constrained environments

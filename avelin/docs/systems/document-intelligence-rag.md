# Y-RAY

## 1) What This System Is

**Y-RAY** is AVELIN's information search and analysis product, powered by Cross-Model MoE technology across the **AI Lab** pillar.

It acts as a **Corporate Brain** that combines internal enterprise knowledge with external intelligence signals, then produces evidence-first outputs for business decisions.

Y-RAY is designed for organizations that need trustworthy research without losing control of sensitive data. It leverages all three model families — Intelligence (AA Index up to 55), Coding (up to 52), and Agentic (up to 67) — to deliver multi-depth analysis at lower cost than direct provider access.

## 2) Who Uses It

- Executive and strategy teams
- Research, product, and operations teams
- Risk, compliance, and due-diligence functions
- Sales and support teams that need validated intelligence quickly

## 3) Business Problem It Solves

- Critical decisions are often made from incomplete or unverified information
- Internal knowledge is fragmented and difficult to reuse at speed
- External intelligence can be noisy, inconsistent, or hard to validate
- Many AI research workflows lack traceability and confidence scoring
- Research costs scale quickly when using direct provider access (Y-RAY delivers up to 85% savings on Intelligence workloads)

## 4) Core Capabilities

### Corporate Brain Intelligence

Transforms internal enterprise content into a searchable, context-rich intelligence memory that can be reused across teams.

### Internal + External Fusion

Combines internal knowledge with live external intelligence to produce more complete analysis.

### Evidence-First Synthesis

Delivers citation-backed conclusions and highlights uncertainty when evidence is insufficient.

### Deep Retrieval for Hard Sources

Designed for difficult research surfaces with resilient retrieval behavior and adaptive analysis workflows.

### Multi-Depth Analysis Modes

- **Alpha Scan**: rapid market pulse and fast signal extraction
- **Structural Breakdown**: strategic mapping of relationships, dependencies, and risk
- **Deep Diligence**: forensic validation for high-stakes decisions

### Long-Run Research Workflows

Supports complex investigations that require multi-step validation and structured synthesis.

## 5) Major Benefits and Advantages

- Decision confidence improves through evidence-backed outputs
- Internal knowledge becomes a reusable enterprise asset
- Research cycle time drops for strategic and operational questions
- Hallucination risk is reduced through validation-first behavior
- Sensitive intelligence workflows remain within sovereign governance boundaries
- Cross-Model MoE delivers up to 85% cost savings on Intelligence workloads vs direct provider access
- Self-improving platform with 48-hour integration for new analysis capabilities

## 6) Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario: Executive Market Pulse

- Trigger: leadership needs immediate clarity on market movement
- Workflow: Alpha Scan compiles key signals and presents high-impact summary points
- Outcome: faster strategic reaction with clearer confidence levels

### Scenario: Risk and Compliance Mapping

- Trigger: organization evaluates policy or regulatory exposure
- Workflow: Structural Breakdown maps internal obligations against external developments
- Outcome: more proactive risk planning and governance alignment

### Scenario: Investment or Vendor Diligence

- Trigger: high-stakes commercial decision requires validation
- Workflow: Deep Diligence verifies claims, identifies contradictions, and surfaces evidence paths
- Outcome: stronger decisions and reduced hidden-risk exposure

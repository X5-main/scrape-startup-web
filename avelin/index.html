//THE TECHNOLOGY · CROSS-MODEL MoE

One question. The best of nine models

Most AI tools send everything to a single model. AVELIN doesn't. For every request, our Cross-Model MoE — a Mixture of Experts across whole models, not just one — routes the work to the best of our nine models (three families for chat, coding, and agents) and combines their strengths. You get higher intelligence than any single model, at lower cost. It picks the right model correctly about 90% of the time.

system://routing
01

It reads your request

A classifier trained on a full year of real usage looks at each request and judges what it needs and how hard it is.

02

It picks the right experts

Simple tasks go to fast, low-cost models; harder ones go to the strongest. When it helps, AVELIN combines several models into one answer.

03

It keeps learning

Every request makes the choice better. AVELIN gets smarter the more it's used, and we add and re-test new open models within 48 hours.

Self-Learning by Design

We improve and re-benchmark our models on a regular cycle, so your AI keeps getting better over time (sharper answers and lower cost) without you changing a thing.

//THE COMPLIANCE TRAP

The best AI is off-limits to the companies that need it most

Regulated companies are stuck: the strongest AI lives in the cloud, but their data has to stay inside. AVELIN is built to close that gap: frontier-grade AI that runs inside your walls, so nothing has to leave.

The Problem

Your data is already leaking

The Trade-off: Around one in three employees paste confidential data into public AI tools, and most companies can't even see it happening. It's now one of the leading causes of data breaches.

The Threat

And the law caught up

The Trade-off: The EU AI Act carries fines up to €35M, and rules like HIPAA bar sensitive data from leaving your perimeter. Sending regulated data to a public cloud model is no longer just risky — it's legal exposure.

The Trap

Every way out is blocked

The Trade-off: You can't safely use the frontier clouds, building your own model rarely works, and the big labs won't run inside your walls. Most enterprise AI pilots end with nothing to show for it.

The Solution

AI you can actually own

The Fix: The best AI lives in the cloud; your regulated data has to stay inside. AVELIN closes that gap: our models run on your premises, under your compliance. Frontier-grade AI, and your data never leaves your walls.

> ENTERPRISE STANDARD

Security built for confidential IP work

Patent work routinely involves unpublished inventions, deal-sensitive product plans, and client secrets. Stilta is built by engineers who deployed AI in security-reviewed enterprise environments at McKinsey and QuantumBlack. We brought that same bar into Stilta from day one.

That means zero AI training on your data, per-tenant isolation by default, privileged work product never leaving your team, and your choice of US or EU data residency. Every model provider we work with is bound by separate confidentiality terms, so your unpublished inventions never enter someone else's training corpus. Standard enterprise controls - SAML SSO, audit logs, encryption at rest and in transit, regular penetration testing - are built in by default.

About Us


> OUR MISSION

Protect the ideas that move
the world forward.

In a world where products and software are replicated overnight, intellectual property is the last durable moat. Every breakthrough in AI, biotech, clean energy, and autonomous systems depends on patent protection to survive.

But the system meant to protect them hasn't kept up. Stilta exists to fix that, for the practitioners doing the work and the innovators depending on the outcome.

> OUR VALUES

How we work.

Genuine care

We care deeply about the work, each other, and the attorneys who rely on our software. That’s why we hold ourselves to high standards. Care without standards lets people down.

Relentlessly resourceful

There’s no map for what we’re building. We tackle hard problems others avoid, do work beyond the job description, and keep pushing until it works. The right people here genuinely care.

Trust over control

No micromanagement or permission culture. We hire people we trust and give them real ownership from day one. That means speaking up when you disagree and taking responsibility for better outcomes.

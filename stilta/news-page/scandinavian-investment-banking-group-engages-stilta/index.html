Stilta has engaged with a Scandinavian investment banking group to run infringement and validity analyses of a music and AI patent portfolio spanning the US and Europe.

Compared to traditional methods, Stilta's AI-driven approach offered a different path: agents that autonomously, at-scale, search for infringement evidence across public sources and map it directly to patent claim elements. Qualitative, fast, and built to surface what manual processes miss. The analysis equips the organization with evidence-backed material to position the patent portfolio's commercial relevance and market value ahead of a potential trade and securitization.

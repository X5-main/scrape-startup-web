The architecture

One revenue objective in.
Your entire sales motion, moving.

Jazon sits at the centre of your sales function. It reads every signal across pipeline and accounts, activates the right plays, and closes every loop back to revenue.

What does your function actually need?

The right question changes everything.

Most sales leaders are managing their pipeline. Jazon starts with why it isn’t moving.

You know your quota. Do you know which deals in your pipeline have the highest probability of closing and which ones are silently dying?

Jazon reads every signal across your CRM engagement patterns, deal velocity, stakeholder activity, competitive mentions and surfaces what’s real versus what’s wishful thinking in your pipeline. You stop wasting cycles on deals that won’t close.

Your reps know which accounts to call. Do they know exactly what to say and when saying it will actually matter?

Jazon handles the execution layer prospecting, outreach sequencing, follow-ups, CRM updates, proposal drafting so your reps spend their time where it counts: in conversations that move deals forward.

When your team walks into a room with a top enterprise account how prepared are they, really?

Jazon builds a live deal brief per account org chart changes, recent news, funding signals, competitive intelligence, stakeholder sentiment, and open risk factors. Your team walks in knowing more than the account expects.

If you lost three of your top reps tomorrow how much of your pipeline intelligence would walk out the door with them?

Jazon maintains a living knowledge layer across every account, every deal, every rep interaction. Institutional sales intelligence doesn’t live in anyone’s head. It lives in Jazon available to every rep, every manager, every cycle.

From objective to revenue

This is how your sales function transforms.

1
Your CRM stays. Jazon sits above it.

Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Gong, Apollo, Clay, LinkedIn Sales Navigator connect what you have. Nothing migrates. Your data stays in your environment. Your reps keep working the way they work.

Zero rip-and-replace
Jazon is not a CRM replacement. It’s the intelligence layer that makes your CRM finally earn its place in the stack.
2
State the revenue objective. Jazon builds the plan.

Not a task list. A goal. “Close $4M this quarter.” “Accelerate the top 20 enterprise deals.” “Open 50 new accounts in financial services.” Jazon reads your live pipeline data and returns a ranked execution plan by deal, by rep, by action with reasoning attached.

Revenue-first, always
If it doesn’t trace back to your revenue objective, Jazon won’t surface it. Every action on the plan earns its place.
3
The right agents activate. You don’t choose them.

From 400+ agents, Jazon calls what’s relevant to your pipeline stage prospecting, enrichment, outreach, deal nurturing, proposal drafting, competitive intelligence. Build new agents at will. Bring existing ones from any framework. All run under one control plane.

400+ agents · Activated by objective
You define the revenue target. Jazon decides which agents get to work on it.
4
Jazon executes, measures, and comes back with insights.

Outreach goes out. Deals move. Jazon tracks every outcome against your revenue objective what progressed, what stalled, what needs a different approach. It doesn’t report on activity. It reports on movement.

Every output loops back
Win/loss patterns feed every next outreach. The OS gets smarter with every deal cycle.

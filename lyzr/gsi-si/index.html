Why Partners Come to Us

The conversations that bring GSIs to Lyzr.

These are the real questions partners are sitting with when they call us. Not theory: the actual friction points in client delivery that brought firms like Accenture, KPMG, and NTT Data to the table.

We built agents on LangChain and AutoGen. How do we move them to current-gen models?
Bring your own agents. Migrate without rewrites, swap models underneath, and keep every workflow running.
We build on LangChain, CrewAI, and Claude. How do we get one registry, deployment, and observability layer?
The control plane. One central registry across every framework, with one-click deploy and full traces.
We have agents sitting across many providers. How do we bring them under one workbench?
Govern centrally. Every agent, every framework, one pane. Tool access, data access, and policy enforced in one place.
Our internal AI platform is not production-ready. How do we accelerate it for our own teams?
Plug in the missing blocks. License only what you need: simulation, orchestration, guardrails, observability.
Why is it taking so long to get agents into production? We need platform and people.
People + platform. Embedded Lyzr engineers harden and ship alongside your delivery teams.
How do we crowdsource automation ideas from business teams and build a real agentic pipeline?
Architect. Business users describe workflows in plain language and a prioritized, buildable pipeline appears.
Clients want agentic outcomes now. How do we deliver before we have a platform of our own?
Run on Lyzr from day one. Full-stack agent infrastructure, white-glove onboarding, and your brand in front.
How do we run agents on-prem or in our own VPC, with full data privacy and zero data egress?
Any cloud, any infrastructure. Deploy inside your environment or your client’s. Data never leaves.
How do business users build agents without us losing control of tool access, data, and security policy?
Governed self-serve. Guardrails, role-based access, and policy sit underneath every agent anyone builds.
How do we move from selling hours to selling a platform our firm owns?
Joint IP. Productize your domain expertise on Lyzr infrastructure. The product, and its revenue, are yours.
How do we record every decision trace so the firm keeps its intelligence when people move on?
Institutional memory. Every agent decision is logged, auditable, and owned by the organization.
There are plenty of agent builders out there. Isn’t the hard part production?
We agree. Productionization is precisely what Lyzr is built for: hardening, governance, deployment, and scale.

If any of these sound familiar, that’s exactly what the first call is for. We come prepared: no slides, no demo script, just the right people.

Why AI Projects Stall? <\/h2>\n\n\n\n

We know why businesses can\u2019t afford to let AI projects stall: the costs are too high, and the opportunities are too big. That\u2019s exactly why agentic AI has become the next frontier.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Unlike traditional automation that handles one-off tasks, agents can think through a process, make decisions, and complete workflows end-to-end. <\/p>\n\n\n\n

Whether it\u2019s resolving customer cases, qualifying leads, or publishing marketing content, agents bring speed and consistency that manual work or basic bots can\u2019t match.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

The Agent-Based AI Opportunity <\/h4>\n\n\n\n

And the market is moving fast. By 2030, agent-based AI is expected to automate tasks worth over $6 trillion globally, making it one of the biggest levers for business growth across industries like healthcare, finance, and retail.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

The market for agent-based AI solutions is expected to expand from about $5 billion in 2024 to more than $50 billion by 2028, reflecting an annual growth rate of 78%. <\/p>\n\n\n\n

This surge is fueled by rising demand for advanced automation in industries including healthcare, financial services, and retail.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

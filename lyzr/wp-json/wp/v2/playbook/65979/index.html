The Current Landscape<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

Before the advent of ChatGPT and modern AI agents, HR automation was largely rule-based, relying on pre-defined workflows and limited machine learning models. <\/p>\n\n\n\n

The automation efforts were driven by Robotic Process Automation (RPA)<\/a>, which could perform repetitive tasks but lacked intelligence and adaptability.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  1. Resume Screening:<\/strong> Basic keyword matching tools in Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) attempted to filter candidates, often missing context or nuance.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  2. Performance Management:<\/strong> Structured review cycles depended on static forms and manual tracking, often leading to delayed insights.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  3. HR Chatbots:<\/strong> Early chatbots could answer simple FAQs but struggled with context and conversational depth.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  4. Employee Engagement:<\/strong> Surveys were sent periodically, requiring HR teams to manually analyze and act upon results.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n

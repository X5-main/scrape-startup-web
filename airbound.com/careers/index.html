OUR MISSION

ENGINEER AND DEPLOY THE WORLD'S MOST EFFICIENT AUTONOMOUS DELIVERY NETWORK.

Airbound is India's most ambitious hardware company, reinventing delivery for a world that can't wait.

Founded by Naman Pushp, Airbound is built around a single conviction: that all movement belongs in the air.

Our aircraft are designed, built, and tested in India, serving real missions from rural healthcare to e-commerce.

Airbound's aircraft leverage a proprietary blended wing body tailsitter architecture, combining the efficiency of fixed wing aircraft with the flexibility of VTOL. Custom carbon fiber manufacturing, advanced control systems, and obsessive weight reduction enable unprecedented range, reliability, and scalability.

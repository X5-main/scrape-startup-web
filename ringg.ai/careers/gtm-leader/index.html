The Role

We're looking for someone to own marketing end-to-end — brand, demand gen, content, digital, and events. This is a build-from-scratch opportunity. You'll be the first dedicated marketing hire and will have the mandate (and budget) to build a small team around you.

The split is roughly 50/50 between digital marketing (SEO, SEM, content, social, thought leadership) and offline/field marketing (enterprise roundtables, CXO dinners, conferences, demo showcases). You'll work directly with the founder and the sales team to drive pipeline.

What You'll Do

Digital & Content

  • Own SEO, SEM, and paid acquisition strategy — we've onboarded 91Ninjas as consultants, and you'll work alongside them to build out the top of funnel
  • Build Ringg's brand narrative in the voice AI / conversational AI space — positioning us against players like Vapi, Bland AI, and Bolna with a clear India-first, enterprise-grade story
  • Drive content production: case studies, blog posts, whitepapers, social (especially LinkedIn), and video content that positions Ringg as a category leader
  • Own website, landing pages, and conversion optimization
  • Track and report on CAC, pipeline contribution, and marketing-sourced revenue

Events & Field Marketing

  • Plan and execute enterprise roundtables and CXO dinners targeting VP/CXO-level buyers in Fintech, E-Commerce, Insurance, and Healthcare
  • Manage Ringg's presence at industry conferences — booth strategy, demos, speaking slots, and post-event nurture
  • Run webinars and online thought leadership events featuring customers, partners, and internal experts
  • Build a quarterly events calendar aligned with sales targets and expansion into new verticals
  • Manage vendor relationships, event logistics, and budgets

Cross-Functional

  • Partner closely with Sales to ensure marketing efforts translate to qualified pipeline
  • Work with Product to translate platform releases and technical capabilities into compelling market-facing messaging
  • Build and manage a small team (1-2 hires) over the first 6-12 months

What We're Looking For

  • 4–7 years of experience in B2B marketing, ideally in SaaS, AI/ML, or enterprise tech
  • Hands-on experience running both digital campaigns and offline events — not just strategy, but execution
  • You've planned and delivered enterprise events (roundtables, dinners, conference booths) — you know the logistics, the costs, and how to measure ROI
  • Strong content instincts — you can write or direct compelling narratives for a technical product
  • Comfortable with data: you track MQLs, pipeline, CAC, and can tie marketing spend to revenue
  • Scrappy and resourceful — this is a Series A company, not a large enterprise marketing org
  • Bonus: Experience marketing to Indian enterprises, familiarity with the voice AI / contact center / CX space

Why Ringg

  • Ground floor of a category-defining company — voice AI for Indian enterprises is a massive, underpenetrated market
  • Direct access to the founder and leadership team
  • Real budget and mandate to build the function your way
  • Work with marquee logos (CRED, Flipkart, Policybazaar, Practo) from day one
  • Competitive compensation + equity

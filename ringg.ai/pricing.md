### Can I try Ringg for free?

Yes. Ringg is self-serve and does not require code to get started. Sign up, build your first agent, and use the free credits included with your account.

### How quickly can I get started?

You can create and test an agent as soon as you sign up. Start with a simple use case, then move to production as your call volume grows. You can join our discord in order to get started and ask any questions.

### How does Ringg pricing work?

Ringg's pricing is based on your usage and a monthly minimum commitment. Your total cost depends on factors such as call volume, telephony, AI usage, integrations, and any additional enterprise requirements.

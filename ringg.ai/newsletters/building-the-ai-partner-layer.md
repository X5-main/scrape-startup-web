## Ringg Lens

### Building the AI Partner Layer Anthropic has launched a $100M partner programme to expand how Claude

is adopted across enterprises. The initiative focuses on training, certifications, co-marketing, and technical support for consultancies and system integrators that help businesses implement AI. It also includes a partner portal, certification tracks, and dedicated engineering support for deployments. The structure closely follows the model used by major cloud providers, where partners play a central role in translating technology into real-world use cases.

**What Ringg thinks:**

This is a clear signal of where the AI market is heading. The bottleneck is no longer model capability, but implementation. Most companies don't struggle with access to AI, they struggle with actually using it. Partners become the bridge between promise and execution. By investing early in this layer, Anthropic is ensuring that when enterprises decide to adopt AI, Claude is already embedded in the decision-making process. In this phase, distribution, trust, and execution may matter just as much as the underlying technology.

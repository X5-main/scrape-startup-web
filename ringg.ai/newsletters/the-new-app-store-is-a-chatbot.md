## Ringg Lens

### The New App Store is a Chatbot!

It's official: OpenAl is trying to kill the browser tab. This week, the company opened submissions for the ChatGPT App Store, allowing developers to build full- featured applications that run inside the chat interface. Unlike the old plugins that just fetched text, these new apps can display interactive widgets, render charts, and execute complex workflows like ordering groceries or editing a Canva design without you ever leaving ChatGPT.

**What Ringg thinks:**

This is a calculated play to kill churn: it's easy to cancel a chatbot, but nearly impossible to cancel an operating system. By locking apps directly into the interface, OpenAl aims to become the only website you need. For founders, building inside ChatGPT offers instant access to 300 million users, but the price is invisibility. You lose the direct customer relationship. The era of "Destination Websites" is fading, now you have to decide if you want to be the platform, or a feature in someone else's.

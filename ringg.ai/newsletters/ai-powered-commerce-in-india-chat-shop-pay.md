## Ringg Lens

### AI-Powered Commerce in India: Chat, Shop, Pay

NPCI, Razorpay, and OpenAI have joined forces to introduce "Agentic Payments," a feature currently in pilot, within the ChatGPT app in India. This innovation allows users to browse, select, and pay for products entirely within a chat interface, effectively merging conversational AI with e-commerce and digital payments. The initiative leverages the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) infrastructure provided by NPCI and the payment processing capabilities of Razorpay. Bigbasket is among the first merchants to participate in this pilot, demonstrating how users can discover products, compare options, and complete transactions without leaving the chat.

**What Ringg thinks:**

This isn't just about payments, it's about reimagining the entire customer journey. Startups and brands now have a blueprint for embedding intelligence directly into experiences, reducing steps, and creating real-time, personalised interactions. The real opportunity lies in designing AI that not only facilitates transactions but actively guides and enhances the way users discover products. Conversational commerce is no longer futuristic, it's a proving ground for the next era of hyper- personalised retail.

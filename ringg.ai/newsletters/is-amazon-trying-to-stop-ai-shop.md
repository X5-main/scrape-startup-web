## Ringg Lens

### Building the World's Next AI Backbone

Salesforce bet the farm on AI agents, but now they're pumping the brakes. After cutting 4,000 roles to focus on "Agentforce," executives admitted this week that current models are getting confused, drifting off task when things get complicated. They aren't ditching AI, but they are pivoting back to hard rules and strict guardrails rather than relying on open-ended chat for critical business tasks.

**What Ringg thinks:**

This isn't a failure of the technology; it's a correction of the strategy. We spent 2024 thinking AI was a magic wand that could replace workers entirely. Salesforce just proved that LLMs are powerful engines, not autonomous drivers. The "fire everyone and let the bot run it" approach is over. The winning play now is "Augmentation" or using AI to make your best people faster, rather than trying to replace them with a slightly confused chatbot. Reliability is the new hype.

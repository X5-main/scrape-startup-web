## Overview

smallcase sees investor intent every day. Users explore products, begin onboarding, and show clear interest in subscription offerings. But in fintech, intent is fragile, a user can hesitate, abandon KYC halfway through, or decide to come back later and never return.

That was the problem smallcase wanted to solve, they did not need more top-of-funnel demand. They needed a better way to act on the demand that was already there.

![smallcase-case-study](https://images.prismic.io/ringg-ai/agMBeqYofJOwHHkb_smallcasecasestudy.png?auto=format%2Ccompress&fit=max&w=3840)

## The gap between intent and action

Across a few high value journeys, the pattern was clear. Users were showing interest in US equities, but many were not taking the final step. Others were starting KYC and dropping off before completing onboarding. And for the advisory subscription, interest existed, but many users were not understanding the value strongly enough to convert or renew.

The challenge was not getting users interested. The challenge was reaching them when intent was still live, questions were still fresh, and the next action still felt easy.

## What smallcase built with Ringg

smallcase used Ringg to create a real-time engagement layer across these journeys.

When users showed intent or dropped off at a key step, Ringg triggered fast, context-aware outreach designed to move the conversation forward.

That included:

*   Outbound calling to support US equities adoption
*   Triggered calls to recover KYC drop-offs
*   Lifecycle nudges to improve subscription conversion and renewal

The idea was simple: reach users when the decision was still in motion, not hours or days later when intent had already cooled off.

## Built to fit the workflow, not slow it down

The setup was designed to work inside smallcase’s existing systems.

Ringg was integrated through APIs within smallcase’s n8n workflows. The team deployed 20 agents across different use cases, with a multi-agent setup tailored to different journeys. They also used 20+ voices and background audio to make conversations feel more natural and less robotic.

That meant the system could scale across different user flows without becoming operationally heavy.

"What stood out for us was how effective Ringg was in the moments where user intent was already there, but follow-through was not. Whether it was someone exploring US equities, dropping off during KYC, or considering our advisory subscription, Ringg helped us engage users with the right context at the right time, and that had a clear impact on conversion."

# Siddharth Shankar Tripathi

## About

Siddharth Shankar Tripathi is the Founder and CEO of Ringg AI. He previously worked as a Product Manager at Groww, Flipkart and has entrepreneurial experience as the founder of Flywheel, which was acquired by [Withsylva.com](http://Withsylva.com).

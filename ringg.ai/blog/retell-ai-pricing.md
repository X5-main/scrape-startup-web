## What Is the Retell AI Pricing Structure?

You may see Retell AI price advertised as low as **~$0.07/min**, but most real business setups land closer to **~$0.13–$0.31/min** once dependencies are included.

**Key components that make up Retell AI pricing:**

*   **Platform fees:** Core API usage to manage call state and orchestration.
*   **LLM usage:** Per-token charges based on model selection, prompt size, and output length.
*   **Telephony fees:** Per-minute carrier costs (often via Twilio) for inbound/outbound calls.
*   **Premium voice fees:** Higher rates for more human-sounding voices (e.g., ElevenLabs).
*   **Concurrency scaling:** Extra slots or subscriptions to handle more simultaneous calls.

Retell AI doesn’t sell a “finished car”. It sells the engine, wheels, and chassis separately. The advertised rate often covers only one slice of the stack. To run a working AI agent, you’re paying for voice + LLM + telephony (and sometimes scaling) together.

## Breakdown of Retell AI Costs

To estimate your true Retell AI cost, think in three layers. Each layer has its own billing logic, and your final Retell AI pricing is the combined per-minute total.

*   **The Voice Engine**: This powers text-to-speech and largely determines how human the agent sounds. Base text-to-speech commonly ranges around **$0.07–$0.08/min**, depending on the provider.
*   Premium voices can significantly raise the Retell AI price. If you need multilingual voice agents with high-fidelity speech, this line item often becomes the largest chunk of your per-minute budget.
*   **The LLM**: Large Language Models are the “brain” that interprets the caller and generates responses. LLM costs can vary widely—roughly **$0.003–$0.50+/min** depending on the model, prompt size, and conversation length.
*   Small models keep costs low; stronger models for nuanced sales, objection handling, or complex workflows can push your Retell AI cost up quickly.
*   **Telephony**: This is the fee for the physical phone line connection, usually powered by Twilio. Connecting your agent to the public telephone network generally costs around **~$0.015 per minute**.
*   You pay while the call is connected, even during ringing, hold time, and silence. At high call volumes, these fees become a fixed cost you can’t “optimize away” with better prompts.

## Hidden Retell AI Costs You Might Miss

Beyond usage rates, several structural fees can raise Retell AI’s pricing and make monthly spend harder to predict.

*   **Concurrency fees:** Often ~20 free concurrent calls included; beyond that, you may pay about **$8/month per extra slot**, which can penalize fast scaling.
*   **Knowledge base fees:** You may get a few free knowledge bases; additional ones can be **$8/month each**, plus usage fees when the AI reads documents during calls
*   **Phone numbers and verification:** Verified/brand-safe numbers can cost up to **$100/month,** depending on verification level. This is important for avoiding “Spam Likely” and protecting answer rates.
*   **The “developer tax”:** Not on the invoice, but real. Retell is developer-first, so many teams need engineering time to build flows, integrations, and maintenance.

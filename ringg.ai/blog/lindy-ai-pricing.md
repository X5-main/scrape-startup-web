### Lindy Plus Plan

The Plus plan is the entry paid tier for users who want basic AI assistance across everyday work. It fits small teams testing emails, calendar help, Google Workspace tasks, and light workflow automation. It is not the strongest fit for advanced users running production voice agents or high-volume phone calls.

### Lindy Pro Plan

The Pro plan provides users with a larger credit pool and suits teams that use Lindy AI for daily delegation, Gmail workflows, CRM updates, qualification tasks, and sales outreach. However, teams should review phone access and call limits carefully before using this tier for high-volume voice workflows.

### Lindy Max Plan

The Max plan is designed for heavier business applications and users who want to run more of their work through Lindy. It includes a larger credit allocation and more connected inboxes, making it more useful for email management, outreach, documentation workflows, and multi-step automation.

### Lindy Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is built for teams with advanced features, security requirements, compliance needs, or bulk usage. It may suit healthcare, financial services, or HIPAA-sensitive teams that need stronger controls. However, pricing is custom, so buyers should ask for full transparency around setup, API access, invoices, and credit rules.

## How Does Lindy AI Pricing Work?

Lindy AI pricing is based on a combination of user plans and credits. The subscription gives each user a monthly credit allocation, and the team draws from a shared workspace balance. Lindy’s documentation says that credits measure the work Lindy performs, and that bigger jobs require more credits.

### How Lindy Credits Work

Credits are consumed whenever a custom agent runs a task. Under Lindy AI pricing, every task costs at least 1 credit, with most basic-model tasks using 1 to 3 credits and larger-model tasks using around 10 credits. Actual usage depends on model intelligence, task complexity, premium actions, and agent duration.

*   **Model intelligence:** More advanced models use more credits than basic models.
*   **Task complexity:** Processing hundreds of rows is more complex than updating a single field.
*   **Premium actions:** Specialized integrations and advanced features may increase credit usage.
*   **Agent duration:** Longer-running agents consume more credits until the task is complete.

### Why Credit Usage Changes by Workflow

Credit usage changes because every workflow is different. A simple chatbot response or email summary may use fewer credits than a multi-step workflow that checks a knowledge base, updates Salesforce, sends emails, calls a prospect, and logs the result through Zapier or an API connection.

### What Happens When You Hit Usage Limits?

When credits run out, Lindy pauses credit-using actions and puts new tasks on hold until the next cycle or until admins top up credits. Lindy also states that plan credits do not roll over, while purchased top-up credits are treated differently.

## Hidden Costs You Might Miss in Lindy AI Pricing

Here are the hidden variable costs that can significantly inflate Lindy AI pricing once a team moves from testing to production.

*   **Extra Credit Usage:** Credit usage can rise when workflows include deeper research, long-running agents, complex qualification, large context windows, or advanced model selection. This makes budgeting harder for teams with changing customer needs or seasonal urgency.
*   **Voice Calling Costs:** Lindy Phone supports inbound and outbound phone calls, but standard US configuration is listed at 20 credits per minute, approximately $0.19 per minute. That can matter for high-volume outreach and customer service operations.
*   **Phone Number Costs:** Lindy’s phone documentation states that each phone number costs $10 per month, supports concurrent calls, and is available across 100+ countries. Teams running regional outreach may need multiple phone numbers.
*   **Connected Inbox Limits:** Connected inbox limits can become a scaling constraint for sales and support teams, as well as small businesses managing multiple brands. A team may need a higher tier when more Gmail or shared inbox connections are required.
*   **Implementation and Workflow Maintenance:** Building useful workflows still requires setup, testing, customization, and adherence to best practices. Teams need clear prompts, clean documentation, reliable CRM data, and ongoing review to maintain consistency across specific use cases.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Lindy AI safe and secure?

Lindy provides standard encryption, but for sensitive industries like healthcare requiring HIPAA compliance, you must upgrade to the Enterprise tier. Always review their documentation on data handling before connecting it to confidential business applications or client data.

### Is Lindy AI better than other AI assistants?

Unlike basic voice bots, Lindy can execute actions. For specialized sales outreach, dedicated voice agents such as Ringg AI often outperform Lindy’s generalist capabilities in consistency and speed.

### Does Lindy AI charge per task or per usage?

Lindy AI charges on a usage basis via credits. Basic tasks like email management cost a few credits, while complex actions like phone calls or processing extensive documentation consume significantly more credits, making it a variable-cost model.

### What Is the Best Lindy AI Alternative for Voice Automation?

Ringg AI is a strong Lindy AI alternative for voice automation because it is built around calls rather than general admin tasks. It supports voice agents, sales outreach, customer service, lead qualification, call analytics, and predictable per-minute pricing for teams that need operational voice workflows.

### What Happens When Lindy AI Usage Runs Out?

When Lindy usage runs out, credit-using actions pause, and new tasks are put on hold until credits reset or admins top up. Lindy documentation says there is no surprise bill, but paused automation can still affect customer inquiries, follow-ups, and time-sensitive sales outreach.

### Is Ringg AI Cheaper Than Lindy AI for Voice Calls?

Ringg AI can be more predictable for voice calls because it uses a dedicated voice pricing model instead of a credit system. Lindy AI price can look simple at the plan level, but actual voice costs depend on credits, phone numbers, call duration, and workflow complexity.

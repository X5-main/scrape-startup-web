## What Is an AI Call Answering Service?

_**An AI call answering service is a system that answers incoming calls using [conversational AI](https://www.ringg.ai/blog/conversational-ai-for-customer-support) that can talk to the caller like a real person. In addition to this, an AI voice answering service also resolves the caller’s queries, screens them for things like interviews, sales leads, KYC, and more. This frees up the human team so they can handle more nuanced cases.**_

Think of it this way

You run a business. Someone is interested in your service or product and calls at 11 PM to ask about your pricing or book an appointment. There's no one on the other end. So they hang up. You lose that opportunity.

An AI call answering service solves this problem.

It listens. It understands what the caller needs and talks to them naturally. Depending on how it’s set up, it can:

*   Answer common FAQs
*   Qualify leads by asking the right questions
*   Book appointments directly into your calendar
*   Route calls to the right person or team
*   Log everything into your CRM automatically

Here's a simple (and practical) example. Let’s say you run a dental clinic. A patient calls on a Sunday evening to book an appointment. Instead of reaching voicemail, they speak to an [AI voice agent](https://www.ringg.ai/) that asks for their name, what they need, and books the appointment instantly.

No human intervention is needed. The patient's happy. And you wake up on Monday with a full schedule.

That's exactly what a good AI call answering service does. And it’s not a gimmick. It acts as your front desk, running 24/7 without the overhead.

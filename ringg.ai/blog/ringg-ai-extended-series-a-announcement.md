Today, we are announcing the close of Ringg AI’s **$15 million Series A**, led by Peak XV Partners, with participation from Arkam Ventures and Capital 2B.

When we started Ringg in 2023, we believed voice would become the most natural interface for getting work done.

That belief has only grown stronger. But we have also learned that a conversation, however natural, is only the beginning.It is becoming easy to build an agent that can talk. It remains incredibly hard to build one that works reliably at enterprise scale and actually finishes the job.

Ringg exists to solve that harder problem.

## The problem we are obsessed with

Most customer journeys do not live inside a single conversation or channel. A customer might begin with a phone call, continue over WhatsApp, complete a verification in the browser, and eventually need help from a human. Behind that journey sit business rules, approvals, CRMs, support systems, payment tools, and other enterprise applications.

Most AI systems treat these as separate interactions.

The conversation happens in one place. Context lives somewhere else. Actions are completed in another system. When the channel changes or a human steps in, much of the history is lost.

The result is an agent that can sound intelligent but cannot reliably move the work forward.

The winning agentic experience must own the entire journey from intent to execution. It must understand what the customer wants, navigate the relevant enterprise workflow, take action across systems, and preserve context as the interaction moves between channels or people.

That is what we are building at Ringg.

## Built for the hardest version of the problem

When we began building Ringg, much of the infrastructure we needed did not exist, so we went exploring.

We worked through how interruptions should behave in a real conversation. We reviewed batches of calls by hand because it was often the fastest way to understand where the system failed. We built orchestration that selects the right model for each task. We created harnesses that make probabilistic systems more dependable. Then we connected conversations to the tools and workflows required to complete the job.

Enterprise environments expose every weakness in an AI agent.

Calls are noisy. Customers switch languages midway through a sentence. WhatsApp conversations unfold asynchronously. Browser workflows span multiple applications. Latency requirements are strict. Data residency and compliance cannot be afterthoughts.

We chose to solve this version of the problem first.

"If an agent can handle a noisy call, switch across languages, and still complete a real workflow, it can work almost anywhere. We are building this in India for a global market.

Ringg supports in-region deployments across India, the GCC, the US, and Europe, as well as on-premise deployments for enterprises that require greater control over compliance and data residency."

## Shared context for every agent and every human

When Ringg is deployed inside an enterprise, it builds a context graph across customers, conversations, business rules, workflows, and systems.

This context graph becomes shared memory for Ringg’s voice, WhatsApp, chat, and browser agents. It also gives any human who steps in the information required to continue the work.

Instead of treating every interaction as a new ticket, the system understands what has already happened, what actions have been taken, and what needs to happen next.

The result is a customer journey that can move across channels without losing continuity.

Ringg also treats deployment as a continuous improvement cycle, not a one-time implementation.

Built-in evaluations measure conversation quality, policy adherence, workflow completion, and business outcomes. Enterprise teams can monitor agents, review decisions, intervene, or take over a workflow without losing context.

Every conversation becomes an opportunity to make the system better.

## Where Ringg is already working

Ringg’s agents are already in production at enterprises including [Policybazaar](https://www.ringg.ai/case-studies/policybazaar), CRED, Flipkart, [PharmEasy](https://www.ringg.ai/case-studies/pharmeasy), Groww, [Practo](https://www.ringg.ai/case-studies/practo), Tabby, SuperNova, and GoodScore.

We serve customers across marketplaces, healthcare, banking, fintech, and IT, where reliability and measurable outcomes matter far more than how impressive a demo sounds.

Our internal standard remains simple: if an agent cannot work reliably in production, it does not count.

## What is next

We believe the next generation of customer experience will not be divided into separate calls, chats, apps, and tickets.

It will be one continuous journey. The agent will understand the customer’s intent, preserve context across channels, navigate the relevant business systems, and carry the work through to a measurable outcome. When a human steps in, they will inherit the full context instead of starting again.

Ringg is the platform for building these experiences. This capital gives us the fuel to build a world-class product that understands your customers and gets their jobs done.”

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much has Ringg AI raised in its Series A?

Ringg AI has closed a $15 million Series A. The latest extension was led by Peak XV Partners, with participation from Arkam Ventures and Capital 2B.

### What will Ringg AI use the funding for?

Ringg will use the capital to strengthen its enterprise AI platform, expand its voice, WhatsApp, and browser agents, invest further in proprietary AI models and its context graph, and accelerate enterprise growth across India and international markets.

### What does Ringg AI build?

Ringg builds enterprise AI agents that manage customer conversations and complete the business workflows that follow. Its platform connects agents across voice, WhatsApp, chat, the browser, and enterprise systems while preserving shared context.

### How is Ringg different from a voice AI or chatbot platform?

Ringg is built around completed outcomes rather than standalone conversations. Its agents can share context across channels, take actions inside enterprise applications, hand work to humans when required, and continuously improve through built-in evaluations.

### Which enterprises use Ringg AI?

Ringg’s agents are in production at enterprises including Policybazaar, DCB Bank, CRED, Flipkart, Groww, Practo, Tabby, SuperNova, and GoodScore.

### Does Ringg support regional or on-premise deployments?

Yes. Ringg supports in-region deployments across India, the GCC, the US, and Europe, as well as on-premise deployments for enterprises with specific compliance, control, or data-residency requirements.

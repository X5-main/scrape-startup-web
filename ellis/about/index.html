Our mission

Make institutional-grade operations available to every private credit manager.

Private credit scaled. Its infrastructure did not. Many managers still run critical workflows across spreadsheets, disconnected systems and manual processes. Ellis turns that fragmentation into one reconciled, source-verifiable book—and puts purpose-built agents to work across the workflows that slow firms down.

Larger vision

Private credit is where we begin.

Capital is the lifeblood of the economy. Our larger mission is to help it move with greater clarity and confidence to the businesses and entrepreneurs building what comes next.

This Privacy Notice explains how Portanova Inc. (dba “Ellis,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal data in connection with any specific product, service, or application that references or links to this Privacy Notice (our “Services”) and our websites (our “Websites”).

This Privacy Notice does not address our privacy practices relating to Ellis employees, contractors, and other individuals in an employment relationship with us, nor data that is not subject to applicable data protection laws (such as deidentified or publicly available information in certain jurisdictions). This Privacy Notice is also not a contract and does not create any legal rights or obligations not otherwise provided by law.

01

Our Role in Processing Personal Data

Data protection laws sometimes differentiate between “controllers” and “processors” of personal data. A “controller” determines the purposes and means (the why and how) of processing personal data. A “processor,” which is sometimes referred to as a “service provider,” processes personal data on behalf of a controller subject to the controller’s instructions.

This Privacy Notice describes our privacy practices where we are acting as the controller of personal data. However, this Privacy Notice does not cover or address how our customers may process personal data when they use our Services, or how we may process personal data on their behalf in accordance with their instructions where we are acting as their processor. As a result, we recommend referring to the privacy notice of the customer with which you have a relationship for information on how they engage processors, like us, to process personal data on their behalf. In addition, we are generally not permitted to respond to individual requests relating to personal data we process on behalf of our customers, so we recommend directing any requests to the relevant customer.

Customer Content
“Customer Content” means the data, files, and records that a customer or its authorized users submit to, upload to, or connect to the Services, or that we retrieve from a customer’s systems or third-party sources at the customer’s direction — for example, loan and portfolio data, fund administrator and general ledger extracts, borrower and obligor files, investor and limited partner records, supporting documents, and the reports, reconciliations, and other outputs we generate from them. Customer Content may contain personal data. We process Customer Content as a processor (or service provider) on behalf of the relevant customer, in accordance with our agreement with that customer, and not for our own independent purposes. Where this Privacy Notice describes rights or choices, those rights and choices apply to personal data we process as a controller; for personal data contained in Customer Content, see “Personal Data We Process for Our Customers” below.
02

Our Collection and Use of Personal Data

The categories of personal data we collect depend on how you interact with us and our Services. In the course of doing business, providing the Services, and operating our Websites, we collect personal data in different ways and from different sources, as described below. Information we collect from one source may be combined with information we collect from another.

Information you provide directly

Account Information
When you or your employer creates an account with us, and throughout the time you or your employer uses the Services, we collect information associated with your account, including your name, business email address, job title and role, employer, telephone number, language and notification preferences, account credentials and multi-factor authentication details, billing and payment contact information, and your transaction history with us (collectively, “Account Information”).
Communication Information
If you communicate with us, for example when you contact us about the Services, complete a survey, attend an event we host or sponsor, participate in a design partner or pilot program, or request support, we collect your name, business contact details, information about your role and firm, survey and feedback responses, how you interact with our communications, and the contents of any messages you send us (collectively, “Communication Information”).
Meeting and Call Information
With notice and, where required, your consent, we may record or transcribe onboarding sessions, implementation working sessions, support calls, and product feedback calls in order to produce notes, capture requirements, and improve the Services. Recordings and transcripts, and the notes derived from them, are “Meeting Information.”
Social Media Information
We maintain pages on services such as LinkedIn and X. When you interact with our pages, or with our personnel through those services, we collect the personal data you choose to provide, such as contact details and the contents of your messages, posts, or profile. Third parties may also provide us with aggregate analytics about activity on those pages (collectively, “Social Media Information”).
Applicant Information
If you apply for a role at Ellis, we collect your resume or CV, contact details, work history, education, portfolio or code samples, interview notes and scorecards, screening assessments, references, and, where you choose to provide it, information about your work authorization (collectively, “Applicant Information”). We use Applicant Information to evaluate your application, communicate with you, and administer our hiring process.
Demo and Inquiry Information
When you request a demo or otherwise submit a form on our Websites, we collect the information you enter — such as your name, business email address, firm, and role — together with the page you submitted it from, the website that referred you, and any campaign parameters contained in the link you followed.

Information we collect automatically

Log Data
Information your browser or device sends automatically when you use the Services or visit our Websites, including your IP address, browser type and settings, the date and time of your request, referring page, and how you interact with pages and features (collectively, “Log Data”).
Usage Data
When you interact with the Services, metadata is generated that provides context about your use of them. This includes your account identifier and email address, how often you sign in, which features and modules you use, time spent in the Services, the volume and type of operations you run, and how those operations performed (collectively, “Usage Data”). Usage Data does not include Customer Content.
Cookies and Similar Technologies
Our Websites do not use advertising cookies, pixels, tags, or cross-site tracking technologies, and we do not permit advertising networks to collect information through them.
We do use a privacy-preserving analytics service, provided to us by our hosting provider, to understand how our Websites are used. It records page views and the actions people take on a page — for example, which page was viewed, which website referred a visitor to us, any campaign parameters in the link that was followed, the general category of device and browser used, the country the request came from, and whether a visitor clicked a call to action, began filling in a form, or submitted one. This service does not set cookies, does not create a persistent identifier for you, and does not follow you across other websites. To estimate how many distinct people visited on a given day, it derives a non-reversible value from your IP address and browser type; your IP address is not retained. The information recorded about clicks and form interactions describes the page and the action, not you: it does not include your name, email address, employer, or anything else you typed into a form. Our provider processes this information on our behalf as our service provider, under a contract that prohibits it from using the information for its own purposes, and we do not use it for advertising.
Your browser may also store a small amount of data locally to remember interface preferences — for example, that you dismissed a notice — and that data remains on your device. Where we present a security challenge before accepting a form submission, that challenge is provided by our bot-mitigation vendor and is strictly necessary to prevent automated abuse. The authenticated Services use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in, maintain session integrity, and enforce access controls. If we begin using advertising technologies or non-essential cookies, we will update this Privacy Notice and, where required by applicable law, obtain your consent first.
Device Information
Device and connection information collected when you access the Services, including device type and name, operating system, device identifiers, and browser (collectively, “Device Information”).

For information about the choices you may have in relation to our use of automatic data collection technologies, please refer to the “Your Privacy Choices” section below.

Information we collect from third parties

We also receive information about you from:

  • security and infrastructure partners, who may provide information such as compromised credential alerts and threat intelligence, to protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats;
  • sales and marketing data providers, who provide business contact details such as name, business email address, telephone number, employer, job title, and information about professional affiliations, for the purpose of identifying and contacting potential customers;
  • event organizers, industry associations, and market research firms (for example, private credit and SBIC industry conferences we attend or sponsor), who may provide business contact details and information about your firm and role;
  • your employer or the organization you represent, where you interact with us in connection with your employment — for example, we may receive your contact information from your employer so that we can communicate with you about your employer’s customer relationship with us; and

(Collectively, “Information Collected From Third Parties.”)

Publicly available information

We collect publicly available business information, including information published on professional networking sites, firm websites, and public regulatory filings and registers, such as Form ADV filings, SBA licensee lists, and fund registration records, about our customers and prospective customers, in order to identify, offer, and provide our Services (“Publicly Available Information”).

Retention of Personal Data

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. No single retention period applies to all personal data. In determining how long to retain personal data, we consider:

  • how long we need the data to provide the Services and operate our business, including establishing, maintaining, and administering accounts;
  • whether we have an ongoing relationship with you or with the organization you represent;
  • the nature, volume, and sensitivity of the data, and the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure;
  • whether we are subject to a legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, audit, or contractual obligation to retain the data, including obligations we owe to our customers;
  • whether retention is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, to resolve disputes, to enforce our agreements, or to comply with a legal hold; and
  • whether we can achieve our purposes by aggregating or deidentifying the data rather than retaining it in identifiable form.

Personal data contained in Customer Content is retained, returned, and deleted in accordance with our agreement with the relevant customer.

When personal data is no longer needed for the purposes described above, we delete it or deidentify it.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the rights described below with respect to personal data we process as a controller. These rights are not absolute. In some cases we may decline a request, in whole or in part, as permitted by applicable law — for example, where granting it would adversely affect the rights and freedoms of another person, or where we are required or permitted to retain the data.

Right to know and access
You may ask us to confirm whether we process personal data about you and to provide you access to that data, together with information about the categories of personal data we have collected, the categories of sources, our purposes for collecting and disclosing it, and the categories of third parties to which we have disclosed it.
Right to a copy (portability)
You may ask us for a copy of the personal data you provided to us, in a portable and, where technically feasible, readily usable format.
Right to correct
You may ask us to correct inaccurate personal data we maintain about you, taking into account the nature of the data and the purposes of processing.
Right to delete
You may ask us to delete personal data we maintain about you.
Right to opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising
We do not sell personal data, and we do not share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. Because we do not engage in these activities, there is nothing for you to opt out of. If this changes, we will update this Privacy Notice and provide the opt-out mechanisms applicable law requires.
Sensitive personal data
The Account Information we collect includes account credentials and multi-factor authentication details, which some laws treat as sensitive personal data. We use and disclose this data only as necessary to authenticate you, secure your account, provide the Services you have requested, and for other purposes permitted by applicable law, and not to infer characteristics about you.
Automated decision-making and profiling
We do not use personal data to engage in profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.
Right to withdraw consent
Where we rely on your consent to process personal data, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
Right to non-discrimination
We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights, including by denying you Services, charging you a different price, or providing you a different level or quality of Service.
Right to appeal
If we decline to take action on your request, you may appeal our decision by contacting us at privacy@ellis.ai with the subject line “Privacy Rights Appeal.” We will inform you in writing of the outcome of the appeal and the reasons for it, and, where applicable, of your ability to contact your state attorney general to submit a complaint.
How to exercise your rights
Submit a request by emailing privacy@ellis.ai. To protect your personal data, we will take reasonable steps to verify your identity before acting on your request, which may include asking you to confirm information already associated with your account or your relationship with us. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law — generally within 45 days, which we may extend where permitted, in which case we will let you know. We do not charge a fee to exercise these rights, except where applicable law permits us to do so for repetitive or excessive requests.
Authorized agents
You may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. We may ask the agent for proof that you gave them signed permission to submit the request, and we may ask you to verify your own identity directly with us or to confirm that you gave the agent permission.
Personal Data We Process for Our Customers
If your personal data is contained in Customer Content, we process it as a processor on behalf of our customer, and that customer — not Ellis — is responsible for responding to your requests. Please direct your request to the relevant customer. If you contact us instead, we will, where we are able to identify the relevant customer, refer your request to them and assist them in responding as required by our agreement and applicable law.

How People Use Pangram for Multilingual AI Detection

Multilingual AI text detection provides an additional tool for those reviewing content in their non-native language.

For example, English as a Second Language (ESL) and foreign language teachers can check for AI plagiarism across student content.

This also supports fairness for students - with non-native speakers more likely to be unfairly flagged by less accurate AI detectors because of their phrasing. Not with Pangram.

Our tool is similarly used by those in publishing, marketing, government, and more, to verify the originality of content.

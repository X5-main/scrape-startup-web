What do I get with Pangram's Individual plan?

What's included with Pangram's Individual plan.

Last reviewed Jul 24, 2026

The Individual plan is for regular personal use — much higher limits than Free, plus plagiarism detection and automatic feed scanning.

Pricing

  • $20 per month, or $15 per month billed annually ($180 per year — save $60).
  • Includes a 7-day free trial.

What's included

  • 3,000 credits per month — scan up to 300,000 words monthly.
  • 100 image detection scans per month.
  • Everything in the Free plan.
  • Plagiarism detection.
  • Automatically scan your entire feed on social media, Substack, and more.

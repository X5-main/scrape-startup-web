Pangram Labs Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2025

Our Commitment to Data Privacy

Pangram prioritizes privacy and security, and strives to protect all data submitted to our services.

Your Information Stays with You

Your submissions to Pangram are not retained, used, nor disclosed for any purpose outside of our contractual obligations to our Customers.

Your Information Stays Out of our Models

Your submissions to Pangram are not used to train our AI or machine learning systems, ensuring better confidentiality. See how it works!

Your Information Stays Yours

Your submissions to Pangram do not grant us ownership. We neither sell nor share (e.g. for cross-context behavioral advertising) the Personal Information of any User.

Your Information is Used for You

Your submissions are processed only for the specific purposes outlined in our contract with the Customer.

Overview

Pangram Labs, Inc. ("Pangram," "Pangram Labs," "we," "us"), operates web-based tools at Pangram.com to identify AI content, helping educators, content moderators, and publishers preserve integrity (the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect Personal Information submitted to, or collected by our Services. Personal Information is any information that identifies, describes, or relates to a specific individual or household, or can reasonably be linked to them, directly or indirectly ("Information").

The Policy describes your choices related to your data rights (e.g. data use, access, and changes) and applies to all our customers ("Customers," "you," "your"), such as individual subscribers, organizations, or educational institutions, and their Users ("Users"), such as employees, teachers, or students.

Pangram complies with all applicable and relevant laws, and is committed to being transparent about our data practices.

Beyond this Privacy Policy, your use of our Services is also subject to our Terms of Service.

By using our Services, you agree to the collection, use, disclosure, and procedures this Privacy Policy describes.

Personal Data that We Collect

When you or your Users use our Services, engage with us, interact with our website, or submit information to us in any other way, we may collect Personal Information.

Account Registration Data

When an account is registered to you or your Users, information such as name, email address, password, and place of employment may be collected.

Content Submissions Data

For our Free Service: Text content submitted for AI detection is collected.
For Registered Customers: Submissions and associated metadata (e.g., submission date, user ID) are collected.

Communications

If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information about you, such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of a message or attachments that you may send to us, and other information you choose to provide. If you subscribe to our newsletter, then we will collect certain information from you, such as your email address.

Careers

If you decide that you wish to apply for a job with us, you may submit your contact information and your resume online. We will collect the information you choose to provide on your resume, such as your education and employment experience.

Payment Data

When paying for our Services, transaction history and billing information processed by third-party integration with Stripe may be collected. We do not store complete payment card details.

Automatically Collected Data

Location Information. When you use our Services, we infer your general location information, for example by using your internet protocol (IP) address.
Device Information. We receive information about the device and software you use to access our Services, including IP address, web browser type, and operating system version.
Usage Information. We automatically receive information about your interactions with our Services, like the pages or other content you view, purchases you make, and the dates and times of your visits.
Information from Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies. We and our third-party partners collect information using cookies, pixel tags, SDKs, or other tracking technologies to collect information about your activities on our Services.

How Personal Data is Used and Disclosed by Pangram

Pangram does not sell, rent, or unnecessarily store and/or process the data we collect. We use your data for the following purposes:

What We DO Use Your Data For:

Service Provision: To provide services to our Customers and Users such as our AI detection tool and our browser plugin, and facilitating institutional review of works submitted to Customer accounts by their Users.

Customer Support: To provide Customers with support, when requested. Pangram may access user submission data to diagnose and fix reported issues with the services provided.

Security and Maintenance: To troubleshoot, debug, and maintain the security and performance of our Services and website.

Business Transfers: We may share or transfer Personal Information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business to another company.

Legal Compliance: When we are compelled to by legal or regulatory obligations, we will share personal information to respond to any applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

Your data IS NOT used for any of the following purposes:

  • It is NOT used to train, develop, refine, or improve AI models or any machine learning or AI systems - we use outside datasets to train our system.
  • It is NOT used to build new products for future use, or to expand the capabilities of our existing products.
  • It is NOT used for marketing, including advertising to you and providing your data to advertisers.
  • It is NOT used to provide services to customers outside of your Customer account.

How Long Personal Data is Retained by Pangram

Our data retention policy is designed to prioritize data privacy and intellectual property rights. We retain data only as long as necessary to serve you (our Customers and their Users). Pangram does NOT claim an indefinite, perpetual royalty.

Content submitted to registered accounts is securely deleted within thirty (30) days of the Customer account being closed, or as defined in the terms of the Customer agreement.

Your Choices

Inputs. You can delete your queries from your history. If you do, we will delete the details of the query submitted, but we will still record that you made a query for billing and analytics purposes.
Marketing Communications. You can unsubscribe from our promotional emails via the link provided in the emails. Even if you opt out of receiving promotional messages from us, you will continue to receive administrative messages from us.
Do Not Track. There is no accepted standard on how to respond to Do Not Track signals, and we do not respond to such signals.

Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for the length of time needed to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. You may request for your data to be deleted by emailing privacy@pangram.com

Third-Party Services

To operate our Services, Pangram Labs engages trusted third-party vendors who provide infrastructure hosting, analytics, authentication, payment processing, customer support, and other services. These providers are contractually bound to use personal data only for the purpose of delivering services on our behalf, and only under strict confidentiality and security obligations.

We perform diligence and monitoring to ensure these partners meet our security and privacy standards as well as all legal requirements.

For example, we use industry-leading services like Amazon Web Services for secure data hosting and Stripe for payment processing.

Security

We make reasonable efforts to protect your information by using physical and electronic safeguards designed to improve the security of the information we maintain. However, because no electronic transmission or storage of information can be entirely secure, we can make no guarantees as to the security or privacy of your information.

Children's Online Privacy and Protection Act (COPPA) & Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)

COPPA Compliance

Where our Services are utilized with students under the age of thirteen, or otherwise below the applicable age of consent, we require Customers to obtain necessary verifiable parental or guardian consent, and we commit to promptly deleting any inadvertently collected Personal Information obtained without such consent.

FERPA Compliance

For educational institutions governed by FERPA, we act as a "School Official" with a legitimate educational interest, strictly managing and using student Personal Information and educational records as directed by the Customer agreement and applicable law.

We follow the terms set in FERPA §§ 99.31(a)(1).

Parent/Guardian Rights: Parents or guardians should contact the respective Customer directly to request review or deletion of their child's Personal Information, and we will collaborate with Customers to facilitate these requests.

International Visitors

Our Services are hosted in the United States and intended for visitors located within the United States. If you choose to use the Services from the European Union or other regions of the world with laws governing data collection and use that may differ from U.S. law, then please note that you are transferring your personal information outside of those regions to the U.S. for storage and processing.

We may also transfer your data from the U.S. to other countries or regions in connection with storage and processing of data, fulfilling your requests, and operating the Services.

By providing any information, including personal information, on or to the Services, you consent to such transfer, storage, and processing.

How to Correct your Personal Data

Customers have the right to have control over the personal data they own or steward. Customers may request to correct, access, or delete their data by emailing privacy@pangram.com.

Users that are part of a Customer account may contact their organization to exercise these rights, and we will work with that organization to satisfy their requests.

Customers may also request deletion of any of their User's personal data.

As a service provider, Pangram does not sell or share Personal Information, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and similar state laws.

We assist our Customers in fulfilling consumer rights including requests to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of Personal Information by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures as directed by our Customers.

Our commitment:

Contact:

For questions about our role as a service provider, contact us at privacy@pangram.com. For exercising your consumer rights regarding Personal Information we process, please contact the Customer/institution that collected your information.

When processing education records, or Information relating to students, we operate in accordance with our Customers' instructions and applicable educational privacy requirements.

Text Messaging (SMS / RCS)

Pangram operates a text messaging service at +1 (989) 726-4726 (delivered via SMS and, where supported, RCS) that allows users to submit text or article URLs for AI-content detection and receive a reply with the result. See /sms for details.

When you text our messaging service, we collect your mobile phone number and the contents of your message (including any URL you send). We use this information solely to (a) process the request through our AI-content detection model, (b) send a one-time reply with the result, and (c) honor STOP, START, and HELP keyword requests as required by law and by mobile carrier guidelines.

Pangram does not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or message content with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information collected through our messaging service is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We may share information with subprocessors only to the extent necessary to deliver the reply (for example, our messaging provider, Twilio) or to operate the AI-detection service itself.

You may opt out of text message communications at any time by replying STOP to any message from Pangram. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

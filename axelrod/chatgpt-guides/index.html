FAQ

Connecting ChatGPT to your PMS

What does connecting ChatGPT to my PMS let me do?

Ask your booking system plain-English questions about today's arrivals, missing guest details, calendar gaps, and occupancy, and get answers from your live data without opening another dashboard.

Is it safe?

Yes, when you keep it read-only. Your PMS credentials stay in a private connection you control, not inside ChatGPT, and the setup only reads data; it cannot create, change, or cancel reservations.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You create a read-only API credential in your PMS and connect it; there is no coding. Most operators finish in about an hour, though some systems need API access approved first.

What if my PMS is not listed?

We cover 18 systems so far, including Smoobu, Hostaway, Guesty, Lodgify, and more. If yours is not here, tell us and we will add it.

How is this different from Axelrod itself?

The guides give you a do-it-yourself, read-only connection. Axelrod is the managed operating layer that handles the daily work for you across your tools.

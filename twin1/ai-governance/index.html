Your Data Will Never Train an AI Model

This is a contractual commitment, not a policy statement. Customer data is never used to train underlying LLMs without your explicit written consent. Your firm's data remains strictly private and unique to your deployment.

Tenant Isolation
Each tenant has a dedicated, encrypted storage bucket. No data is ever co-mingled at the storage layer.
Index Separation
Each tenant’s data is stored in separate indices within the search service.
Right to Delete
Data deleted by a user is deleted by the Twin. On termination or expiry of a contract, Twin1 deletes all customer data.

Data Residency

Data is hosted with cloud providers in a jurisdiction agreed with each customer. Current available regions include the USA, EU, and Singapore.

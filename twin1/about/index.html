About Twin1 AI

We are solving the siloed knowledge problem for businesses by building the coordination and trust layer.

The most valuable knowledge in professional organizations is massively distributed and trapped in email inboxes, call transcripts, and individuals' heads rather than centralized systems.

Meet our founders

Founder & CEO

Dr. Lewis Z. Liu is a repeat founder who previously founded and sold Eigen Technologies, a pioneering AI company that digitized financial and legal documents. Eigen processed over $100 trillion worth of financial contracts for half of the world's largest banks and was the first AI system to receive regulatory approval from the Federal Reserve and FDIC to process contracts without human intervention. At Eigen, he also pioneered small-data AI and deployed one of the world's first production RAG systems in 2020. Lewis began his career at McKinsey & Company. He holds a PhD in Physics from Oxford, where he invented a new class of X-ray lasers; a Master's in Physics from Harvard; and Harvard's first joint Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts and Physics. Lewis is one of the world's preeminent experts in AI governance and continues today to advise governments, central banks, and corporate boards on the safe deployment of AI.

Co-Founder & CTO

Huiting Liu is a machine learning engineer who has built production ML systems at global scale for over a decade. Most recently he led the core AI/ML team at Moloco, where his team built the real-time ad targeting models behind the company's monetization. Before that, as a Senior ML Engineer at Apple, he was central to transitioning Siri's natural language understanding from statistical models to deep learning across 35+ countries and 22 languages. Huiting was also a founding engineer at Eigen Technologies, where he built out the core text extraction ML system. Prior to Eigen, Huiting was a founding Engineer at Baidu's Zuoyebang, which spun out to reach a $10bn valuation. He holds a Master's in Computational Data Science from Carnegie Mellon and a BS in Computer Science from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.

Co-Founder & CPO

Mission & Values

Our mission and values

Twin1 is building the privacy-first coordination layer for businesses, transforming fragmented knowledge into a governed, queryable intelligence network

A secure AI version of you that understands your context, scales your knowledge, and personalizes AI output.

What we build

Twin1 creates personalized digital AI twins for professionals. A secure AI version of you to amplify your expertise and securely tap into the collective knowledge of your organization.

Who we serve

Law firms, financial services firms, regulated enterprises, and AI native teams

Our values

AI should not be done to humans, it should work for humans

Organizations should have control over their AI destiny and their data

AI should not flatten knowledge, it should amplify individual human expertise

AI needs to preserve individual privacy and dignity

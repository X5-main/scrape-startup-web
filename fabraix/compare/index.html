Where Nyx fits

A managed, autonomous adversary - not a tool you operate.

Nyx is an automated red-teaming AI agent, but three things set it apart in that category: it reaches surfaces most tools can't, it self-evolves into a better attacker against your system every time it runs, and its arsenal is private and always growing.

Pure blackbox

No integration, no source, no weights, no network access. Nyx reaches your agent the way your users do - an endpoint is enough.

Multi-surface

HTTP, browser, voice, and email - not just the prompt and API. It plants adversarial pages and drives voice agents on the fly.

Managed audit

We run it and hand you an AIVSS-scored report with reproduction and remediation - not one more dashboard to triage.

Private arsenal

A lab arsenal fed by live disclosures and a public break-the-agent challenge - not a catalog an attacker can read too.

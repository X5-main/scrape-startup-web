Job Overview<\/strong><\/p>\n

Artemis ingests and analyzes exabytes of streaming data from cloud platforms, identity providers, network logs, and more. We\u2019re building a new, large-scale data platform from the ground up – and we\u2019re looking for exceptional Software Engineers<\/strong> to help shape and build it. You\u2019ll work alongside engineers who architected some of the world\u2019s biggest distributed systems.<\/p>\n

This is a high-impact role with significant ownership. You\u2019ll work across critical parts of the system – from customer-facing experiences to large-scale data and analytics infrastructure – depending on your strengths and the problems at hand.<\/p>\n

You will help define architecture, make key technical decisions, and drive projects from concept through production. This role is for engineers who are comfortable operating in ambiguity, care deeply about quality, and want to build systems that matter.<\/p>\n

    Make architectural decisions – <\/strong>Evaluate trade-offs, influence technical direction, and help ensure the system remains scalable, secure, and maintainable as we grow.<\/p>\n<\/li>\n

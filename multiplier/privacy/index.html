Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

WithAI Research, Inc. (“Multiplier,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the public website at multiplier.ai. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit the website, contact us, or follow a link to book a consultation. Separate customer or product agreements may include additional data terms.

1. Information we handle

Information you provide. If you email us or book a consultation, we may receive information such as your name, contact details, company, and anything you include in your message or booking request.

Website usage. We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate use of the website. The service may process information such as page views, referring pages, approximate location, and browser or device information. Vercel describes Web Analytics as cookieless and anonymized.

Local preferences.The website stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. This preference stays on your device unless you clear it.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to questions and arrange consultations.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • To understand aggregate website traffic and content performance.
  • To comply with law and protect our rights and users.

3. Third-party services and links

Vercel hosts the website and provides analytics. Its handling of information is described in the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Consultation links take you to Cal.com. Information you enter there is submitted directly to Cal.com, and we may receive the resulting booking details. Cal.com handles that information under its own Privacy Policy.

If you choose to play an embedded video, your browser connects to YouTube, which may receive technical and viewing information under Google's Privacy Policy. Other outbound links, including social-media links, are also governed by the destination's policies. We do not control those third-party services.

4. Disclosure of information

We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the website or communicate with you; when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security; or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets. We do not sell personal information collected through this website.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including to maintain business records and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You can clear the theme preference through your browser controls. You may also contact us to ask about information we hold about you or to exercise privacy rights available where you live. We may need to verify your request, and some exceptions may apply.

7. Children and international visitors

The website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. We are based in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do. For questions or privacy requests, email ian@withai.co.

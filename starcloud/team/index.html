Meet our team

We are a team of deep-tech engineers entirely focused on building massively scalable data center infrastructure in space.

Adi Oltean
Co-Founder & Chief Engineer
Adi Oltean
Co-Founder & Chief Engineer

Adi was previously a Principal Software Engineer at SpaceX where he was the responsible engineer in charge of 'tracking beams', which enables Starlink for other spacecraft, including Starship. Before that, he spent 20 years as a Principal Software Engineer at Microsoft working on large production GPU clusters, and inventing more than 25 unique patents. Adi holds degrees in Computer Science from University of Bucharest and Chemistry from University "Politehnica" of Bucharest.

Bailey Montaño
Engineering manager
Bailey Montaño
Engineering manager

After SpaceX, Bailey was a senior mechanical engineer at Helion Energy where he was the program lead and head designer of the arc fusion crucible program. Bailey earned his BS in Mechanical Engineering from MIT.

He has a BSc in CS from The Politehnica University of Bucharest and an MS in Computer Science & Engineering from the University of Washington.

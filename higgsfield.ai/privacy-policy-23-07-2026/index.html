1. Personal Information We Collect

1.1. Information you provide to us. Personal information you may provide to us through the Service or otherwise includes:

  • Contact data, such as your first and last name, email address, billing and mailing addresses, and phone number.
  • Profile data, such as the username and password that you may set to establish an online account on the Service, biographical details, links to your profiles on social networks, interests, preferences, information about your participation in our contests, promotions, or surveys, and any other information that you add to your account profile.
  • Demographic data, such as your date of birth, age, and gender.
  • Communications data based on our exchanges with you, including the contents of your messages, when you contact us through the Service, social media, or otherwise.
  • Transactional data, such as information relating to or needed to complete your orders on or through the Service, including order numbers and transaction history.
  • Payment data needed to complete transactions, including payment card information or bank account number. Please note that payment data is collected and stored by our payment processors and not by Higgsfield directly.
  • Marketing data, such as your preferences for receiving our marketing and other communications and details about your engagement with them.
  • User-shared multimedia data, such as text, photos, images, and videos that you upload to and process through the Service, as well as associated metadata. Metadata includes information on how, when, where and by whom a piece of content was collected and how that content has been formatted or edited. Metadata also includes information that users can add or can have added to their content, such as keywords, geographical or location information, and other similar data.
  • Query and prompt data, such as commands, descriptions, comments, questions, messages, and other content or information that you generate, transmit, or otherwise make available through our demos and otherwise through the Service, as well as associated metadata.
  • Feedback data, such as information regarding your experiences with the Service.
  • Other data not specifically listed here, which we will use as described in this Privacy Policy or as otherwise disclosed at the time of collection.
  • When you use certain features of the Service, you may upload photographs, images, videos, or audio containing a face or voice. Under certain laws, use of these features may involve the collection of biometric information, such as a faceprint or voiceprint, which we handle as described in the Biometric Information section below.

  • Public sources, such as government agencies, public records, social media platforms, and other publicly available sources.
  • Service providers that provide services on our behalf or help us operate the Service or our business.
  • Third-party services, such as social media services and cloud storage platforms that you use to log into, or otherwise link to your Service account, for example, Google: you may log in with your Google account.   • Device data, such as your computer or mobile device’s operating system type and version, manufacturer and model, browser type, screen resolution, RAM and disk size, CPU usage, device type (e.g., phone, tablet), IP address, unique identifiers (including identifiers used for advertising purposes), language settings, mobile device carrier, radio/network information (e.g., Wi-Fi, LTE, 5G), and general location information such as city, state or geographic area.

  • Communication interaction data such as your interactions with our email, text or other communications (e.g., whether you open and/or forward emails) – we may do this through use of pixel tags (which are also known as clear GIFs), which may be embedded invisibly in our emails.

1.4. Data about others. We may offer features that help users invite their friends or contacts to use the Service, and we may collect contact details about these invitees so we can deliver their invitations. Please do not refer someone to us or share their contact details with us unless you have their permission to do so. When we refer to biometric information, we mean information generated by measurements of your physical or biological characteristics, such as a scan of face geometry (a faceprint) or a voiceprint, and this term includes any biometric identifier and biometric data as those terms are defined under applicable law. This information is protected under laws including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, Washington’s biometric law, and, in the EEA/UK, as special category data under the GDPR. Depending on where you reside and how we use it, applicable law may define this information as a biometric identifier or biometric data. Notwithstanding anything else in this Privacy Policy, we handle biometric information as described in this section.

  • Purpose. Where we collect biometric information, we use it only to provide the feature you requested.
  • Consent. Where applicable law requires your consent before we collect or process your biometric information, we will obtain it, and we will present any required consent separately from this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use. Providing biometric information is voluntary; if you choose not to provide it, you may be unable to use the specific feature that relies on it, but this will not affect your use of the rest of the Service. For any questions about your biometric information, or to withdraw any consent you have given, you may contact us at privacy@higgsfield.ai, after which we stop processing your biometric information.
  • Retention and destruction. We do not store your biometric information. It is extracted and used only transiently, for the time needed to provide the feature you requested, and is destroyed as soon as that processing is complete, which satisfies the initial purpose for which it was collected. Because we do not retain it, there is no stored biometric information to delete afterward. This section is our written retention-and-destruction policy for biometric information.
  • No sale. We do not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise disclose your biometric information for profit.
  • Disclosure. We disclose biometric information only as described in this section. We may disclose it to limited service providers and other vendors who help us provide the Service, or as required by law or with your consent.
  • Security. We protect your biometric information while we process it, using a reasonable standard of care that is at least as protective as the way we protect other confidential information.

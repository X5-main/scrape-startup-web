We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is AI Video Upscaling and how does it work?
Video upscaling increases a video's resolution (e.g., from 1080p to 4K). Unlike traditional tools that just stretch pixels, our AI intelligently analyzes and reconstructs each frame, generating new pixels to add genuine detail, sharpen edges, and create a true high-definition image.
Can I use this video upscaler entirely online?
Yes, our platform is fully web-based. There's no software to download or install. You can access our upscaler directly from your web browser on any computer.
How long does the enhancement process take?
Processing speed depends on the video's length and original resolution. However, our platform is optimized for speed, and most standard videos are fully enhanced and ready for download within a few minutes.
Can I upscale my videos to 4K?
Yes, absolutely. 4K upscaling is a core feature of our platform. You can choose desired output resolution.
What video formats are compatible with your upscaler?
We support all major video formats, including MP4, MOV, AVI, WEBM, and many more.

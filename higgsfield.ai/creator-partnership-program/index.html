We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is the Higgsfield AI Creator Partnership Program?
The Higgsfield AI Creator Partnership Program is how Higgsfield backs the creators who are pushing AI content forward. Higgsfield is a full creative suite for making AI video, images, and audio, with tools like Cinema Studio, Soul, and Marketing Studio. A lot of creators build their work on it. The CPP is the structure we've built around our relationships with the best of them. The point of the program is simple: give serious creators the resources, access, and support to do their best work without hitting walls, and bring them together into one strong community.
Who can apply to the Higgsfield Creator Partnership Program?
Anyone creating with AI, at any level. That includes filmmakers, cinematographers, designers, editors, animators, tutorial makers, and social creators. Platform doesn't matter. YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, and cross-platform creators are all welcome. Audience size doesn't matter either. What matters is that AI content is genuinely central to what you make, not a side experiment. We work with both sides of the range: creators building their first audience from zero, and industry veterans bringing decades of experience into AI.
How to apply to the program
Applying is simple: 1. Fill out the application form on our site. 2. A real person on our team reviews every application. 3. There's no automated filter sorting people by follower count. 4. Everyone gets a response by email, whether it's a yes or a no. 5. If you're accepted, that email includes your next steps to get set up. How long the review takes depends on how many applications we're going through at the time. Either way, you'll hear back.
What do I get as a CPP partner?
Six things, from day one: 1. A plan and credits, every month. A full Higgsfield plan that renews each month, plus extra credits for bigger projects, films, and series. You make the ambitious version of your idea, not the cut-down one. 2. Growth support. Real, hands-on advice from our team on growing your audience and sharpening your content. Tips and playbooks tailored to what you make. 3. A direct line to the team. Talk directly with the Higgsfield team whenever you need to, for credits, questions, or feedback. 4. Early access. New models and features reach partners before they go public, plus exclusive offers. 5. An exclusive community. A curated community of partners across 35+ countries, sharing what works and collaborating. 6. Getting featured. Partner work gets featured across Higgsfield's channels and the creator hub, extra reach on top of your own.
What's expected of me as a CPP partner?
We ask you to tag or mention Higgsfield when the tools are part of your work, and stay actively engaged with our launches when they're relevant to what you're making. Beyond that, you have full creative freedom. You keep full ownership of your work and your voice.
How the community is built
The community is the heart of the program, and it's built to stay focused and useful. It's a curated space for a group of creators who take AI content seriously and are happy to share what's actually working, swap strategies, and collaborate when there's a real reason to. On top of the day-to-day, we run challenges, creator spotlights, and sessions where partners can learn from each other. The biggest thing partners tell us is that they're not doing this alone anymore. The credits and tools matter, but it's the support and the people around them that tie it all together.
Is this the same as a creative partner program?
Yes. The Higgsfield AI Creator Partnership Program is our take on a creative partner program. We say Creator because Higgsfield is a full creative suite, and the program is for everyone building with it.
What's the difference between CPP and the Earn program?
CPP is our partnership program with a plan, credits, early access, and community. The Earn program at higgsfield.ai runs paid campaigns open to creators of any size, a good place to start if you want to work with us before applying to CPP. The two also work together: as a CPP partner, you can receive your own individual Earn campaigns on top of everything the program already gives you.

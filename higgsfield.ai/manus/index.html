Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

Do I need a Higgsfield account?
If you have one, sign in and your credits + trained characters carry over. If not, the connection sets one up the first time you call a generation. No API keys to manage.
Does it really post to my Instagram?
Manus drafts and schedules. You connect your account and approve once — after that, the calendar publishes on the cadence you set. You can pause or take over any time.
Will the content actually look like my brand?
Higgsfield trains on your product photos and reference shots. The same jar, the same label, the same creators show up across the whole calendar — not a different aesthetic every post.
What if I don't like a post?
Tell Manus. "Reshoot day 3 outdoors." "Make the hook louder." "More UGC, less hero shots." It re-runs Higgsfield with the new direction. No exports, no re-uploads.
How is this different from just using Higgsfield directly?
Higgsfield generates. Manus plans, researches, schedules, and learns. Direct Higgsfield is a tool — Manus + Higgsfield is a workflow that runs without you.

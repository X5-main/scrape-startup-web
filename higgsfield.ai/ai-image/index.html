Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What AI image models are available on Higgsfield?
Higgsfield gives you access to 15+ leading models in one workspace - including Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Seedream, FLUX, Kling O1, and Higgsfield Soul. You can switch between models instantly and compare outputs to find the best fit for your project.
What is the maximum image resolution I can generate?
You can generate images at up to 4K (4096x4096) resolution natively - no upscaling required. Nano Banana Pro and Seedream both support native 4K output straight from the prompt.
Can Higgsfield generate accurate text inside images?
Yes. Nano Banana Pro is built on a reasoning engine that understands spelling, layout, and typography. It renders legible headlines, labels, and packaging text in multiple languages - a problem most AI image generators still struggle with.
Can I keep the same character consistent across multiple images?
Yes. Soul ID locks a character's facial structure, style, and identity across generations. Whether you're building a campaign, a storyboard, or a lookbook - the same person appears in every frame without drift.
Can I turn my AI images into video?
Yes. You can push any generated image directly into video models like Sora 2, Kling, Seedance, or WAN - all within the same editor. Add cinematic camera movements, motion control, and lip-sync without exporting to another tool.
What aspect ratios and formats are supported?
Higgsfield supports all standard formats - 1:1, 9:16, 16:9, 3:4, 4:3, and more. You can generate platform-ready visuals for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and print from the same workspace.
Can I edit specific parts of a generated image without regenerating the whole thing?
Yes. Nano Banana Pro Inpaint lets you brush over any area and change it - swap objects, fix backgrounds, rewrite text, adjust colors. The edit blends seamlessly with the rest of the image.
Is Higgsfield free to use?
Yes. You can sign up and start generating immediately with free daily credits. Paid plans unlock unlimited generations, higher resolution output, and team collaboration features.
Can I use Higgsfield with my team?
Yes. The Team Plan provides a shared workspace where your entire team can generate, review, and organize assets together. Each member gets their own credits, and built-in analytics track usage across projects.
Can I input my exact brand colors?
Yes. Nano Banana Pro lets you input Hex or RGB color codes directly to ensure every generation matches your brand guidelines - backgrounds, text, and design elements all stay on-brand from the first output.

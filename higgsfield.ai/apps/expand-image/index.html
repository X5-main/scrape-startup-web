Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is AI Expand?
AI Expand is a tool that expands your image beyond its original borders. Upload any photo and the AI will generate new content around it - extending backgrounds, scenery, and context while keeping your original subject untouched.
How does AI Expand work?
Just upload an image and select your desired aspect ratio. The AI analyzes your photo's lighting, colors, perspective, and scene context, then generates matching content to fill in the new space. No prompts or descriptions needed.
Will my original image be altered?
No. Your original photo stays pixel-perfect. The AI only paints outward from the existing edges - it never modifies, distorts, or reframes your subject.
What aspect ratios are supported?
You can expand to standard formats like 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, and 1:1. This makes it easy to reformat a single photo for different platforms - landscape for YouTube thumbnails, portrait for Stories, square for Instagram posts.
What image formats can I upload?
AI Expand supports PNG and JPG files.
Do I need to write a prompt?
No. AI Expand is completely prompt-free. The AI understands your scene automatically and decides what to generate around it. Just upload and go.

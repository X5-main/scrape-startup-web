Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is the AI Meme Generator?
It's a free tool that lets you create memes using AI-generated characters. Choose from a library of ready-made presets or describe a character from scratch - our AI will generate a unique, photorealistic person for your meme. No stock photos, no real people, no copyright risks.
How does it work?
It takes three steps. First, pick a character preset or type a prompt to generate one. Second, add your caption text - drag it, resize it, place it wherever you want. Third, download your meme for free. You can also hit "Generate text" to let AI suggest five caption options for your image.
Is it really free?
Yes. You can create and download memes at no cost, with no watermarks. Character presets are free to use, and AI text generation is included.
Are the people in the memes real?
No. Every character is fully generated by AI. They don't represent real individuals, which means your memes are IP-safe and cleared for posting anywhere - social media, group chats, brand pages, you name it.
Can I use these memes commercially?
Yes. Since all characters are AI-generated, there are no likeness rights or model release issues. You're free to use your memes for content creation, marketing, social media, or any other purpose.
What is "Generate text"?
It's an AI-powered caption tool. Click the button and the AI analyzes your image, then suggests five different captions with different punchlines. Pick the one you like or edit it to make it your own.
Do I need to sign up?
You can browse presets without an account, but you'll need a free Higgsfield account to generate characters from prompts, use AI text generation, and download your memes.
Can I edit the meme after generating it?
Absolutely. The editor lets you reposition text, resize text boxes, change captions, and switch between different AI-generated variations of your image - all before downloading.

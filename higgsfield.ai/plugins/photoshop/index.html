Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is the Higgsfield plugin for Photoshop?
It's a native UXP plugin that brings five Higgsfield AI tools — Angles, Mockup Studio, Expand, Remove BG, and Relight — directly inside Adobe Photoshop. No need to switch between apps or export files back and forth.
Which Photoshop versions are supported?
The plugin works with Photoshop 2024 and newer on both macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows. Older versions don't support UXP plugins and aren't compatible.
How do I install the plugin?
Download the .uxp installer for macOS or Windows, drag Higgsfield into your Applications folder, and double-click to launch. The installer automatically detects Photoshop. Then open Photoshop and go to Window → Extensions → Higgsfield.
Do I need a Higgsfield account to use the plugin?
Yes. Sign in with your existing Higgsfield account inside the panel to start generating. Free credits are included with every account, and paid plans unlock higher limits and faster generations.
Does the plugin work offline?
No. All generations run on Higgsfield's cloud GPUs, so an internet connection is required. Your results stream directly back into Photoshop as new layers.
Will my files or images be stored on Higgsfield's servers?
Images are processed securely on our infrastructure and aren't used for training. You can review our privacy policy for full details on data handling and retention.
Does it work with layers, masks, and smart objects?
Yes. Each generation comes back as a new editable layer, so you can mask, blend, and refine just like any other Photoshop layer. Smart objects and adjustment layers are fully respected.
What's the difference between this plugin and Photoshop's built-in Generative Fill?
Generative Fill is Adobe's general-purpose tool powered by Firefly. The Higgsfield plugin gives you five specialized tools built for specific creative tasks — like changing camera angles, building product mockups, and relighting scenes — that go beyond what Generative Fill offers.
Can I use the plugin commercially?
Yes. All generations made with a paid Higgsfield plan come with a commercial license. Check your plan details for specifics on usage rights.
What if the plugin doesn't appear in the Extensions menu?
Make sure you're running Photoshop 2024 or newer and restart the app after installation. If it still doesn't show up, reinstall the .uxp file or reach out to support.

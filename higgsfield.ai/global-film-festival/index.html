How it works

Three steps from first scene to final cut

  1. 1

    Publish a scene

    One scene, a poster, a logline - your film is live

  2. 2

    Create in public

    Add scenes and cuts as you go. All versions stay visible, and the community can support you

  3. 3

    Deliver the final cut

    Submit your finished film by September 3. Films that reach the Festival Shortlist move on to jury review.

GOT ANY QUESTIONS LEFT?

A way for the community to back each other, and for the Higgsfield team to back you with grants. You publish an unfinished version to a public page, and people can watch it, like it, and follow along as it develops. That early support does three things for you:

  • Community support - likes that push you toward a shortlist slot
  • Direct team picks - we watch published projects during the generation window and can pull strong work into the shortlist
  • Grant candidacy - published projects are automatically in the running for credits

Publishing is optional, but a private project gets none of this until you submit.

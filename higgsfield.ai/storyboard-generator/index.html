Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield Popcorn?
Higgsfield Popcorn is an advanced AI storyboarding tool that allows you to create visually consistent scenes from text prompts and reference images. It's designed for creators who need full control over their narrative, including consistent characters, objects, and environments, with a direct option to export your storyboard to Sora 2 for video generation.
Is there a free trial or a free version to try?
Yes! You can sign up and start creating right away. We provide all users with free daily credits. These credits allow you to test all of Popcorn's features, from generating consistent characters to creating full storyboards.
Can I use my own character, face, or product in the storyboard?
Yes. You can upload an image of a specific person, object, or product. Higgsfield Popcorn will use this reference to generate new images of that same item, allowing you to create shots from different camera angles, in various lighting, or interacting with new elements.
How many scenes I can create?
You can generate up to 8 images in a single sequence. However, the process is theoretically limitless. You can take the last image of your sequence and use it as the new reference input to continue the story, allowing you to build narratives of any length.
Can I animate the storyboard or to generate video from the images?
Once your storyboard is finalized and approved, you can use our built-in option to easily export the sequence to Sora 2. This seamlessly turns your static, consistent storyboard images into a dynamic, generated video, taking you from concept to final video much faster.

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is this?
Describe a game in plain words and Supercomputer builds it — code, assets, 3D and all. You get a working, playable build with a link to share. No engine, no setup.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Just describe the game you want and pick a genre template. If you do code, you can open the generated project and edit it directly.
What's the difference between Higgsfield MCP and Supercomputer?
MCP lets you generate games from your own client — Claude, Cursor — inside your own stack. Supercomputer is the all-in-one space where deploy, multiplayer and 3D are handled automatically.
How does deploy work?
Automatically. Once the build is ready, Supercomputer hosts it and gives you a live link. It runs in the browser — nothing to install.
Can I make multiplayer games?
Yes. Flip multiplayer on and it's wired for you — lobbies and state sync included. Share one link and play together in seconds.
Can it generate 3D?
Yes. Ask for a world or a character and you get 3D assets, environments and models built right into the game — no modeling or asset hunting.

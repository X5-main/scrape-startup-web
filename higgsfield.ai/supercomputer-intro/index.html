We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Higgsfield Supercomputer?
It's a chat that runs Higgsfield for you. Describe what you want to make — a reel, an ad, a product shot, a week of content — and Higgsfield Supercomputer plans it, picks the right models and presets, and delivers the finished asset. No timelines, no briefs, no tool-hopping.
Do I need to know how to prompt?
No. Plain language works. "Make a TikTok for my sneakers" is a complete brief. Higgsfield Supercomputer fills in the details, checks the plan with you, and starts working.
Do I need to pick a model or a preset?
No. Every model in Higgsfield — Soul, Cinema Studio, Seedance, Nano Banana, and the rest — shows up as "auto." Higgsfield Supercomputer picks the right one for each job.
What are Skills?
Skills are installable workflows — a commercial ad pipeline, a product demo flow, a trend research pass. Install the ones you need from the Marketplace. Higgsfield Supercomputer runs them on demand.
Can I use what I generate commercially?
Yes. Everything you make on Higgsfield is cleared for commercial use.
How much does it cost?
Every plan Higgsfield Supercomputer runs shows the credit cost upfront — before anything renders. You approve the spend, then it generates.
Where does my finished content go?
Every generation lands in a project. Download it, share it, or drop it into the next chat as a starting point.

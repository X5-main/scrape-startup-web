What is Higgsfield MCP, and how is it different from the web?

HiggsfieldAug 1, 20262 min

Higgsfield MCP connects Higgsfield to AI agents. Connect once and generate images, videos, characters, and audio from Claude or any MCP-compatible agent, without leaving your agent. The main difference from generating on the web: MCP always uses credits. Unlimited access and free generations apply only on higgsfield.ai, so anything generated through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, or other automated tools deducts credits at standard rates.

What is Higgsfield MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open protocol that lets AI agents connect to external services and call their tools. Higgsfield MCP is a connector that lets you generate images, videos, characters, and audio from Higgsfield directly inside Claude or another MCP-compatible agent. It isn't a separate product or API: it connects to your existing account and uses your existing plan credits, with no API key required.

Because MCP connects programmatically, using Higgsfield through it falls under the Developer Terms in the Terms of Use. In short: keep your connection secure, and you're responsible for anything an agent generates through your account.

MCP server URL: https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp

How is MCP different from the web?

The most important difference is credit behavior: everything generated through MCP deducts credits, regardless of your plan.

Do Unlimited models and free generations work through MCP?

No. Unlimited access and free generations apply only on higgsfield.ai: they're intended for manual use, not for automated or programmatic generation. Anything generated through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, or other automated tools deducts credits at standard rates. To generate on a model where your plan includes Unlimited access without spending credits, do it on the web at higgsfield.ai.

What can I do through MCP?

Image and video generation, Soul character use, Higgsfield Audio, and credit balance checks. For the full list of operations and how to phrase requests to your agent, see How do I connect Higgsfield to Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI agent?

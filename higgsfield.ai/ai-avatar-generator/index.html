We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is an AI avatar?
An AI avatar is a photorealistic AI-generated person that appears in your videos as a presenter, reviewer, or model. Marketing Studio's avatars are powered by Soul 2.0, Higgsfield's proprietary photorealistic avatar model. They speak, react, move, and perform in every video format Marketing Studio supports.
How many avatars are available?
40+ ready-to-use avatars are available in the library, spanning different age ranges, styles, ethnicities, wardrobes, and contexts. You can also generate unlimited custom avatars by describing the look you want in a text prompt.
Can I create a custom AI avatar?
Yes. Describe the avatar you need in plain language ("a woman in a tailored blazer, urban professional") and Soul 2.0 generates a photorealistic AI person in seconds. Custom avatars can be saved, pinned, renamed, and reused across every campaign.
How realistic are the avatars?
Soul 2.0 produces photorealistic avatars with natural facial expressions, consistent identity across shots, and lifelike lip-sync powered by Seedance 2.0's native audio generation. They perform in iPhone-style UGC, studio-grade commercials, and cinematic TV spots.
Can my AI avatar speak different languages?
Yes. Avatars can speak in multiple languages with natural voice and lip-sync. The default output language is English unless you specify otherwise.
Can AI avatars hold or interact with products?
Yes. Avatars can hold, present, wear, apply, and demonstrate products on camera. Marketing Studio's product understanding system ensures avatars interact with your product realistically, following correct physical mechanics for each product category.
Can I upload my own photo and turn it into an avatar?
Yes. Upload a photo of yourself or any person and Soul 2.0 creates a photorealistic AI avatar from it. Your custom avatar is saved to your library and can be used across every format Marketing Studio supports, from UGC talking heads to cinematic TV spots.
Do I own the videos created with AI avatars?
Yes. Videos generated in Marketing Studio are yours to use across any channel, including paid ads, social posts, landing pages, and email campaigns.
Are the avatars royalty-free?
Yes. Avatars generated in Marketing Studio are royalty-free for commercial use. No actor contracts, no licensing fees, no rights renegotiation.

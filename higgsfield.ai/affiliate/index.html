Got any questions left?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

Who can join?
Anyone with an audience! And your affiliate link will be available immediately. If you're an established creator, contact us on affiliate@higgsfield.ai.
How much do I earn?
Up to 25% of the plan price your referrals pay. Your rate grows with the revenue you generate.
How long does a referral keep paying me?
Twelve months from the day they become your referral. On monthly plans you earn each month they renew. Annual and quarterly plans are paid upfront, so your commission for the whole term is credited straight away.
What earns commission?
All subscription purchases, including renewals and upgrades, within the first 12 months of a referral's subscription.
What does my audience get?
A personal discount of up to 50%, active for three hours from the moment they log in through your link.
Can I run paid ads to my link?
No. Paid advertising, bidding on the Higgsfield brand name, and posting to coupon/cashback sites aren't allowed. Everything organic is fine.

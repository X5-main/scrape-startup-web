Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is AI video generation?
AI video generation is the process of creating videos using artificial intelligence models. Instead of filming with a physical camera, you describe what you want (through text prompts, reference images, or both) and the AI produces a video clip. Modern AI video tools like Higgsfield go far beyond basic prompt-to-video by giving you control over camera movement, character consistency, optical style, and more.
What AI video models are available on Higgsfield?
Higgsfield gives you access to multiple leading AI video models in one workspace, including Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Wan 2.7, Sora 2, and others. You can switch between models without leaving the platform, compare outputs side by side, and pick the best result for your project.
What is Cinema Studio?
Cinema Studio is Higgsfield's flagship feature for professional cinematic video generation. It simulates real optical physics, letting you choose a virtual camera body, lens type, and focal length before generating. You can lock characters across shots, control depth of field, and stack multiple camera movements to create footage that looks and feels like it was shot on a real film set.
Do I need video editing experience to use Higgsfield?
No. Higgsfield is designed to be accessible to beginners while offering advanced controls for professionals. You can start with a simple text prompt and generate a video in minutes. As you get more comfortable, you can explore features like motion control, first/last frame references, and Cinema Studio for more precise results.
Can I use my own images or videos as input?
Yes. You can upload reference images to guide the style, subject, or composition of your generation. You can also upload existing video footage to edit it, swap styles, change objects, or refine details without reshooting. The first and last frame reference feature lets you lock the start and end points of your video for consistent output.
What can I use AI-generated videos for?
Higgsfield is used across a wide range of industries: filmmakers and directors use it for pre-visualization and short films, marketing and fashion teams create product ads and campaign videos, content creators produce social media clips at scale, and educators build training and explainer videos. Any use case that requires visual storytelling can benefit from AI video.
How is Higgsfield different from other AI video generators?
Most AI video tools give you a text box and a single model. Higgsfield is a full creative workspace. You get access to multiple top models in one place, professional-grade camera and lens simulation through Cinema Studio, motion control for precise choreography, character locking for consistency across scenes, and tools like Vibe Motion and Mixed Media for specialized workflows.
What video quality and formats does Higgsfield support?
Higgsfield generates high-fidelity video in HD resolution. Cinema Studio supports cinematic 21:9 aspect ratio alongside standard formats. Output quality depends on the model and settings you choose, with Cinema Studio producing 16-bit cinematic-grade visuals.
Is Higgsfield free to use?
Higgsfield offers free access to get started. Some models, features, and higher usage tiers may require a paid plan. You can explore the platform and generate your first videos without a subscription.

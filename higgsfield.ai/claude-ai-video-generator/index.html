Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Claude Video Generator?
Claude Video Generator is an AI video tool by Higgsfield that turns text, images, or briefs into finished video through a chat conversation. Claude connects with Higgsfield MCP and orchestrates the leading video models (Soul, Veo, Kling, Seedance, and more) so you get the best output for every shot without switching tools or learning each model individually.
Which video models does Claude use?
30+ leading video models including Veo, Kling, Seedance, Soul, Minimax Hailuo, and Cinema Studio. Claude picks the best model for the shot automatically, or you can name one. Run the same prompt across multiple models in parallel and compare results side by side.
What video quality and length can it produce?
Cinematic video up to 4K resolution, up to 15 seconds per clip, in any aspect ratio for any platform (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5). Output is watermark-free on paid plans and broadcast-ready for paid ads, ecommerce listings, and client deliverables.
How fast does it render a video?
Most clips finish in seconds to a few minutes depending on duration and model choice. Renders run asynchronously in the background, so you can keep briefing the next shot, the next variant, or the next campaign while the current one finishes.
How does pricing work?
Higgsfield offers free renders to start. Paid usage runs on a credit system, priced by model, duration, and resolution. Total cost lands at a fraction of a $5,000 agency retainer, per-shoot creator fees, or licensing stock footage for commercial use.
Can I use Claude videos commercially?
Yes, You can generate video that can run in paid ads, client campaigns, ecommerce listings, and social channels without additional licensing. Review individual model terms when your brief uses branded references, copyrighted material, or third-party likenesses.
Can I keep characters consistent across multiple videos?
Yes. Train a character once from a few photos and reuse it across scenes, locations, and campaigns. Claude Video Generator remembers your cast, brand kit, and past renders inside the conversation, so week-three video matches week-one video without retraining.
How is Claude Video Generator different from Sora, Runway, or Veo?
Single-tool generators give you one model and one workflow. Higgsfield orchestrates 30+ video models in Claude from one chat, holds characters and brand kits across sessions, runs full campaigns end to end (research, generation, delivery), and replaces an agency stack rather than just rendering individual clips.
How do I get started with Claude Video Generator?
Open Claude on web, desktop, or mobile, describe the video you want, and your first render lands in seconds. No API keys, no editing skills, no prompt-engineering, no model picking. Operators with zero production background ship broadcast-quality video on the first try.

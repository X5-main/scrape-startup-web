Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Nano Banana Pro?
Nano Banana Pro is Google’s flagship image generation model, technically known as Gemini 3 Pro Image. Built on the Gemini 3 Pro reasoning backbone, it functions less like a traditional diffusion model and more like a digital art director — analyzing your prompt for semantic logic, physical causality, and spatial relationships before rendering. The result: native 2K output that intelligently upscales to 4K with a 16-bit color pipeline, accurate text rendering, and the highest compositional ceiling Google offers for image generation.
What is Nano Banana 2?
Nano Banana 2 is Google’s latest image model, technically Gemini 3.1 Flash Image. It takes the advanced reasoning, world knowledge, and quality of Nano Banana Pro and delivers them at Flash speed — generating images in seconds rather than the deliberate “thinking” pass Pro performs. With resolutions from 512px to 4K, character consistency for up to 5 subjects, 14-object fidelity, and exclusive Image Search Grounding for real-world accuracy, it’s built for rapid iteration and high-volume creative work.
What’s the difference between Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2?
Same family, different priorities. Nano Banana Pro is built for maximum reasoning depth, fine typography, and the highest compositional ceiling — best when quality matters more than speed. Nano Banana 2 brings most of those Pro capabilities to Flash-level speed at a lower cost. Pro is for craftsmanship, Banana 2 is for momentum.
Which model should I use?
Use Nano Banana 2 for rapid prototyping, batch generation, real-time editing, social content, and high-volume work — it’s significantly faster and cheaper, with quality very close to Pro in everyday use. Use Nano Banana Pro for hero images, packaging design, complex multi-element scenes, fine print typography, and any high-stakes deliverable where the quality ceiling matters more than speed. Many teams on Higgsfield run a tiered workflow: Banana 2 to explore, Pro to finalize.
How much faster is Nano Banana 2?
Nano Banana 2 generates images roughly 2–3x faster than Pro on Flash-optimized inference. In practice, that’s seconds per image versus the more deliberate “thinking” pass Pro performs to reason through spatial relationships, lighting, and composition.
Do they produce the same quality?
For most prompts, the gap is small — Banana 2 reaches near-Pro quality on typical scenarios. Pro pulls ahead on complex compositions with strict spatial logic, fine typography on packaging or print, and dense multi-subject scenes where every relationship needs to be precise. If you can’t tell the difference in your use case, use Banana 2.
What resolutions do they support?
Nano Banana 2 generates from 512px up to 4K, with multiple aspect ratios — including a unique ultra-low-cost tier for high-volume work. Nano Banana Pro renders natively at 2K and intelligently upscales to 4K using a 16-bit color pipeline for richer gradients and reduced banding.
Can either model render readable text?
Yes — text is a first-class element in both. You’ll get clean typography for headlines, product names, UI mockups, and packaging. Both also handle in-image translation and localization across languages and scripts. Pro has a slight edge on intricate typography and complex layouts; Banana 2 is fully production-ready for most marketing and social use cases.
What about character and object consistency?
Both models maintain consistency for up to 5 characters and fidelity of up to 14 objects in a single workflow. This makes them practical for storyboarding, multi-character scenes, branded campaigns, and any project where identity has to hold across frames.
Does either model use real-world knowledge?
Both pull from Gemini’s world knowledge for accurate rendering of landmarks, public figures, brands, and specific subjects. Nano Banana 2 also supports Image Search Grounding — it can retrieve real reference images from Google Search during generation to boost factual accuracy on specific subjects.
Can I edit existing images with these models?
Yes. On Higgsfield, both models power precise editing workflows including Banana Placement for product insertion, Nano Banana Pro Inpaint for structure-preserving local edits, and multi-reference composition. Brush over an area, describe the change or upload a reference, and the model handles seamless blending without disturbing the rest of the image.
How do they fit into the rest of the Higgsfield ecosystem?
Use either model as your "layout and logic" stage — set your scene, text, and characters — then push frames into video models like Sora 2, Kling, MiniMax, or Seedance for motion. Combine with Face Swap, Soul ID, or Popcorn for consistent characters across longer narratives. Same identities, same scenes, full pipeline.
How is pricing structured on Higgsfield?
Both models run on Higgsfield’s credit system, with Banana 2 priced lower per generation than Pro to reflect its speed-and-scale positioning. Exact credit costs depend on resolution and settings — see the pricing page for current rates and plan options.

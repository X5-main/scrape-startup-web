Still have questions?

We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Soul Cast?
Soul Cast is an AI actor builder inside Cinema Studio 2.0. It lets you create fully customizable AI characters by setting parameters like genre, era, archetype, physique, outfit, and imperfections - no prompting required.
How does the builder work?
You select options across eight categories: Genre, Budget, Era, Archetype, Identity (gender, race, age), Physical Appearance, Details (scars, tattoos, freckles), and Outfit. Soul Cast generates a unique AI actor based on your selections.
Will my character look the same across different scenes?
Yes. Soul Cast actors are built to stay visually consistent across every scene in Cinema Studio 2.0 - different angles, lighting, and settings, same face.
What genres are available?
14 genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Thriller, Horror, Detective, Romance, Sci-Fi, Fantasy, War, Western, Historical, and Sitcom.
What does the Budget parameter do?
It controls the visual polish of your character. A higher budget produces a refined, blockbuster-grade look. A lower budget gives a grittier, more indie aesthetic.
What are archetypes?
Archetypes define your character's narrative role. Soul Cast includes 12 options: Innocent, Everyman, Hero, Caregiver, Explorer, Rebel, Lover, Creator, Jester, Sage, Magician, and Ruler.
Does Soul Cast generate a backstory for my character?
Yes. Every actor comes with an auto-generated backstory, personality traits, and a character sheet with motivation, fear, flaw, and strength.
Can I use Soul Cast actors in videos?
Yes. Soul Cast is built to work directly with Cinema Studio 2.0. Characters you create can be used across image and video generation with consistent identity.
Can I own exclusive rights to a character?
Yes. For a one-time fee of $5, you can lock any character to your account. No one else will be able to generate or use that face. Full commercial rights included.
Is Soul Cast a separate product?
No. Soul Cast lives inside Cinema Studio 2.0 on higgsfield.ai. You can access it from the Cast tab in the Cinema Studio navigation.

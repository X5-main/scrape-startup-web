We’ve answered the most frequently asked questions

What is Marketing Studio?
Higgsfield's workspace for turning any product into ready-to-post marketing content. Add a product, pick a template or describe a scene, and generate images and videos built around your brand — no shoot, no crew, no post-production.
What can I create?
Six formats: product shots, UGC videos, ads, marketplace images, posters, and motion graphics. Styles run from clean closeup packshots to faceless, full-body, and editorial scenes — and the template library keeps growing.
How does product import work?
Paste a link to your website or social and the product loads in automatically — name, description, images. Or upload photos and build the product card yourself. Products stay in your library and are reusable across every template.
What does Recreate do?
Open any template, swap in your product, pick an avatar, describe what should look different, and hit Recreate. You get the same style rebuilt around your brand — one click, no prompt engineering.
Can I edit the text on posters and layouts?
Yes. Posters open as layers: every headline, date, and image is a separate element you can rewrite, rename, or swap before recreating. The style stays intact; the content becomes yours.
What are avatars?
AI presenters that appear in your content. Choose from 100+ in the library or generate your own, then pin and reuse them so your brand keeps one consistent face across campaigns.
Does it work for my kind of product?
If you can photograph it or link to it, it works — beauty, food and drink, fashion, electronics, digital brands. Marketing Studio builds the scene around whatever you bring.
Do I need design or photo experience?
No. Templates carry the art direction — framing, lighting, styling, layout. You choose the look; Marketing Studio does the production.
How many credits does a generation cost?
Every generation shows its credit cost on the button before you run it, so you always know what a render will use.
Can I use the results commercially?
Yes — content you generate is yours to publish across your channels, from organic posts to paid campaigns, subject to Higgsfield's Terms of Use.

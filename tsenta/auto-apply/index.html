The problem

The problem with most auto apply software

There are two kinds of tools in this category, and neither one fully works.

Autofill extensions (Simplify, Teal, Jobright, Careerflow) save you about 5 minutes of typing per application. You still have to find the job, open the portal, sign in, click through every screen, and hit submit yourself. They are not auto apply tools. They are form-fill shortcuts.

Cloud appliers (LazyApply, JobCopilot, AIApply, LoopCV) actually submit — but most are limited to LinkedIn Easy Apply or job boards where hundreds of people have already applied. Many run on 3-4 day latency. Some cap you at 100 applications before charging add-on fees for résumé tailoring or cover letters.

Tsenta watches 50,000+ company career pages directly. The moment a matching role goes live, it submits a tailored application through the real ATS portal — in 2-3 seconds — before most applicants see the posting on any job board.

How it works

How Tsenta auto-applies to jobs

Step 1

Upload your résumé and set your preferences

Tell Tsenta the roles, locations, salary range, and seniority level you want. Set what to avoid. This takes about 10 minutes once.

Step 2

Tsenta monitors 50,000+ career pages, 24/7

When a role that fits your profile goes live on a company's career page, Tsenta detects it in seconds. You're in the match feed immediately — ahead of anyone waiting on LinkedIn or Indeed to syndicate the listing.

Step 3

Your résumé is rewritten for the role

Tsenta reads the actual job description, identifies the keywords the recruiter is screening for, and rewrites your résumé to align them. Every change is shown to you before anything goes out. Applications use only true facts from the résumé you uploaded.

Step 4

Tsenta logs in and submits the full application

No click required from you. Tsenta navigates the ATS form, fills every field — including open-ended questions written in your voice — uploads your résumé and cover letter, and submits. You get a receipt showing exactly what was sent: every field, every answer, the résumé version, the timestamp.

Step 5

Replies route to the right application automatically

Recruiter emails land back on the correct application in your tracker. Status moves on its own. Your tracker shows what's applied, viewed, replied, and scheduled.

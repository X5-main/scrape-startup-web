Agent, not a tool

What makes this an agent, not a tool

Every job search product calls itself an AI agent now. Most are not.

A tracker extension (Simplify, Teal, Jobright) prefills your name and email. You find the job, sign in, click through, and hit submit yourself. It saves about 5 minutes of typing per application. It is not an agent.

A cloud applier (LazyApply, JobCopilot, AIApply, LoopCV) submits for you — but only after you've told it where to look. You still choose the job boards. It still pulls from listings that are already 24-72 hours old with 100+ applicants in queue. Most charge extra for résumé tailoring or cover letters.

Tsenta is different at the source. It monitors company career pages directly — before listings appear anywhere else — evaluates fit against your résumé, and submits in 2-3 seconds of detection. You are applicant #4, not #247. That gap is the product.

How it works

How Tsenta's AI agent works

Stage 01

Find

Tsenta monitors 50,000+ company career pages, continuously. The moment a role that matches your preferences goes live on a company's own site, Tsenta detects it. You are in the first 100 applicants before the job board even knows the listing exists.

Stage 02

Prep

Tsenta reads the actual job description, identifies the keywords the ATS and recruiter are screening for, and rewrites your résumé to align them. A diff view shows every change before anything goes out. Applications use only true facts from the résumé you uploaded.

Stage 03

Apply

Tsenta logs in, navigates the full ATS form, fills every field including open-ended questions written in your voice, uploads your résumé and cover letter, and submits. You get a receipt: every field filled, every answer given, the résumé version sent, the timestamp.

Stage 04

Track

Recruiter replies route to the correct application automatically. Status updates on its own. Your tracker shows applied, viewed, replied, scheduled — without a spreadsheet.

What sets Tsenta apart

What sets Tsenta apart

Career pages, not job boards

Job boards are secondary markets. By the time a listing appears on LinkedIn or Indeed, it is typically 24-72 hours old with a full first wave of applicants already in. Tsenta watches the original source. Recruiters typically review only the first 100 applications. Being in that window is the entire advantage.

Workday. Actually.

Workday accounts for roughly 40% of real jobs at large companies. Most AI job search agents either skip Workday entirely or break on its multi-page forms. Tsenta handles Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Rippling, iCIMS, BambooHR, Workable, JazzHR, Jobvite, BreezyHR, Oracle Cloud, and more — 15+ ATSes covering 75%+ of Fortune 500 listings. Most competitors cover 25-30%.

Résumé tailoring and cover letters included

AIApply charges $12 on top of the base subscription for résumé optimization and another $12 for cover letters. JobCopilot gates résumé tailoring behind higher tiers. At Tsenta, tailored résumés and cover letters are part of every application on every plan. No add-ons.

You see exactly what went out

Every application has a receipt: the fields filled, the answers given, the résumé version sent, the timestamp. You are never surprised by what went out in your name. No other agent in this category offers this level of transparency by default.

Company Overview

Tsenta is a Y Combinator-backed (S26) startup building a transparent agent for job applications. Tsenta watches 50,000+ company career pages, tailors your résumé per role, submits the application, and tracks the reply — across web, native iOS and Android apps, desktop, iMessage, WhatsApp, Chrome extension, and as an MCP server / CLI for any AI agent.

Every change Tsenta makes is shown to the user before it goes out. Founded to give job seekers volume without losing quality or control over what gets sent in their name.

How AI Is Used in Tsenta

Tsenta integrates AI throughout the job application process to provide a smarter, more efficient experience:

  • Job Matching:Tsenta scores each newly-posted role against your résumé, surfacing only jobs you actually qualify for, often hours before LinkedIn or Indeed index them.
  • Résumé Rewriting:Per-role résumé and cover letter generation from the actual job description, with keyword alignment, ATS-safe formatting, and a diff view of every change before send.
  • Open-Ended Answers:Drafts answers to free-text questions (e.g. “Why this company?”) in your voice, drawing on your résumé and prior submissions.
  • Form Automation:Intelligent form recognition and auto-filling across 19 ATSes, including login flows, multi-page forms, and document uploads.
  • Reply Routing:Recruiter emails are routed back to the right application and the status moves on its own, so the inbox stays a pipeline you can read.

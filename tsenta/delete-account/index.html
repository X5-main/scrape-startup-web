How to Delete Your Account

To request the deletion of your Tsenta account and all associated data, please send an email to founders@tsenta.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your Tsenta account.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your account and profile information will be permanently deleted.
  • Your application history and saved jobs will be removed.
  • Any active subscription will be cancelled.
  • This action is irreversible and cannot be undone.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion, please contact us at founders@tsenta.com.

Why Do You Need a Tool to Mass Apply to Multiple Jobs Fast?

The math of job searching in 2026 is straightforward and painful. Recruiters typically review the first 100 applications submitted to any given role. Every hour of delay costs you position. Applying manually to Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and the dozens of other portals where real jobs live can take 30-45 minutes per application, and the average job seeker needs to submit well over 100 applications to generate meaningful interview activity. That is 50 to 75 hours of repetitive form-filling, most of it before a human ever reads a word you wrote.

Tsenta was built to solve exactly this problem. The founders applied to 3,000+ jobs manually as international students, tried every tool on the market, and found that none of them actually worked end-to-end. So they built their own.

Why Slow Tools Are Disqualifying: The Problems Mass Applicants Face

  • Latency kills your position. A tool that processes applications hours or days after a role posts means you arrive after the first 100 are already in.
  • Limited ATS coverage leaves most jobs out of reach. Most competitors cover only 25-30% of Fortune 500 job listings. Tools that only work on job boards miss the company portals where the most competitive roles live.
  • Autofill is not the same as applying. Chrome extensions that pre-fill your name and email still require you to sign in, click through every step, and hit submit. That saves a few minutes, not hours.
  • Opaque pricing and hidden caps create budget surprises. Add-on fees for cover letters, résumé optimization, and extra credits make the real cost difficult to predict.
  • Generic applications get filtered out. Submitting the same résumé to every role without tailoring it to each job description reduces the quality of every application in the batch.

Tools that address all five of these problems close the gap between applying at volume and applying well. Tsenta was designed with all five in mind.

What to Look for in a Tool to Mass Apply to Multiple Jobs Fast

The category is full of tools that solve one or two of these problems while ignoring the rest. Before evaluating any tool, it helps to have a clear framework for what matters.

Tsenta evaluates every tool in this space against the same criteria it holds itself to: submission depth, ATS coverage, résumé tailoring, pricing transparency, and speed. These are the factors that determine whether a tool actually works for a serious, high-volume job search.

Key Features: What a Mass Apply Tool Should Deliver

  • End-to-end submission: The tool logs in, navigates the full multi-step ATS form, fills every field including open-ended answers, uploads documents, and hits submit. Autofill stops short of this.
  • Broad ATS coverage: Workday alone accounts for roughly 40% of Fortune 500 job listings. A tool that breaks on Workday covers less of the market than it claims. Tsenta covers 75%+ of Fortune 500 job listings across 15+ ATSes.
  • Per-role résumé tailoring with a visible diff: Every application should go out with a résumé rewritten for that specific job description, with keyword alignment and ATS-safe formatting. You should be able to see every change before it is sent.
  • Speed: Applications need to complete in seconds, not hours or days. Recruiters typically review the first 100 applicants, so latency is a competitive disadvantage, not just an inconvenience.
  • Transparent, predictable pricing: Published caps, no add-ons required for core functionality, and a free starting tier to validate the tool before committing.
  • Multi-surface access: The best tools let you apply from a web dashboard, a Chrome extension, a desktop app, a mobile app, or even a text message. Eight surfaces mean your job search is never stuck waiting for one device.
  • A receipt for every application: Every submission should produce a record of exactly what was sent: résumé version, cover letter, answers to open-ended questions, timestamp. No surprises.

Mass Apply Offerings:

4. LoopCV

LoopCV is one of the older platforms in this category, with an established user base of 50,000+ job seekers globally. It operates around a "loop" model: you configure saved search queries that run on a schedule and auto-apply to matching jobs across 30+ job boards. It runs server-side, so your computer does not need to stay on.

Key Features:

  • Loop-based search automation across 30+ job boards including LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor.
  • Cloud-based processing, so no need to keep a browser open.
  • AI CV builder and CV checker for résumé optimization and A/B testing.
  • Automated email outreach to recruiters when application forms are not available.
  • Kanban-style application tracker.

Mass Apply Offerings:

  • Configurable loops that run parallel searches and apply automatically on a schedule.
  • Mass Apply feature toggle that increases submission volume.
  • CV A/B testing to compare which résumé version generates better response rates.

Pricing:

  • Free plan: 10 applications/month, no credit card required, no time limit.
  • Standard Looper: $19.99/month (100 apps).
  • Premium Looper: $59.99/month (300 apps, auto-fill for screening questions).
  • Done For You: $89.99/month (300 apps plus one weekly human advisory call).
  • 3-month plans save roughly 20-25% on each tier.

Pros:

  • Genuinely free tier with no credit card required and no time expiration.
  • Cloud-based: runs without your browser or computer being on.
  • A/B testing capability for CVs and email templates is a differentiating feature not found in most competitors.
  • Long track record in the space with an established user base.

Cons:

  • Coverage is limited to job boards. Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever ATS portals are not covered at the same depth as Tsenta.
  • User reviews on Capterra and Trustpilot are divided, with some users reporting that applications were logged as submitted but were not actually filed with employers.
  • Free plan caps at only 10 applications per month, which is not useful for a serious high-volume search.
  • No mobile app; primarily a web platform with a Chrome extension.
  • Premium Looper ($59.99/month) is expensive relative to what it covers.

  • ATS compatibility score checker with keyword match insights.
  • Smart form-filling that handles multi-step forms, dropdowns, and screening questions.

Mass Apply Offerings:

Pricing:

Pros:

  • Local data storage is a genuine privacy advantage.

Tsenta vs Careerflow, $23.99 for coaching, $19 for actual applications

Careerflow Premium is $23.99/mo for LinkedIn coaching, resume tools, and autofill. Premium Plus is $44.99 for mock interviews on top. Tsenta Starter is $19/mo and applies to 600 jobs for you. different products, fair price comparison.

comparisonchrome-extension

careerflow is in this comparison because google groups it with us, but it shouldn't be.

it's a LinkedIn optimization tool. resume tweaks, profile reviews, AI mock interviews, that kind of thing. the "auto-apply" piece is a chrome extension that fills out fields on application forms. it's a side feature, not the main product.

different product, different problem. but if you ended up here comparing the two, the comparison is worth doing honestly.

what careerflow actually is

careerflow's center of gravity is LinkedIn. they offer LinkedIn profile review with AI suggestions, headline rewriting, "about" section optimization, network outreach templates, and a job tracker. there's also a resume optimization side, an AI mock interview, and yeah, an autofill chrome extension.

pricing is two tiers. Premium is $23.99/mo (or $14.41/mo billed yearly) for LinkedIn optimization, resume tools, and the autofill extension. Premium Plus is $44.99/mo (or $24.99/mo yearly) and adds the AI mock interview and interview analysis. there's also a coaching service on top.

it's a coaching tool with apply features bolted on. if that's what you need, it's a reasonable price for what it does.

the autofill vs apply gap

honestly, the gap here is bigger than the other tools in this comparison, because careerflow and tsenta aren't really trying to do the same thing.

careerflow says: your LinkedIn is weak, your resume could be better, you don't interview well, you don't know how to reach out to people. they sell you tools and coaching to improve all of that.

tsenta says: the job application process is broken, you're applying to hundreds of jobs to get any traction, doing it by hand is destroying your soul, so we apply for you.

both of these are real problems. they're just different problems. someone who needs LinkedIn coaching is not the same person who needs to fire off 200 applications a week. (although sometimes it is the same person, in which case you probably want both tools.)

the part where careerflow overlaps with tsenta is the autofill extension, which is a small feature inside their broader career platform. it's not a strong autofill, it's a "we have one too" autofill. and like every product in this category, it doesn't apply for you. it types into fields when you open an application yourself.

where tsenta is different

if what you actually want is jobs applied to, here's the breakdown:

  • tsenta applies end-to-end. you queue jobs, they go out from the cloud, you don't touch a form. careerflow's extension fills fields on applications you open manually.

  • 8 surfaces to apply from. web dashboard, native desktop on Mac/Win/Linux, Android and iOS apps, Chrome extension, MCP for Claude and ChatGPT, iMessage bot, WhatsApp bot. careerflow is web + chrome extension.

  • the price comparison is fair to make. careerflow Premium is $23.99/mo for LinkedIn coaching, resume tips, and a side-feature autofill. careerflow Premium Plus is $44.99/mo if you want the mock interview piece. tsenta starter is $19/mo for 600 actual applications a month plus AI resume optimization, automated OTP filling, a tracker, an inbox, and a resume editor. roughly $5 less than careerflow Premium, for an entirely different problem solved. if you need higher volume, tsenta pro is $39/mo for 1,500 applications.

if you want LinkedIn optimization and interview prep, careerflow is built for that and tsenta isn't. if you want jobs applied to, careerflow isn't built for that and tsenta is.

the closer

honestly, careerflow and tsenta aren't really competitors. different problems.

but if you ended up here, you probably want jobs applied to. that's not what careerflow is for. that's tsenta starter, $19/mo, roughly $5 less.

Tsenta vs Jobsolv, the remote-only niche play

Jobsolv is a remote-only auto-applier with opaque pricing. Tsenta starts at $19/mo, any role, published prices. Here's where each one fits.

comparisoncloud-applier

if you're only hunting for remote jobs, Jobsolv has a real argument.

if you're hunting for anything else, or you just want to know what you're paying before you give them your email, the conversation gets a lot shorter.

what Jobsolv actually is

a job application automation tool focused specifically on remote roles. they pull remote postings from across the web, then apply on your behalf with your resume and details.

the free resume builder is a real product. genuinely useful. that part i don't have notes on.

the apply service is where it gets fuzzy. pricing varies and isn't easy to find without going through a signup flow. that's a choice, and it's not a great one.

where Jobsolv falls short

remote-only is a real limitation. if your search includes hybrid roles, in-person roles, or you just want to keep options open, you're getting a fraction of the listings. niche tools work great inside their niche and badly outside it.

opaque pricing. "pricing varies" is internet shorthand for "we'd rather get your email first." the moment you go through a funnel before you see what something costs, you're signaling that the price isn't competitive on its own.

no clarity on application cap or apply quality. the marketing leans heavy on the remote angle and light on the apply mechanics. how many apps a month. which ATS platforms work. what happens if the listing is fake. these questions don't get answered upfront.

limited surface area. they're a web tool. no desktop app, no MCP, no SMS, no integrations with where you actually browse jobs.

where Tsenta is different

any role, any location. Tsenta doesn't care if the role is remote or in-office or hybrid or on the moon. you paste a URL, it applies. you give it a search, it goes. you're not boxed into one work model.

transparent pricing, posted publicly. three tiers, no signup needed to see them:

  • Starter: $19/mo, 600 applications/month
  • Pro: $39/mo, 1,500 applications/month
  • Power: $99/mo, 4,500 applications/month

quarterly billing saves up to 32%. annual saves up to 36%. every tier includes AI-powered resume optimization, automated OTP filling, and priority support.

if you want one-time pricing instead, we have credit packs: $19 for 200 credits, $39 for 600, $99 for 2,000. credits never expire. you don't need an account to see any of this.

want to try before paying? free tier gives you 25 applications, full Pro features, no time limit. no card needed.

8 surfaces to apply from. web at app.tsenta.com, native desktop on Mac/Win/Linux, Android and iOS apps, Chrome extension, MCP for Claude and ChatGPT, iMessage bot, WhatsApp bot. Jobsolv is web-only.

where Jobsolv is genuinely useful

if remote work is non-negotiable for you, Jobsolv is a fair specialist. they're built for that niche and they know it. there's nothing wrong with picking a specialist.

i'm not going to pretend Tsenta is better at sorting remote-only postings than a tool that does only that. we're not. we just do more things at a price you can actually see before signup.

the closer

remote-only is a fine angle for the right person. and the free resume tools are real.

but "what does the paid tier cost" should be one click away from a homepage, not three. when a job tool hides its price, the price is usually the part they're least proud of.

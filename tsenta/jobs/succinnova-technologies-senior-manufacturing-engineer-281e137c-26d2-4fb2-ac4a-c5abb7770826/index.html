Job Description

About SuccinnovaTechnologies

Succinnova Technologies is a privately held company, certified to AS 9100D and ISO 9001:2015, specializing in the manufacturing of precision-machined components and offering expert services in global supply chain management. Driven by a team of passionate professionals with strong techno-commercial capabilities, Succinnova has built a values-based, performance-driven culture focused on delivering excellence to clients while creating a positive impact on society and all stakeholders.

Visit https://www.succinnova.com to learn more.

Job summary

Support and supervise all in-house manufacturing activities to enable faster new product development and enhance the productivity of existing parts. This role provides opportunities to introduce and implement best manufacturing practices, creating value for both self and the organization. Additionally, empowered to represent the company with customers in domestic and international markets and make strategic decisions to drive execution

Requirements

1. Manufacturing& Process Planning:

Ø Defined manufacturing processes/operations for machined components by interpreting drawings with respect to GD&T and tolerances.

Ø Generated CNC programs using CAM software, and released supporting documentation (process sheets, tool lists, program sheets) to the development team.

Ø Developed FA (First Article) parts within specified time frames and handed them over to production.

Ø Provided inputs to the process engineering team for stage drawing preparation, validated stage drawings as per final requirements.

Ø Designed jigs and fixtures for machining using CAD software; supported production to achieve required OEE by increasing productivity without compromising quality.

Ø Conducted and participated in MFIR (Manufacturing Feasibility Inspection Review) meetings with cross-functional teams, tracked and closed action items.

Ø Owned allocated NPD (New Product Development) parts/projects and delivered good parts to internal customers within agreed timelines.

Ø Incorporated process improvements based on CFT feedback to enhance part quality and productivity.

Ø Led route card closure meetings for all NPD parts with cross-functional teams.

2. CuttingTools:

Ø Collaborated with cutting tool experts to select optimal cutting tools and validate cutting parameters for specific operations.

Ø Supported the tool crib in achieving the estimated CPC (Cost per Component).

3. ERP& Traceability:

Ø Uploaded and maintained required data in ERP systems for parts, fixtures, tools, and engineering documents.

Ø Ensured compliance with AS9100 standards and traceability requirements.

4. Quality:

Ø Assisted in preparing control plans and inspection plans.

Ø Recommended appropriate instruments and inspection methods to ensure product quality.

5. Process

Ø Good understanding of manufacturing processes such as milling, turning, grinding, boring, tapping, EDM, and WEDM.

Ø Exposure to casting, forging, and plating processes.

Ø Knowledge of basic quality tools, measuring instruments, and inspection methodologies.

Keycredentials

Behavioural Attributes:

Ø Demonstrates strong respect for people, work, and societal responsibilities.

Ø Environmentally conscious and committed to sustainable practices.

Ø Customer-focused with a mindset oriented toward delivering value and success

Ø Adaptable to organizational culture and open to change in dynamic environments.

Ø Passionate about talent development and team empowerment.

Education& Experience:

Ø Education: Diploma (Polytechnic/NTTF/GTTC) or BE/B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering with 8–10 years of manufacturing experience, preferably in the aerospace industry or low-batch production environment.

Ø Experience: Minimum 3–5 years of strong experience in CNC programming using NX, Solid CAM, or Master CAM. (NTTF and GTTC candidates will have an added advantage.)

Ø Hands-on expertise in programming 3, 4, and 5-axis parts with controllers such as Fanuc, Mitsubishi, Haas, Siemens, WinMax (Hurco), and Heidenhain.

Ø Proficiency in process mapping for machined parts and fixture design.

Ø Strong knowledge and application of GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing).

Ø Sound understanding of inspection methodology and use of appropriate quality tools/instruments

Preferred Certifications:

Ø GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing) Certification – ASME Y14.5

Ø Lean Six Sigma Green Belt / Black Belt

Ø AS9100 Quality Management System Training (Aerospace industry standard)

Ø CNC Programming & Operations Certification – Fanuc / Mitsubishi / Haas

Ø Industrial Automation & Industry 4.0 Certification

Ø Project Management Professional (PMP) or equivalent manufacturing project management credential

Job Description – Key Account Sales Engineer

Location: Mumbai

Experience: 4+ Years

CTC: Up to ₹13 LPA

Industry: Elevators / Escalators / Heavy Machinery & Equipment

Job Summary

We are looking for an experienced Key Account Sales Engineer to drive B2B/project sales, manage key accounts, develop new business and build strong relationships with customers across the assigned territory.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and grow key customer accounts and achieve sales targets.
  • Develop new business across residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.
  • Handle sales of elevators, escalators and related solutions.
  • Prepare techno-commercial proposals, quotations and tender submissions.
  • Negotiate pricing, commercial terms and contracts.
  • Build relationships with builders, developers, architects, consultants, contractors, PMCs and corporate clients.
  • Coordinate with Projects, Installation and Service teams for smooth project execution.
  • Track market trends, competitors and new business opportunities.
  • Drive collections, sales forecasting, MIS and business development activities.

Requirements

  • 4+ years of experience in B2B / Project / Technical Sales.
  • Elevator / Escalator industry experience preferred.
  • B.Tech / B.E. candidates: Must have sales experience in heavy machinery, industrial equipment or engineering equipment.
  • Strong experience in Key Account Management, Project Sales or Technical Sales.
  • Experience in techno-commercial negotiations, tendering and large-value projects.
  • Strong network with builders, developers, consultants, contractors and corporate clients.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation and presentation skills.
  • Willingness to travel within the assigned territory.

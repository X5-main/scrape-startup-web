The agents

Agents that follow your client's journey

From the first request through the life of the policy, each agent takes on one specific servicing task and hands you the result, ready to approve.

01

Submission intake

From a prospect's request, the agent derives the exact list of documents to collect for the coverage, checks what has come in, and drafts the follow-up email to the client.

Missing documents

02

Carrier quoting

It enters the risk profile into each carrier portal to request quotes, mapping fields from one carrier to the next. No more double entry.

Re-keying across carrier portals

03

Quote comparison

It reads the quotes that come back, compares coverages, deductibles, and exclusions, and produces a sourced coverage proposal, ready to present to the client.

04

Claims handling

It triages the claim, checks coverage, lists the exact documents needed, tracks what is received, and drafts the follow-up ready to send.

05

Past-due collections

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Covera for?

Insurance brokers and captive agents, from local agencies to larger, established firms. The agents adapt to your organization at deployment.

Can I connect my agency management system and my CRM?

Yes. Covera connects to your agency management system (Applied Epic, AMS360, EZLynx, HawkSoft) and your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), as well as your email and storage (Gmail, Outlook, Google Drive, SharePoint).

How long does it take to get started?

We start with a short audit of your day-to-day to find the highest-impact tasks. The first agent is then deployed in a few weeks, without disrupting your operations.

Are the agents tailored to my agency?

Yes. We start from your real processes and adapt each agent to your policies, your carriers, and your methods. It can go all the way to a fully custom solution.

What happens to my data?

It is never reused or used to train a model, and stays on secure, compliant infrastructure. You keep final approval on every action, in line with GDPR, DDA, and the EU AI Act.

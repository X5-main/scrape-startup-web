Best Fractional General Counsel for Startups: 6 Top Options

General Legal Team
August 11, 2026

Running out of cash and making the wrong hires are among two of the biggest challenges for startups. Hire in-house counsel too early—or hire the wrong one—and you’ve combined both risks into a single line item.

Founders are already delaying hiring more than ever, with the median time to the first hire increasing from 214 days in 2019 to 284 days in 2024. Given that median General Counsel (GC) pay sits at $296,000 as of early August 2026, it’s easy to see why bringing legal in-house is a decision many startups just aren’t ready to make.

That’s why some founders choose to start with fractional GC instead, getting senior-level legal judgment on demand and at a fraction of the cost.

If you’re considering that route, too, this guide compares the six best fractional General Counsel for startups.

Key takeaways

  • Choose the right fit, not the cheapest option
    The best fractional GC is the one that matches your startup’s stage, industry, and legal needs, not simply the lowest-priced provider.
  • Fractional GC becomes valuable once legal work is constant
    If you’re regularly negotiating contracts, hiring employees, raising funding, or dealing with compliance, ongoing legal support can prevent costly mistakes.
  • Different firms solve different startup problems
    Some firms specialize in AI, fintech, fundraising, or revenue-stage businesses, so picking a provider with relevant experience matters.
  • Look beyond contract review
    A strong fractional GC should act as a strategic advisor, supporting fundraising, governance, employment, commercial contracts, and long-term growth.
  • General Legal is the best all-around choice for most B2B startups
    If you’re a Seed to Series B company scaling commercial operations, General Legal combines startup expertise, embedded legal support, and predictable pricing in a model designed to grow with your business.

How we chose the 6 best fractional general counsel for startups

According to DiliTrust, fractional GC can cost as little as $60,000 per year, making this legal model an obvious place to start for startups still managing burn and extending runway.

But choosing the cheapest option misses the point, as a fractional GC isn’t just another legal vendor. They’re the person reviewing your enterprise Master Services Agreements (MSAs), helping structure fundraising, advising the board, and keeping product, sales, and hiring decisions from becoming legal problems six months later. 

That’s why the real question isn’t “Who’s cheapest?” but “Who’s most suitable for the kind of startup I’m building?”

That’s the lens we used to evaluate more than 25 providers before selecting the six firms that best represent different startup stages, industries, and legal operating models. These were the criteria that carried the most weight in the selection process:

  • Startup Specialization & Founder Fit (30%): We prioritized firms with demonstrated experience serving specific startup sectors or growth stages, including AI, SaaS, deep tech, venture-backed companies, and regulated industries.
  • Fractional GC Offering & Legal Coverage (25%): We assessed whether firms provide true ongoing GC services rather than project-based legal work, including support for startup contracts, fundraising, board governance, employment, privacy, intellectual property, and compliance.
  • Pricing Model & Engagement Structure (20%): Preference was given to firms offering predictable engagement models, such as flat-fee retainers or structured subscriptions, along with delivery models that fit different founder needs, from embedded legal partners to flexible outside General Counsel for startups.
  • Attorney Expertise & Startup Experience (15%): We examined founder and attorney backgrounds, focusing on teams with concrete startup, in-house, or BigLaw experience and a demonstrated track record advising venture-backed companies.
  • Scalability & Operational Fit (10%): We considered how well each provider could support a startup as it grows, including responsiveness, geographic reach, technology adoption, and the ability to scale from early fundraising through later-stage commercial growth.

1. General Legal: Best for seed to Series B B2B startups scaling commercial operations

General Legal is an AI-native law firm that provides fractional GC services and commercial legal support for high-growth startups. It combines experienced US-barred attorneys with AI-powered workflows to deliver faster contract review and ongoing legal guidance without the traditional law firm overhead.

As of early August 2026, the firm has represented over 650 growth-stage companies and maintains a 95% Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT), reflecting its focus on high-quality legal support for startups.

General Legal is our top pick for Seed to Series B B2B startups scaling commercial operations because that’s the stage where legal work shifts from occasional to constant. Founders aren’t just reviewing an isolated one-way NDA anymore; they’re negotiating customer MSAs, onboarding vendors, hiring employees, issuing SAFEs, and closing larger deals. 

At that point, waiting days for outside counsel or relying on ad hoc legal advice starts slowing the business down.

While commercial contracts remain its core expertise, General Legal also advises startups on employment, data privacy, venture financing, financial regulation, tax, and other legal matters that emerge as companies grow. This breadth makes it well-suited to serve as an outsourced legal department rather than just a contract review service.

General Legal’s core strengths for startups

General Legal combines the responsiveness of an embedded legal team with the expertise of experienced startup counsel, offering strengths like:

  • Commercial contract expertise: The firm handles the agreements that growth-stage startups negotiate every day, including MSAs, DPAs, BAAs, NDAs, customer contracts, vendor agreements, SAFEs, side letters, and employment documents. Founders can also request drafting from scratch when standardized templates aren’t enough.
  • Embedded communication: Instead of relying on long email chains, General Legal works directly inside clients’ Slack workspaces, with a client portal available for teams that prefer a more structured workflow. 
  • Attorney-led AI workflows: AI accelerates issue spotting, redlining, and document analysis, but every matter is ultimately reviewed and signed off by a US-barred attorney. As a result, founders get faster turnaround without replacing legal judgment.
  • Operational speed: The firm’s workflows are optimized for fast-moving companies, with most standard commercial agreements reviewed in hours rather than days.
  • Deep startup ecosystem roots: General Legal was built by former Casetext leaders and experienced startup attorneys, is backed by Y Combinator, SV Angel, BoxGroup, Susa Ventures, and Audacious, and works with everyone from two-person YC startups to venture-backed companies that have raised more than $100 million. This gives the team firsthand exposure to the legal challenges founders face as they scale.

Pricing

Unlike many traditional law firms, General Legal publishes its pricing upfront, so founders know what to expect before work begins.

Most services are offered on a fixed-fee basis, with custom pricing reserved for ongoing fractional GC engagements and more complex legal work.

2. Jacobs Counsel: Best for startups building or selling generative AI products

Jacobs Counsel is a boutique US law firm offering fractional GC services for startups, with a particular focus on AI, SaaS, gaming, and technology companies.

It’s particularly well-suited for startups building or selling generative AI products because the firm advises on legal issues that fall outside the expertise of most generalist startup lawyers. 

Beyond contracts and fundraising, it helps founders navigate training data licensing, model licensing, AI output intellectual property, customer AI clauses, vendor flow-down obligations, and emerging AI regulations—areas that become critical as AI companies begin signing enterprise customers and entering regulated markets.

Jacobs Counsel’s core strengths for startups

Jacobs Counsel functions as an embedded legal partner for AI startups, with strengths such as:

  • AI risk management: Address AI-specific legal risks early by building protections around training data, model licensing, AI output ownership, and customer use provisions, reducing legal exposure as your product evolves.
  • Legal infrastructure: Standardize contract templates, fundraising documents, employment paperwork, and compliance processes so your legal operations can scale alongside the business.
  • Product-focused counsel: Integrate legal guidance into leadership decisions, contract negotiations, and product development to resolve issues before they slow down engineering, sales, or fundraising.

Pricing

Jacobs Counsel primarily works on fixed-fee monthly retainers and project-based flat fees. While it publishes the scope of its services, pricing is customized and isn’t publicly disclosed.

3. Intellectual Strategies: Best for revenue-stage SaaS and e-commerce startups

Intellectual Strategies is a boutique law firm that offers fractional GC and fractional legal team services for SaaS, e-commerce, B2B, and technology companies.

It’s a strong fit for revenue-stage SaaS and e-commerce startups because these businesses often face increasing legal complexity as they grow. 

Higher contract volume, expanding teams, investor expectations, intellectual property management, and supplier relationships all require more than occasional legal advice. Intellectual Strategies addresses those challenges by embedding legal strategy into day-to-day business operations while remaining aligned with leadership and long-term growth goals.

Intellectual Strategies’ core strengths for startups

Intellectual Strategies supports founders beyond one-off legal projects, with strengths including:

  • Business strategy alignment: Connect legal decisions to your company’s growth roadmap so contracts, fundraising, and compliance support long-term business objectives.
  • Intellectual property strategy: Build an IP portfolio that aligns with your products, funding plans, and competitive position, helping strengthen investor confidence and protect key business assets.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: Work directly with leadership, finance, and operations teams to make legal part of everyday decision-making, enabling faster execution across the business.

Pricing

Intellectual Strategies offers monthly retainers for fractional GC services, while transactional work is billed using fixed-fee or hourly pricing. Specific pricing isn’t publicly disclosed.

4. Next Era Legal: Best for founder-led startups preparing for board governance and institutional fundraising

Next Era Legal is a business law firm whose attorneys work alongside leadership teams to provide ongoing legal strategy, risk management, governance support, and transactional guidance as the business evolves.

It’s a solid choice for founder-led startups preparing for board governance and institutional fundraising because those milestones introduce new legal responsibilities that extend far beyond closing an investment round. 

Next Era Legal helps founders build the legal infrastructure investors expect, supporting everything from governance processes and investor communications to legal due diligence and strategic decision-making before complexity slows the business down.

Next Era Legal’s core strengths for startups

Next Era Legal’s strengths include:

  • Governance planning: Establish board processes, corporate governance practices, and legal systems early to make future fundraising and investor oversight more manageable.
  • Leadership integration: Involve legal counsel in executive meetings, investor discussions, and cross-functional planning so legal decisions support broader business objectives instead of becoming an afterthought.
  • Legal infrastructure: Build scalable contract templates, employment documentation, compliance processes, and operational workflows that can continue supporting the business as it grows.

Pricing

Next Era Legal bills its fractional GC services through flat monthly retainers, beginning with a fixed-fee legal assessment that determines the appropriate level of ongoing support.

5. Scale LLP: Best for fintech, payments, and regulated financial services startups

Scale LLP is a national, full-service law firm that provides fractional GC, embedded legal department, and interim GC services to high-growth companies.

Founded by former in-house technology counsel, the firm combines experienced business attorneys with a technology-enabled delivery model to provide ongoing strategic legal support across the business lifecycle.

It’s an excellent choice for fintech, payments, and regulated financial services startups because these companies operate in legal environments where industry-specific expertise is essential. 

In addition to handling day-to-day legal matters, Scale advises on payment products, lending, card programs, money transmission, licensing, regulatory engagement, compliance, and other areas where a general startup lawyer may lack the specialized knowledge needed to support rapid growth.

Scale LLP’s core strengths for startups

Scale’s flexible fractional GC model offers capabilities like:

  • Product counsel: Develop legal frameworks for payment products, lending platforms, card programs, and other financial services offerings, allowing product teams to launch with greater confidence.
  • Regulatory guidance: Navigate licensing requirements, regulatory examinations, and enforcement issues before they become obstacles to expansion, reducing compliance risk as the company grows.
  • Executive-level legal support: Access attorneys with extensive in-house and BigLaw experience to strengthen strategic decision-making without adding a full-time GC to the leadership team.

Pricing

Scale LLP primarily works on an hourly basis, with attorney rates generally falling in the mid-market range, although fixed-fee arrangements may be available depending on the engagement.

6. Outside GC: Best for scaling startups with 20–200 employees needing a full outside legal team

Outside GC (OGC) is a national law firm built around the outsourced GC model. Instead of relying on a single attorney, clients gain access to a team of experienced former GC and in-house lawyers who provide strategic legal guidance, day-to-day support, and specialist expertise across multiple practice areas.

Scaling startups with 20–200 employees that need a full outside legal team will benefit most from this approach, as growing companies often require expertise across several areas simultaneously. 

In addition, OGC’s team-based approach allows startups to draw on specialists as new legal needs emerge, without hiring multiple in-house lawyers or coordinating several outside firms.

Outside GC’s core strengths for startups

The firm’s long-standing outsourced GC model is designed to provide comprehensive legal support as businesses grow, with capabilities like:

  • Multidisciplinary legal support: Access experienced attorneys across employment, commercial transactions, privacy, intellectual property, corporate governance, and other practice areas, giving your business the right expertise when new issues arise.
  • Business-first counsel: Solve legal challenges with practical advice shaped by former in-house lawyers who understand commercial priorities, helping legal decisions support business growth.
  • Flexible engagement: Customize the level of legal support based on your company’s workload and priorities, allowing your legal function to expand alongside the business without adding permanent headcount.

Pricing

Outside GC uses predictable pricing through fixed-fee engagements and hourly billing that’s positioned below traditional BigLaw rates.

What is the best fractional General Counsel for your startup?

The best fractional General Counsel for your startup depends on the legal problems you’re trying to solve. 

A pre-seed AI startup preparing to launch its first enterprise product needs very different legal guidance than a Series A SaaS company negotiating dozens of customer contracts every month or a fintech startup navigating licensing requirements.

For example, if you’re scaling a payments platform, embedded finance product, or other regulated financial service, Scale LLP offers the kind of product and regulatory expertise that generalist startup firms rarely provide.

If, however, you’re looking for a firm that combines deep startup experience spanning multiple areas with a true fractional GC model, General Legal stands out as the strongest choice.

Instead of focusing on a single industry or narrow legal niche, it was built for the realities of high-growth B2B startups, providing ongoing strategic counsel, embedded collaboration, predictable pricing, and commercial legal support in a model designed to scale alongside the business.

FAQ

What is fractional General Counsel?

A fractional General Counsel is an experienced attorney who provides ongoing legal leadership on a part-time or outsourced basis, giving businesses executive-level legal support without hiring a full-time General Counsel.

When should a startup hire fractional General Counsel?

A startup should hire fractional General Counsel once legal work becomes continuous—for example, when signing customer contracts, raising funding, expanding the team, or entering regulated markets.

Why should high-growth companies consider fractional General Counsel?

High-growth companies should consider fractional General Counsel because it provides strategic legal guidance, predictable costs, and scalable support without the overhead of a full-time legal executive.

What is the best fractional GC attorney at law?

The best fractional GC attorney at law depends on your startup’s stage, industry, and legal needs. General Legal is our top overall recommendation for Seed to Series B B2B startups, while firms like Jacobs Counsel and Scale LLP are better suited to specialized industries.

What is the best company formation company?

The best company formation company depends on the level of legal support you need. If you’re simply forming an LLC or corporation, online incorporation services may be sufficient. If you’re building a venture-backed startup, working with a startup law firm that can advise on incorporation, founder equity, and fundraising is often the better long-term choice.

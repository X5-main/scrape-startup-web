7 Best Contract Review AI Tools for Legal Teams [Comparison]

General Legal Team
July 15, 2026

Contract review is the process of analyzing a contract before signing to identify risks, validate terms, and ensure the agreement actually protects your business.

Startups might not handle over 50,000 contracts per year like the busiest enterprise legal teams do, but they still routinely negotiate contracts such as Master Service Agreements (MSAs) and SaaS terms. Getting those contracts wrong can create significant financial, legal, and operational risk.

However, manual contract review creates a costly bottleneck, with delays, unfavorable contract terms, and operational friction eroding up to 9% of annual revenue.

That’s why legal teams are increasingly adopting artificial intelligence (AI) to accelerate review cycles, identify risky clauses, and reduce negotiation bottlenecks.

If contract review is starting to delay deals for your business, the seven contract review AI tools below are worth evaluating.

Key takeaways

  • The best contract review AI tool depends on your needs
    Enterprise legal teams, startups, and small businesses often require different solutions based on contract volume, workflow complexity, and legal resources.
  • AI speeds up review, but it doesn’t replace legal judgment
    Contract review tools can identify risks and suggest edits, but important legal and business decisions still require human expertise.
  • Workflow integration is critical
    The best tools fit into existing systems like Microsoft Word, Slack, and CLMs, making contract review easier to adopt and scale.
  • Growing companies often outgrow basic AI tools
    As legal needs become more complex, software alone may not provide the breadth of expertise required across different legal matters.
  • General Legal offers an alternative to software-only solutions
    General Legal combines attorney-led legal services with AI-enabled workflows, helping startups and growth-stage companies access legal expertise without building a large in-house team.

How we chose the 7 best contract review AI tools

There’s no single “best” contract review AI tool for every organization, as different teams operate under different constraints. Some need enterprise-grade review infrastructure; others require fast self-service reviews, outsourced legal support, or tools that integrate into their existing workflows.

So, rather than ranking tools based on marketing claims or feature volume, we selected platforms that solve different operational problems across startups, in-house legal teams, and enterprise organizations.

The following factors played the largest role in our selection process:

  • Quality of contract review (25%): We prioritized tools that improve review accuracy, identify meaningful risks, surface problematic clauses, and help legal teams review contracts faster without sacrificing legal judgment.
  • Fit for different company sizes (20%): Some tools are built for lean startup teams handling a handful of agreements a week. Others are designed for enterprise legal departments reviewing thousands of contracts per year. We included both.
  • Level of legal involvement required (15%): We included both AI copilots designed for legal professionals and solutions geared toward founders, operators, procurement teams, and businesses seeking more hands-on legal support.
  • Workflow integration (15%): Contract review happens inside operational workflows, not in isolation. We looked closely at integrations with Microsoft Word, Slack, CLM systems, e-signature platforms, approval workflows, and existing legal processes.
  • Practical usability and adoption (15%): A powerful platform nobody adopts is useless. We favored tools that reduce friction, accelerate negotiations, and fit naturally into existing workflows.
  • AI realism instead of hype (10%): Many legal AI tools promise full automation, but very few deliver it. We prioritized platforms that understand where AI is genuinely useful and where human legal judgment remains essential.

1. Harvey AI: Best for enterprise legal teams and large law firms

Harvey AI is an enterprise-grade legal AI platform that supports contract analysis, legal research, drafting, due diligence, regulatory work, and large-scale document review inside a centralized AI workspace.

This platform is geared toward large legal departments and Am Law firms handling substantial volumes of unstructured legal data while maintaining strict security, governance, and compliance standards.

Organizations managing highly specialized matters spanning multiple practice areas will find more value in Harvey than those looking for a point solution focused solely on routine contract review.

Key features

Harvey supports large-scale legal analysis and workflow automation through features like:

  • Vault document analysis: Extract and compare key contract terms from thousands of agreements to identify renewals, compliance risks, and operational inconsistencies at scale.
  • Review workflow builder: Build multi-step legal review processes that automate repetitive analysis, document assessment, and information extraction.
  • Knowledge and playbook management: Apply internal precedents, templates, and negotiation standards consistently across legal teams and matters.

Pricing

Harvey operates on a custom enterprise pricing model with costs varying based on seat count, integrations, and workflow complexity.

The platform is designed for large firms and enterprise legal departments rather than smaller legal teams or startups, and its pricing reflects that positioning.

2. Spellbook: Best for legal teams working primarily in Microsoft Word

Spellbook is an AI contract review copilot built directly into Microsoft Word. It helps legal teams review, redline, and negotiate agreements without leaving the document itself.

This makes it a strong fit for transactional lawyers, small firms, and operators who spend most of their time reviewing contracts inside Word rather than managing complex enterprise CLM systems.

Key features

Spellbook streamlines in-document review and negotiation support through tools like:

  • AI-powered redlining: Generate contract edits and negotiation markups directly inside Word to accelerate review cycles.
  • Custom playbooks: Apply pre-approved legal guidance and fallback language automatically across agreements.
  • Risk and drafting detection: Surface risky clauses, drafting inconsistencies, and missing terms before agreements move forward.

Pricing

Spellbook uses a custom pricing model based primarily on team size and organizational requirements. 

3. Juro: Best for lean legal teams managing growing contract volumes

Juro is an AI-native contract automation platform that combines contract review, redlining, approvals, signing, and storage inside a collaborative browser-based workspace.

It’s particularly useful for companies handling increasing contract volume, as it enables commercial and business teams to self-serve routine agreements while legal retains oversight and control.

Key features

Juro keeps contract review fast, collaborative, and embedded inside existing business systems, thanks to tools like:

  • AI review agent: Analyze third-party contracts against internal playbooks to identify deviations from approved terms and generate fallback redlines automatically.
  • Smart data extraction: Pull key contract terms into structured fields that trigger approvals, workflows, summaries, and reporting.
  • Cross-platform integrations: Review and manage contracts directly inside Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Word, and other existing business systems.

Pricing

Juro’s pricing is customized based on contract volume, workflow requirements, AI functionality, and integration needs.

4. goHeather: Best for SMBs reviewing routine contracts

goHeather is a lawyer-trained AI contract review platform that helps users review, edit, and redline agreements through plain-English issue spotting, risk analysis, and guided negotiation suggestions.

It’s designed for contracts that require review but don’t necessarily justify involving outside counsel. That’s why small businesses, procurement teams, and fractional GCs reviewing employment agreements or routine commercial contracts are likely to find the most value in it. 

Key features

goHeather makes deep contract review more accessible to non-lawyers through features like:

  • Traffic-light risk scoring: Flag problematic clauses with a red, yellow, and green system, making it easier to prioritize legal risk.
  • Plain-English clause explanations: Translate dense legal language into practical business implications and explain how specific terms could affect negotiations, IP ownership, or liability exposure.
  • Custom AI playbooks: Apply internal standards and fallback positions automatically to uploaded Word documents and PDFs.

Pricing

goHeather offers tiered pricing for individuals, small teams, and larger organizations with higher contract volume. The higher-tier plan adds collaborative review tools, obligation tracking, drafting support, and expanded AI capabilities.

5. Legly: Best for freelancers and small businesses reviewing straightforward agreements

Legly is a web-based AI contract review platform that supports users in analyzing contracts, identifying deal-breakers, and organizing agreements without requiring any software installation.

Freelancers, independent contractors, and smaller businesses reviewing straightforward commercial agreements will benefit from its lightweight setup and highly accessible interface.

Key features

Legly prioritizes fast, low-friction contract analysis for business users, offering features like:

  • Deal-breaker detection: Highlight missing clauses, restrictive terms, and non-standard contract language before agreements get signed.
  • Metadata extraction: Pull deadlines, payment terms, renewal conditions, and termination notices into structured data for easier tracking and follow-up.
  • Contract dashboard: Organize agreements visually, and search across contract portfolios to monitor obligations and key terms more efficiently.

Pricing

Legly offers a freemium model for basic contract reviews, alongside paid plans for businesses with higher review volume and more advanced analysis requirements. The enterprise plan adds portfolio management, customized review guidelines, integrations, and dedicated support.

6. Docusign IAM: Best for companies already using the Docusign ecosystem

Docusign IAM is an AI-powered agreement management platform that combines contract analysis, review automation, storage, workflow management, and e-signatures inside a single agreement ecosystem.

It makes the most sense for businesses that already rely on Docusign for signing workflows and want to layer AI-powered contract review into systems their teams are already familiar with.

Key features

Docusign IAM centralizes agreement operations and accelerates contract handling at scale, through capabilities like:

  • AI-assisted review: Compare agreements against internal playbooks to identify high-risk provisions and generate suggested redlines automatically.
  • Agreement intelligence: Extract key contract data, monitor renewals, and surface operational insights from stored agreements.
  • Conversational contract editing: Generate fallback clauses, rewrite contract language, and answer agreement questions directly inside Microsoft Word.

Pricing

Docusign IAM offers multiple pricing tiers, ranging from individual e-signature plans to enterprise-scale agreement management deployments.

More advanced plans expand automation, integrations, compliance controls, AI review functionality, and organization-wide administration.

7. LegalFly: Best for small legal teams handling high-volume contract review

LegalFly is an AI contract review platform focused on fast clause analysis, playbook-based redlining, and audit-ready contract reviews inside Microsoft Word.

Small legal teams reviewing large numbers of agreements will benefit most from LegalFly’s ability to standardize contract decisions, maintain consistent negotiation positions, and reduce manual review time without losing visibility into why changes were made.

Key features

LegalFly offers scalable and defensible contract review through tools like:

  • Automated playbook enforcement: Apply approved fallback clauses and negotiation standards automatically to incoming agreements.
  • Audit-ready reasoning: Explain flagged risks, suggested edits, and clause deviations with supporting rationale and transparent review history.
  • Negotiation-ready redlines: Generate fully formatted contract markups directly inside Microsoft Word for faster counterparty review and response.

Pricing

LegalFly operates on a custom quote-based pricing model tailored to contract volume, workflow complexity, and organizational requirements.

What is the best contract review AI tool?

The best contract review AI tool for your team depends on how your company handles contracts internally and where the review process tends to break down.

For large legal departments with complex approval chains, compliance requirements, and high contract volume, platforms like Harvey AI and Docusign IAM are often the most suitable choice. Smaller teams reviewing relatively straightforward agreements may find tools like goHeather, Legly, and Spellbook sufficient for issue spotting, redlining, and basic analysis.

The challenge often emerges in the middle.

Many startups and growth-stage companies have outgrown lightweight review tools but aren’t yet large enough to support a full in-house legal function.

In that situation, software alone only solves part of the problem. AI can identify clauses, suggest edits, and accelerate reviews, but someone still needs to make legal judgments, negotiate terms, and understand how employment, fundraising, commercial, privacy, and regulatory issues interact.

Hiring a single attorney can help, but their expertise may not cover every practice area the company encounters.

So, how do companies solve this problem without continuously adding headcount? The answer lies in AI-native law firms.

FAQ

Which AI tool is best for contract review?

The best AI tool for contract review depends on your company’s size, contract volume, and legal workflow requirements. Harvey AI is generally better suited to enterprise legal departments, while tools like Spellbook, Juro, goHeather, and Legly are often a stronger fit for smaller teams handling routine contract reviews. 

Companies that need legal support beyond software alone may also consider working with an AI-native law firm like General Legal.

Can ChatGPT review a contract?

Yes. ChatGPT can summarize clauses, identify potential issues, and suggest edits. However, it can’t provide legal advice and shouldn’t replace professional legal review for high-stakes agreements or complex negotiations.

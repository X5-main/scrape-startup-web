§ 03 — Contracts

Need help with contracts?

Here are starting points from our template library, or have us draft you one.

View Template Library→Have us draft you one→
General Commercial

Master Services Agreement (MSA)

A tech-oriented Master Services Agreement designed for companies that deploy software, connect to third‑party systems, and provide ongoing platform or integration services.

Employment

Employee Offer Letter (California Exempt Employees Only)

A formal Employee Offer Letter defining compensation, duties, exempt‑employee policies, at‑will status, confidentiality and inventions obligations, and mandatory arbitration terms for California hires.

Employment

Advisor Agreement

A streamlined Advisor Agreement that defines advisory services, equity compensation, full IP assignment, strict confidentiality, and at‑will termination.

Data & Privacy

Global Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

A processor-friendly Global Data Processing Addendum for processing personal data in the U.S., EU/EEA, UK, and Switzerland, setting clear processing limits, security and incident‑response standards, subprocessor controls, international‑transfer safeguards, and GDPR‑aligned obligations.

Data & Privacy

U.S. Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

A processor‑friendly Data Processing Addendum for handling personal data within the United States, defining clear processing limits, required security measures, incident‑response obligations, and state‑privacy‑law compliance.

General Commercial

Terms of Use

A foundational website Terms of Use agreement that sets the rules for accessing and using a company’s site, outlining user obligations, permitted and prohibited activities, intellectual‑property protections, liability limits, and a binding arbitration framework

Data & Privacy

Global Privacy Policy

A global privacy policy covering U.S. and European requirements, detailing how a company collects, uses, shares, and transfers personal information, with GDPR‑specific disclosures on lawful bases, international data transfers, and expanded user rights for individuals in the EEA and UK.

Data & Privacy

U.S. Privacy Policy

A U.S.‑focused privacy policy outlining how a company collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information, with detailed disclosures on data categories, tracking technologies, state‑specific privacy rights, and user choices under U.S. law.

Data & Privacy

Cookie Notice

A concise cookie notice describing how cookies function, what technologies are used, and how users can manage or opt out of tracking.

General Commercial

One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

A protective unilateral NDA governing confidential information, safeguarding standards, disclosure limits, and end‑of‑term obligations.

General Commercial

Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

A standard mutual NDA covering confidential information, permitted use, access controls, and return‑or‑destruction obligations.

Healthcare

Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

A HIPAA-compliant Business Associate Agreement designed for startups and technology companies that handle protected health information (PHI) on behalf of covered entities.

§ 05 — FAQs

Common questions about how we work

What are the hours of operation?
Our standard operating hours are 5:00 AM–6:00 PM PT, Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.
What kinds of agreements can you help with?
We can help with any standard commercial, employment, incorporation or early fundraising documents (ie SAFEs and side letters).
In what jurisdictions do you practice?
For most agreements, we support companies doing business in all US states.
How will I work with my lawyer?
Our lawyers work where you work. Slack, email, our client portal - wherever you send us a contract, we'll send you the mark-up!
What is included in the contract review price?
We offer two tiers of pricing: for $250 or $500, we'll conduct a review and give you negotiation guidelines for most contracts. For $1,000 we'll do the full negotiation, including limitless turns and calls. Check out our pricing page for more granular details on different contract prices.
Can you draft a contract from scratch?
Yes, we're happy to draft new agreements. See our pricing page for our flat rate drafting fee.
Is my lawyer an AI?
No, we're a law firm that hires only highly-qualified, US barred attorneys. Our attorneys simply work hand in hand with our AI to deliver you high quality, speedy services.

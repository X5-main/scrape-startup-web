Everything a contract review should cover.

Every General Legal contract review includes all of the following. No extras, no upsells.

Risk flagging

Every non-standard clause is flagged with severity scoring (critical, medium, or low) so you know exactly where to focus.

Redlining & negotiation

Receive a fully marked-up document with tracked changes and plain-English explanations for every proposed modification.

Contract summarisation

Get a one-page executive summary of any contract: key terms, obligations, deadlines, and termination rights, in plain English.

Clause-level analysis

Drill into any specific clause. Ask our AI why a provision is problematic, what market standard looks like, and how to fix it.

Benchmark vs. market

Every clause is compared against market standard for your industry and deal size. Know when you're being asked to accept unusual terms.

Playbook enforcement

Upload your legal playbook. General Legal enforces your preferred positions automatically on every contract it reviews.

Deadline & obligation extraction

All key dates (notice periods, renewal windows, termination rights) are extracted and formatted into a clean obligations register.

Slack & email native

Send contracts and get back redlines without leaving your existing workflow. No separate dashboard required.

FAQ

Common questions about contract review.

How is this different from using ChatGPT on my contract?+
ChatGPT is not a law firm and cannot give you legal advice. General Legal is a licensed law firm. Every review is performed by a U.S.-barred attorney. Our AI accelerates the attorney's work; it never replaces their judgment.
Is my contract data kept confidential?+
Yes. Your documents are covered by attorney-client privilege and end-to-end encrypted. We never use client documents to train AI models, and your data is never shared with third parties.
What types of contracts can you review?+
We handle any standard commercial agreement: NDAs, MSAs, DPAs, vendor agreements, SAFE notes, employment agreements, SaaS subscriptions, and more. See the full list above.
Does the flat fee cover negotiations?+
Our Standard Review covers the initial review, redlines, and one round of revisions. Our Review + Negotiation package covers unlimited turns and counterparty calls through to signature.
Can I work with General Legal directly from Slack?+
Yes. You get a private Slack channel with your named attorney from day one. Drop a file in, ask a question, send a revised draft. No email threads, no scheduling.

How to get started

How to get started

1

Go to refer.micro1.ai, sign up and get your unique referral link immediately

2

Browse the content library and choose content for your niche

3

Save the content, replace [your referral link] with your actual link, and post across your platforms

4

Track referrals from your dashboard and get paid automatically via Whop once your referral logs 10 hours within 3 months

Platform guidelines

Platform guidelines

Instagram stories (9:16)

Add the “apply now” sticker  with your referral link. Post between 9-11am or 6-8pm local time. Tag @micro1.ai so we can repost.

LinkedIn (1080x1080 or short caption)

Lead with why you  think the role is worth it. Personal notes perform best. Add your referral link in the first comment. Add your referral link in the post and in the comments

Facebook

Post in professional or niche community groups for the best reach. Use the 1080x1080 format or the short caption.

WhatsApp / DMs

Use the short caption format. Works best in professional communities or when messaging contacts directly.

Reccomendations

Recommendations

1

Always replace [your referral link] with your actual link before posting - that’s how your referrals are tracked.

2

Feel free to add your own caption above the post copy to make it feel more personal to your audience.

3

For Instagram Stories, write “apply now” in the sticker text and link it to your referral URL.

4

For LinkedIn and Facebook, also share your referral link in the first comment or your bio for best results.

5

Rotate niches and formats week to week - consistency and variety work better than repeating the same post.
